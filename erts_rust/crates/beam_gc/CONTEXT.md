# `beam_gc` — Per-Process Garbage Collector (Stage 4)

## Intent
Owns the **per-process collector** of the Rust ERTS rewrite: a **generational semi-space Cheney copying** collector over the per-process heap, the off-heap **refcounted large-object (MSO / refc-binary)** interface and the process **heap-size policy**.
It is **barrier-free by construction**: terms are immutable ⇒ *no term on the mature heap may refer to a term on the young heap*, so the mutator pays zero per-store overhead.
Reproduce the collector semantics of the legacy C (`../../../erts/emulator/beam/erl_gc.c` + `copy.c`) without its C structure; the design source is `../../ERTS_RUST_REWRITE_DEEPDIVE.md` §D and the Stage-4 row of §H.
Consumed by `beam_process`, which drives collections at safepoints (wave 1); it depends downward only on `beam_common`, `beam_term`, `beam_alloc` and `libc`.
Honours the crate-layer contracts of `../CONTEXT.md`: the root-set contract (no frame liveness maps), the frozen term layout (§2) and the return-based error strategy (§3).
MMTk/Boehm are **rejected**; GenImmix is a **measurement-gated** mature-generation option selected by the **measurement-derived automatic rule** (`selection.rs`) or an explicit pin.
**How it is built** — the design decisions, the collector-internal gaps, the observational-forensics rules, the handoff seams and the module-by-module routing — lives in `src/CONTEXT.md` (the implementation node).

  The nursery's post-collection capacity is a **live-set target**, never a function of the nursery's own capacity or of its pre-collection fill — one target per collection kind, and both collapse seams may grow *or* shrink onto it:
  a **fullsweep** uses `young_target_words(live) = next_words(YOUNG_TARGET_LIVE_MULTIPLE * live + need_reserve_words, 0)` = `next_words(2 * live + 64)` over the **whole-heap** live set (`young_after_fullsweep(capacity, live)` / `Nursery::collapse_after_fullsweep`: grow to the target, shrink back once the live set has fallen `YOUNG_SHRINK_FACTOR` times below it, else keep), because a fullsweep is the one collection that measures the whole live set exactly;
  a **minor** uses `young_minor_target_words(live) = next_words(live + need_reserve_words, 0)` = `next_words(live + 64)` over the **young** live set the round evacuated — its copy volume, `GcStats::words_copied` (`GenImmixCollector::run_minor` passes the words the promotion moved, the identical quantity, so both strategies collapse to the same capacity for the same live set). One times the live set: a young-only figure under-estimates the heap's whole live set, and the 2× copy-amortisation trade belongs to the fullsweep.
  `young_after_collection(capacity, young_live)` / `Nursery::collapse` **installs the target** when `capacity > YOUNG_SHRINK_FACTOR × target` (the live set fell far below what the nursery is sized for: give the memory back) or when `capacity < target` (enlarge onto it), and otherwise **keeps** the capacity. The **mature space's occupancy is never a minor's live figure**: a minor does not copy the mature generation, so it holds every garbage object the minors since the last fullsweep promoted, and a target derived from it follows the run's total promotion instead of its live set.
  `nursery_floor_words() = next_words(need_reserve_words, 0)` is a real **lower bound** on every capacity the policy installs, assertable by a dependent: `young_minor_target_words(live) >= nursery_floor_words()` and `young_target_words(live) >= floor` for **every** `live` (both go through the monotone `next_words` on an argument of at least `need_reserve_words`, and `young_minor_target_words(0) == nursery_floor_words()`), `nursery_floor_words() >= need_reserve_words` unless `max_heap_words` clamps it, and because the three-way rule installs the target whenever the capacity is below it, a collection can never leave a nursery below the floor. Figures: `HeapPolicy::default()` floor = **90** words; a spawn policy (`min_heap_words = DEFAULT_MIN_HEAP_WORDS`) floor = **233** words = `DEFAULT_MIN_HEAP_WORDS` = `young_minor_target_words(0)` (the figure `beam_rt`'s `COLLECTOR_FLOOR` pin reads); `HeapPolicy::default().initial_heap_words() == 233`.
  Every result is the capacity or the target, so both rules are **fixed points**, and the live-set target a collection derives is remembered (`Nursery::live_target_words`, recorded by *both* collapse seams) to **narrow the no-relocation `Nursery::grow` from above** — `min(target, max(one_step, live_target))` with `one_step = next_words(need).max(capacity)` — and then, **last so the narrowing cannot defeat it**, `max(target, HeapPolicy::next_words_above(capacity))`: a growth always installs a space strictly larger than the one it retires (the C's "never the same size when *growing*", `erl_gc.c:310-338`). A fresh nursery that has not collected yet has no live target and follows the plain curated growth rule (the `next_words` step, at least a doubling).
  **A collection, not the growth seam, bounds the retained capacity.** A growth with no collection retains the space it replaces (its words stay addressable at their original addresses), and `capacity_words()` is the honest chain sum of that retained capacity. The seam therefore never refuses a growth to *bound* the chain — only the two legitimate ceilings (the growth table's, and `max_heap_words`) refuse — because a caller holding no root set cannot collect and a refusal is not a bound: a runtime that absorbs the refusal keeps the words elsewhere. `collection_debt()` reports the retained chain so the caller can collect; a nursery that is never collected grows with its allocation.
  The initial young space is **not** modelled on the C's `arg_size + S_RESERVED` heap need: a child's spawn arguments are copied in later and the heap grows on demand, so a spawn with a very large argument graph starts smaller than the oracle's. The visible consequence is the `-eval`/`-s`/`-run` action process, which the oracle spawns with the expression as its argument and therefore starts it at `erts_next_heap_size(heap_need, 0)` above the 233 minimum, while here it starts at its `min_heap_size`. Two probes are unaffected: a **fresh spawned process** (`spawn`/`spawn_opt` of a fun with no arguments) answers exactly the oracle's figure, and a `+hms` above the heap need dominates on both runtimes.

## The allocation request (`need`) — why `ensure_capacity` does NOT carry one
The legacy C folds the pending allocation request into the next young block's size — `need_after = (HEAP_TOP(p) - HEAP_START(p)) + need + stack_size (+ S_RESERVED)` (`erl_gc.c:1503-1509`, `:2062-2075`) — so that a collection run *because* the mutator must allocate leaves that room behind. `HeapPolicy` carries the additive rule (`young_after_collection_with_need`, `young_minor_target_words_with_need`, `young_target_words_with_need`, `young_after_fullsweep_with_need`; their un-augmented forms are the `need == 0` cases) but **no collector calls it with a non-zero need**.
A measured fold (threading `ensure_capacity`'s request through `Nursery::collapse`/`collapse_after_fullsweep` and the mark-region `run_minor`/`run_major`/`grow_major`/`relocate_major` chain) was **rejected**: it raises the whole `beam_rt` boot from 384 to 414 young collections and +0.44 % retired instructions (`crates/beam_rt/CONTEXT.md` owns the boot figure).
Why: `need_reserve_words` (64) already stands for the C's `need + S_RESERVED` inside the sizing rules, so folding the actual request in additively **double-counts** it (`next_words(64 + 64) = 142` where the rule's own figure is `next_words(64) = 90`); and because it makes the trailing `grow` a no-op, the growth seam's strict-progress step (which installed a two-request-wide space) never runs, so the nursery settles exactly **one** pending request wide — one collection per request instead of one per two on an admission shape.
A future attempt must therefore either subtract `need_reserve_words` from the request or keep the growth step's width, and must re-measure the boot collection count before it lands.

## What frees term memory (the root-set obligation)
Only a **successful collection** frees an Eterm-carrying region, and it frees it **unconditionally** — there is no check that the supplied root slice named every live term, and the crate has **no conservative/heap-scan mode** (every walk descends from `roots`: `reachable.rs::walk_reachable`).
- `Nursery::install_collapsed` (`src/nursery.rs:575`) is the nursery's only free site: `retired.clear()` (`:576`, every growth-retired space) and the old current space dropped when the fold's target differs from its capacity (`:583`, else a bare `reset()` at `:580`).
  Reachable only from `collapse`/`collapse_after_fullsweep` (`:544`/`:561`), i.e. from `Collector::collect_minor`/`collect_major` (`src/collector.rs:361`/`:466`) and `GenImmixCollector::run_minor`/`run_major`/`relocate_major` (`src/immix_collector.rs:622`/`:723`/`:880`).
- A fullsweep also drops the old **mature** space (`src/collector.rs:452`) / mark-region (`src/immix_collector.rs:858`).
- `Nursery::grow`/`grow_nursery` (`src/nursery.rs:381`, `src/collector.rs:530`) frees **nothing** and moves **nothing** — the retired space keeps every live word at its original address and stays in the from-set (`add_to_from_set`, `src/nursery.rs:274`) — so it is legal for a caller holding no root set, but it **moves the danger to the next collection**: a pointer the host never publishes dies at that collapse. `collection_debt` (`src/collector.rs:174`) is opt-in; a host must not carry unpublished terms across a `grow_nursery`.
- Off-heap payloads (`OffHeap::sweep`, `src/mso.rs:356`) are the only other release and they free **no** Eterm region.
- A **foreign-region compaction** (`Relocator`/`compact`, §Foreign regions) frees **nothing**: it copies out, rewrites the roots and reports `dest_words_used`, so the host owns both regions and decides when to release the source — it must not release it before every root slice has been forwarded and `complete` has returned.
- Freed memory reaches the OS only sometimes: `HeapSpace` → `beam_alloc::Heap::drop` (`../beam_alloc/src/heap.rs:242`) → carrier release (`../beam_alloc/src/allocator/core.rs:520`/`:534`) → mseg reuse cache (default 10, `MAX_CACHE_SIZE` 30) → `munmap` (`../beam_alloc/src/mseg/state.rs:124`, `:212`). A **slab** block is merely returned to its free list with the carrier retained for the allocator's lifetime (`core.rs:544-571`), so a stale pointer into a *small* freed space reads recycled words (silent corruption) while a *large* one (a compiler-sized nursery) is eventually munmapped (SIGSEGV) — the allocation-count-sensitive signature.

## Constraints
- **Errors:** `beam_common::{Result, BeamError}` only.
  Heap exhaustion while copying a term is `BeamError::Alloc(AllocFailure::new(AllocKind::Heap, bytes))` — never panic, never a crate-local error enum, never `unwrap()` on runtime data.
  `beam_term::TermError` is reconciled to `BeamError` upstream, so `?` crosses the boundary.
- **`unsafe` confinement:** production `unsafe` lives only in the word-level code — `copy.rs` (the Cheney copier and the marker primitives), `relocate.rs` (the destination seam and the compaction driver), `immix_collector*` (the promotion driver), `reachable.rs` (the read-only whole-heap walk and its one-bit-per-word visited set), `live.rs` (the exact-live counter), `immix/` (the mark-region mark/evacuation walks), the two narrow `NonNull`-deref / `Box::from_raw` blocks in `mso.rs`, and `space.rs`'s one `unsafe` constructor (`FromSet::from_ranges`, which takes the caller's word-region assertion).
  Every `unsafe fn` carries a `# Safety` section and every `unsafe` block a preceding `// SAFETY:` rationale; `unsafe_op_in_unsafe_fn` stays clean.
  `policy.rs`, `heap_info.rs`, `footprint.rs`, `stats.rs`, `bounds.rs`, `config.rs`, `offheap.rs`, `bin_vheap.rs` and the `Collector`/`GenImmixCollector`/`AnyCollector` driving logic are safe Rust.
- **Do NOT modify `beam_term` or `beam_alloc`** — build on their public APIs only.
  Do not touch `../../../erts/` (read-only ground truth) or `../../tools/`.
- **Never break the frozen term layout or the barrier-free invariant** (a minor must not leave a mature→young pointer).
- Files target ~1000 lines; split by responsibility.
- Licensing Apache-2.0; deps `beam_common`, `beam_term`, `beam_alloc`, `libc` only.

## Capacity growth — what feeds it, and where a capacity feedback can arise
Every `heap_size` / `total_heap_size` / `erlang:memory(processes)` / `max_heap_size` figure this crate publishes is a **chain sum**, never one space's capacity, and the chain is released only by a collection.
- `Nursery::capacity_words()` (`src/nursery.rs:247`) sums `current` **plus every space a no-relocation growth retired**; `HeapObservable::heap_size_words`/`total_heap_size_words` (`src/collector.rs:654,659`) forward it and `process_info(P,{total_heap_size,memory})`, the `processes` category and `MaxHeapUsage::heap_words` all read it. Each space is eagerly allocated at its full capacity (`HeapSpace::with_allocator` → `Heap::with_allocator`, `src/space.rs:51`), so the figure is reserved memory, not an occupancy.
- `Nursery::install` (`src/nursery.rs:501`) **retires** the space it replaces — nothing frees it outside a collapse (`Nursery::install_collapsed`, `src/nursery.rs:575-578`) — so capacity-per-space and summed capacity are different quantities; because a growth installs a space strictly larger than the one it retires (the rule is `Nursery::grow`'s, below), every link of the chain is strictly larger than the one behind it.
- `Nursery::grow` (`src/nursery.rs:381`) is the one capacity rule keyed on the **previous capacity**, and it **always installs a space strictly larger than the one it retires**: `one_step = policy.next_words(need).max(capacity)`, `next_words(capacity * 2)` when that would not enlarge, the remembered live-set bound narrowing that choice from above (`min(target, max(one_step, live_target))`), and then — **last, so nothing above can defeat it** — `max(target, HeapPolicy::next_words_above(capacity))` (the C's "never the same size when *growing*", `erl_gc.c:310-338`). The live-set bound (`live_target_words`, set **only by a collapse**, `install_collapsed`) can therefore only *narrow* the choice: it cannot shrink a space below the current one and cannot install a same-size space, so the chain never stacks equally sized spaces (each of which would append a whole capacity to the chain while granting no room). The only refusals are the two ceilings that a larger space cannot honour either: the growth table's own limit and `capacity_words() <= max_heap_words` (the summed-capacity ceiling), both returned as the typed `error::heap_exhausted` **before** anything is allocated or retired, so a refused growth leaves the nursery byte-for-byte unchanged with `collection_debt()` untouched.
- The retained chain is **released by a collection and by nothing else**, because only a collection proves a retired space's words dead: `install_collapsed` drops the whole chain (`retired`, `retired_regions`, the empty-space index) and installs one space on the live-set target. The seam is therefore bounded by the caller's collection cadence, not by a growth-time cap — a growth-time cap converts into retained capacity elsewhere in the runtime, which is strictly worse than an honest chain.
- `Nursery::collapse` (`src/nursery.rs:544`) asks the policy with `collected_capacity = self.capacity_words()` (the **chain sum**) and `live_words = stats.words_copied` (exact). `HeapPolicy::young_after_collection`'s keep branch (`capacity ∈ [target, 8·target]`) then materializes that summed capacity as **one** space; its shrink branch (`capacity > 8·target`) is what bounds it.
- **The retained tail is freed by exactly one statement.** `Nursery::install_collapsed`'s `self.retired.clear()` (`src/nursery.rs:576`, with `retired_regions.clear()` and `empty_retired.clear()` beside it) drops the `HeapSpace`s and their `beam_alloc::Heap` `Drop` (`../../beam_alloc/src/heap.rs:242`) returns each block to the allocator. There is **no** `free_retired`/`shrink`/`reclaim` seam and `Nursery` itself is `pub(crate)` (`src/nursery.rs:111`, reachable crate-internally as `Collector::nursery`), so nothing outside `beam_gc` can release a provably-garbage retired tail without running a collection.
- **Of the growth/collection doors only a collection bounds the chain.** `grow_nursery` (`src/collector.rs:530`, `src/immix_collector.rs:480`) adds a link and reclaims nothing; `ensure_capacity` (`src/collector.rs:497`, `src/immix_collector.rs:447`) is the collecting growth API — it collects **then** grows, so the collection collapses the chain first and the growth starts from one space; `collect`/`collect_minor`/`collect_major`/`collect_kind` collapse the chain but must be asked for. A caller that only ever grows accumulates the chain until `max_heap_words` refuses.
- `Collector::collect_major` (`src/collector.rs:383`) and `GenImmixCollector::run_major` (`src/immix_collector.rs:635`) fold the young heap's occupancy into `HeapPolicy::mature_after_fullsweep` **clamped** to the capacity the collapse is about to install: `young_occupancy = nursery.used_words().min(young_after_fullsweep(nursery.capacity_words(), live))`, so the mature target is `next_words(MATURE_TARGET_LIVE_MULTIPLE × (live + young_occupancy + reserve))` — a function of the **live set** alone. The clamp is what makes `tests/mature_capacity_convergence.rs` and `tests/mature_capacity_feedback.rs` hold: `Nursery::used_words()` is chain-summed, so unclamped it also counts every word the mutator (or a host's unrooted BIF copy-out) has allocated and abandoned since the last collection, and a dead figure folded into the mature target is the one feedback no collection can pay back (the next fullsweep measures it again). Clamping keeps the term's purpose — a whole nursery's worth of promotion room, so a *run* of minors follows a fullsweep — bounded by `YOUNG_SHRINK_FACTOR × young_target_words(live)`, past which the collapse installs the target itself.
- Forensic signature of a capacity feedback rather than a policy blow-up: a process's `minor_gcs` flat at 0 while its `total_heap_size` climbs — no collection reached it (only `ensure_heap`/`ensure_heap_full`/the park arm/`reds_door` OR `collection_debt`, and the unrooted `alloc_words` never collects; see `beam_rt/src/context/gc.rs`), so `live_target_words` stays `None`, the growth seam climbs its table, and the retained chain is what the reported figure accumulates. A second signature is `heap_size` flat while `total_heap_size` climbs — that half is the **mature** capacity, and because both of its inputs are live-set functions (the exact live set and the *clamped* young occupancy) such a climb means the **live set itself** is growing, not the allocation history; a mature capacity that tracks a dead occupancy is a rule reading `Nursery::used_words()` unclamped.
- The invariants a monotonically growing capacity would violate are pinned by `tests/nursery_bounded.rs` (a settled capacity is a live-set target or the capacity it already had, never a third value), `tests/nursery_growth_retention.rs` ("a settled capacity is a function of the live set and never of the iteration count"; a grow + debt⇒collect loop keeps the chain at ≤1 retired space), `tests/mature_capacity_convergence.rs`, `tests/mature_capacity_feedback.rs` (the mature capacity is flat in the dead occupancy: 4 062 and 16 062 dead words both size it to 987, two consecutive fullsweeps are a fixed point, and a dropped live set gives the capacity back) and `tests/capacity_ratchet.rs`.
- **A chain is short, but it is not hidden: `erlang:memory(processes)` reads the chain sum.** Each growth installs a space strictly larger than the one it retires and the plain candidate is `next_words(2 × capacity)` (`src/nursery.rs:396-402`), so the retired tail sums to less than the space that replaced it — a chain's summed capacity stays under ~2× the current capacity, and its length is `O(log)`. Because `HeapObservable::heap_size_words`/`total_heap_size_words` (`src/collector.rs:654-661`) are that sum and are what `beam_rt`'s `processes` category reads, a retained chain is **visible** in the reported figure: a large `processes` against a much smaller `processes_used` is the retired-chain signature (a retired space's `used_words` still counts in `heap_used_words`, but `processes_used` reads `young().used_words()` — the current space only, the hole in `src/ACCOUNTING.md`). A reported total far **below** RSS therefore points the other way: at the bytes no key counts — the runtime's pointer-stable arena, pooled BIF scratch heaps, retained `Module` IR and the JIT block cache (`src/ACCOUNTING.md`, "Categories this crate cannot see").
- **A nested/blocking run never collapses the chain.** `Nursery::collapse` has exactly two production callers (`src/collector.rs:361`, `src/immix_collector.rs:622`) plus the two `collapse_after_fullsweep` sites, all inside a collection; a host that declines every collection in a window (e.g. `beam_rt`'s relocation-exempt blocking `compile:file/2` pump) leaves the spaces its `grow_nursery` retirements created alive until the next slice collects — `collection_debt` is a real retained allocation for that whole window.
- **The growth seam ratchets nothing by itself; a collection-free growth loop is bounded by the caller's collection cadence, not by its own event count** — measured by `tests/capacity_ratchet.rs`. A growth always makes strict progress, so the retained chain is `O(log)` long in *spaces* for a given total and its summed capacity is bounded by the words actually installed (never more), but a nursery that is **never** collected still grows with its allocation: that is the honest residual, and the fix belongs to the caller (the C answers the same situation by collecting — `delay_garbage_collection` sets `F_FORCE_GC`, `erl_gc.c:500-599`). The properties pinned are strict progress (a growth installs a strictly larger space than it retires), geometric chain growth, the figures as a faithful reserved-capacity account, refusal inertness for the two legitimate ceilings, the O(log) empty-space re-adoption, and the collapse control. `Nursery::empty_retired_for` (`src/nursery.rs:456`) selects the smallest adequate provably-empty space from the `empty_retired` index (`BTreeSet<(capacity, serial, base)>`) in `O(log #spaces)`, so a 50 000-event growth loop completes in milliseconds instead of scanning the whole chain per event.

## From-space lifetime — retired, freed, what a stale word sees
`beam_gc` releases a region at exactly four places, each reached only by a **collection** (plus the owner being dropped at process death); nothing in this crate can tell a word that still names a released space from a live word, because the from-set is an address range, not a generation.
| free point | code | what is released |
|---|---|---|
| nursery collapse (minor and fullsweep) | `src/nursery.rs:576` `self.retired.clear()` — via `collapse`/`collapse_after_fullsweep` `:544`/`:561`, called from `Collector::collect_minor` `src/collector.rs:361`, `Collector::collect_major` `src/collector.rs:467`, `GenImmixCollector::run_minor` `src/immix_collector.rs:622`, `::relocate_major` `src/immix_collector.rs:881` | every retired `HeapSpace`'s block (`retired: Vec<HeapSpace>`, `src/nursery.rs:116`) |
| collapse replacement | `src/nursery.rs:583` `self.current = HeapSpace::with_allocator(alloc, target)?` | the old current space's block — only when `capacity_words() != target`; the equal branch `:580` `current.reset()` rewinds the bump pointer and frees nothing (`src/space.rs:102`, contents untouched) |
| fullsweep mature replacement | `src/collector.rs:452` `self.mature = fresh;` (after `Copier::run` at `:451`) | the old mature `HeapSpace` |
| mark-region relocation | `src/immix_collector.rs:858` `self.mature = fresh;` in `relocate_major` (a refused trial region is dropped at `:846-868`) | the old `ImmixSpace` region |
Process death drops the whole `AnyCollector` (`../../beam_process/src/ptab.rs:2598` `drop(process)`, after `take` at `:2350`) — nursery chain and mature together. `HeapSpace` has no `Drop` of its own (`src/space.rs:37`); the release is `beam_alloc`'s `impl Drop for Heap` (`../../beam_alloc/src/heap.rs:242-260` → `alloc.dealloc` at `:259`).
**Whether that becomes an `munmap` is `beam_alloc`'s decision, not this crate's**: `Allocator::dealloc` (`../../beam_alloc/src/allocator/core.rs:484-571`) routes by block size/alignment (`core.rs:314-328`) — ≤1024 B (`SizeClasses::default_small_block` top) → slab free list, stays mapped and reusable; 1025 B…`sbct` (512 KiB) → variable-size carrier free space, unmapped only once the whole carrier is free (`core.rs:496-524`); ≥ `sbct` → dedicated carrier `unmap` (`core.rs:527-537`) whose segment the `mseg` reuse cache normally keeps mapped (`../../beam_alloc/src/mseg/state.rs:142-156`, `max_cache_size` 10 → `destroy`/`os_unmap` only on eviction). A nursery of 233/987 words (1.9/7.9 KB) is on the mid path; ≥65 536 words (512 KiB) is a dedicated carrier.
- **Retired ≠ freed.** `Nursery::grow`/`install` (`src/nursery.rs:381`, `:501`) only push the superseded `HeapSpace` onto `retired`; the memory stays mapped, `Nursery::contains` (`:261`) still answers `true` for it and `add_to_from_set` (`:274`) still evacuates it. A word into a retired space is indistinguishable from a live one and reads real (or later-reused) bytes. `readopt_retired` (`:477`) re-installs a provably empty space.
- **Recycling aliases a stale word onto a new space.** `FromSet::contains`/`locate` (`src/space.rs:189`, `:203`) are pure address tests and `Visited` (`src/reachable.rs:272`) is keyed by address: a freed region reused for a fresh `HeapSpace` makes an old word land inside a *live* `[base, top)`, so the copier forwards it as a live reference (`Target::Live`, `src/copy.rs:334-361`) and the walk admits it as an object — garbage sized from whatever header word sits there.
- **Root-set completeness is the host's claim, and this crate never checks it.** `collect*`/`ensure_capacity`/`for_each_reachable_object{,_view}` take a flat `&mut [Eterm]` (there is no `RootSet` here); `collect_minor` copies (`src/collector.rs:339`) and *then* collapses (`:361`), so any live word the caller omitted is left pointing into a space freed two statements later. No post-collection verification, no debug assertion and no generation tag exists to catch that — the only defence is the host's unscanned-region valve (`ScannedBounds`, `src/bounds.rs`, a **fast-reject only**).
- **The walk trusts the header for size.** `move_size` (`src/copy.rs:70`) derives an object's word count from its header and `walk_reachable` only checks the *first* word is in a range (`src/reachable.rs:88`, `:103`), so `for_each_reachable_view`'s `core::slice::from_raw_parts(ptr, total)` (`:220`) and `for_each_ref`'s `ptr.add(offset)` (`src/copy.rs:135`) are not bounded by the covering range's `hi`. With a well-formed heap this never matters; over a conservatively-admitted garbage word it is an out-of-region read.
- `Nursery::grow` never frees and never moves a term (`Collector::grow_nursery`, `src/collector.rs:530`; `Process::grow_nursery`, `../../beam_process/src/process.rs:1747`, adds only `record_max_heap_verdict`).
- Nothing here dereferences a cons cell through `beam_term::list::{car,cdr}`: a crash *inside* `list::car` is a mutator-side read of a word a collection had already left dangling, not a read this crate performs.
**Consequence:** a stale word into a collapsed from-space is **not** a deterministic SIGSEGV — it is either unmapped (trap) or mapped and reusable by an unrelated allocation (silent garbage), so a change in allocation SIZE alone can flip a silent wrong answer into a fault. Soundness rests on the root set being complete, never on the free.

## Foreign regions — the relocating compaction surface
The walk and the copy are both **region-generic and public**: a region need not be a process heap, so a host can compact its own word area (a `beam_alloc::ReservedHeap`, a pre-reserved `Vec<Eterm>`, an mmap'd arena, `beam_engine`'s term arena) without a `Collector`, a `Nursery` or a `HeapSpace`.
What remains outside this surface is the *enumerating* and the *publishing*: naming the regions (`FromSet::from_ranges`) and flattening the roots are the owner's job.
Production posture today: the **engine side hosts this surface** — `beam_engine`'s Tier-B `MinimalContext::reclaim_heap`/`reclaim_heap_with_capacity` (`beam_engine/src/context/reclaim.rs`) drives exactly `FromSet::from_ranges` + `LinearDest` + `Relocator::new_checked` + one `forward_roots` per root slice (the machine's root families are handed over by `Machine::for_each_root_mut`), and it deposits the copies **straight into the survivor region** because the relocator bakes each child slot's address in at copy time (a scratch destination would leave the survivor's interior pointers dangling). `beam_rt` still declines any collection that reaches an engine-arena word (`beam_rt/src/context/gc_walk.rs`), so this surface remains the foundation for bounding its two unbounded regions (the `-eval` store's retired chain and the engine arena).
- **From ranges.** `unsafe FromSet::from_ranges(&[(base_address, used_words)])` — its `# Safety` list is the load-bearing part: each base word-aligned, its byte extent readable **and writable** (a copier forwards in place), `used_words` a **prefix of a real live allocation** (an over-stated extent rewrites words no term owns, an under-stated one strands live terms outside the evacuation), the ranges pairwise disjoint, no other thread freeing/reallocating/mutating those words during a copy, and **completeness** (no live term outside the ranges) as the caller's claim — exactly the claim a root slice already carries.
  Zero-length entries are skipped and every entry goes through the one insertion point, so the sorted-by-`lo` invariant and `locate`'s single interpretation of the range geometry are preserved rather than re-implemented; alignment and disjointness are `debug_assert!`s, so a release build trusts the caller.
  `Heap` and `ReservedHeap` share no trait, so enumerating a `Vec<ReservedHeap>` grid is still the owner's job (`beam_engine` does expose `MinimalContext::for_each_heap_region`, which hands out each region's written prefix as a raw-word `&mut [Eterm]`).
- **Destination.** `EvacDest` (+ `Target`) is the public seam: a host's own region type becomes a destination by implementing `classify_boxed`/`classify_cons`/`can_reserve`/`reserve` and declaring walkability with `copied_top() -> Option<*mut Eterm>` — `Some` means copies land in one contiguous run that advances monotonically in reservation order, which is what the Cheney destination scan requires; the default `None` declares the destination unwalkable (the honest answer for the line-granular mark-region space, which keeps its own driver), and `Relocator::new` refuses such a destination with `BadArg` before any copy.
  `LinearDest` is the turnkey impl over a flat host region (`unsafe LinearDest::new(&FromSet, base, capacity_words)`, then `base`/`capacity_words`/`used_words`/`available_words`), so a host never reimplements the forwarding-marker protocol.
  The marker primitives (`forward_slot`/`forward_accounted`/`move_size`/`for_each_ref`) stay `pub(crate)`: a host supplies a destination and calls the driver, never the marker scheme itself.
- **Driver.** `Relocator::new`/`new_checked` → `forward_roots(&mut [Eterm])`, callable **once per root slice**: the property that makes a scattered root set correct is **idempotence** — a rewritten slot names the destination, which the destination classifies `Foreign`, so a repeat call over the same slice copies and rewrites nothing (pinned by `tests/region_relocation.rs` and `src/relocate/tests.rs`) → `complete()`, the Cheney scan of the destination → `Relocation { stats: GcStats, dest_words_used }`; `compact(from, roots, dest)` is the one-shot form.
  `dest_words_used == stats.words_copied` for a linear destination, i.e. the live length of the host's new region; **trimming and releasing that region is the host's**, since nothing here frees a foreign region.
- **Walking a foreign region read-only.** `live_words_in(&FromSet, &[Eterm]) -> usize` is the exact copy volume (hence the right `required_words` for `new_checked`) and `for_each_reachable_in(&FromSet, &[Eterm], visit)` is the same walk; both are thin wrappers over the one walker (`walk_reachable`) and the one counter (`live`), never a second implementation.
  The walk reports **boxed** objects (cons cells are traversed but not reported), so its count reconciles with `objects_copied` by `boxed_words + 2 × cells`.
- **Failure semantics — what a host must plan for.** `new`/`new_checked` mutate nothing: a too-small destination is the typed `heap_exhausted` (`BeamError::Alloc` with `AllocKind::Heap`) and an unwalkable destination is `BadArg`.
  A `heap_exhausted` raised *inside* `forward_roots`/`complete` leaves the source **partially forwarded** — some objects hold forwarding markers, some roots are rewritten — which is not recoverable, so the two sound patterns are `compact` (one root slice, atomic) and `live_words_in` over every slice → `Relocator::new_checked(from, dest, total)` → one `forward_roots` per slice → `complete`.
  Summing `live_words_in` over **overlapping** slices over-estimates, which only refuses conservatively.
- **There is still no root-set trait**: the root contract is a flat `&mut [Eterm]`, so flattening a host's scattered roots is its own job (`beam_engine::context::RootSet` — `x`/`stack`/`fr` — is already that shape).
- **Still absent, by design:** a conservative (word-by-word) scan — every walk descends from exact roots and sizes each object from its header (`move_size` → `beam_term::layout::object_words`), so a word that merely *looks* boxed is never treated as an object start (a fabricated start could yield a garbage size and an out-of-region read; this is also why `ImmixSpace` allocates line-aligned); a mark bitmap or a free-list over an arbitrary address range (the walk's `Visited`, one bit per from-set word, is the natural seed); and any region *enumeration* (`beam_engine` does not depend on this crate — `beam_engine → beam_gc` would be a legal downward, acyclic wave-4 → wave-1 edge).
- `for_each_reachable_binary_payload`/`sweep_unreachable_binaries` still see only a collector's own spaces, so a refc-binary payload named *only* from a foreign region is a false reject for them — and a false reject frees a live payload; a host that compacts a foreign region but sweeps payload liveness must contribute that region's roots itself.
- Per-word predicates that **do** exist: the tag test (`primary_tag`/`is_boxed`/`is_list`/`is_term_word`) plus a containment test (`FromSet::contains`, `HeapSpace::contains{,_term}`, `ImmixSpace::contains`, `AnyCollector::young_contains`/`mature_contains`, and `ScannedBounds::may_contain` as a sound fast-**reject** only).
- The **shape requirement differs between the walk and the copy**: the walk descends from roots and never scans a range linearly, so a from-set need not be a dense object chain (holes are simply never visited; `words_reclaimed` counts them as reclaimed), while the *destination* must be one dense contiguous run scanned `while scan < dest.copied_top()`, and the source must be handed writable words (the marker is written into the evacuated object).

## Test Strategy
- Per-crate in-module `#[cfg(test)]` unit tests plus a `tests/` integration suite; **each integration file's `//!` module doc states the contract it pins and why**.
- The suite is organised around six invariants (exact copy correctness per term shape; generations/cadence/sizing; the nursery chain; the aggregate scanned bounds; Immix; off-heap reclaim), plus cross-cutting adversarial coverage — the detail, per-file map and the mutation-verified cases live in `src/CONTEXT.md`.
- Four gates cover the capacity-feedback and escalation family: `tests/capacity_ratchet.rs` — a growth event may never strand an equal-or-smaller space, so no door but a collection bounds the retained capacity; `tests/mature_capacity_feedback.rs` — the mature capacity is flat in the dead young occupancy, and consecutive fullsweeps are a fixed point; `tests/escalation_bound.rs` — a dead-inclusive fill keeps selecting minor, a genuine over-capacity live promotion escalates, and `collect_kind(Minor)` refuses before copying; `tests/minor_room_upper_bound.rs` — `minor_room_words` is a tight upper bound, exactly `W` words admitting the minor and `W - 1` the typed refusal.
- **`tests/region_relocation.rs`** (+ its `tests/region_relocation/fixtures.rs` module) is the acceptance suite for the foreign-region surface, written **out of crate** so it can only use the public API: a two-region synthetic grid (a `Vec<Eterm>`-backed region and a `beam_alloc::ReservedHeap`-backed one, with cross-region references) proves structure-preserving relocation with the roots rewritten, dead objects not copied (sentinel-verified), per-root-slice forwarding that accumulates with repeats as no-ops, the refc `[BinRef][ErlSubBits]` pair as **two** allocations with its payload pointer verbatim, a fun's opaque descriptor with its free variables relocated, a foreign root left byte-identical (its subtree neither copied nor visited), `for_each_reachable_in`/`live_words_in` agreeing with the compaction, atomic refusal of an undersized destination (source words + roots byte-identical), `dest_words_used == words_copied`, an **out-of-crate `EvacDest` implementation** driving the same relocation, and `BadArg` for an unwalkable destination. Its `//!` doc states the contract; `src/relocate/tests.rs` holds the in-module half.
- Gate (GREEN required): `erts_rust/scripts/cargo.sh test --manifest-path erts_rust/Cargo.toml -p beam_gc` and `erts_rust/scripts/cargo.sh build --manifest-path erts_rust/Cargo.toml --workspace`.
- Cross-checks against the real system ERTS belong to `../tools/differential/`, not here.
