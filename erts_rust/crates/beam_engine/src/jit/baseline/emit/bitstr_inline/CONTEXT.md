# `baseline/emit/bitstr_inline` — the native bitstring shapes
## Intent
The baseline tier's native lowerings of the bitstring family's small, allocation-free shapes: eight match-context shapes plus the private-append construction shape.
Nine shapes are lowered: `bs_get_position`, `bs_set_position`, `bs_test_tail2`, `bs_skip_bits2`, `bs_start_match3`, `bs_start_match4`, `bs_get_integer2`, a fully static `bs_match` and the `bs_create_bin` accumulator build.
The eight match-context shapes are straight-line guarded x86-64 sequences over the frozen `ErlSubBits` match-context words — `[thing_word, base_flags, start, end, orig]` (`erts/emulator/beam/erl_bits.h:60-82`) — that emit **no** helper call.
The construction shape emits one positioned helper call (`jit_open_append_bin` for an x-register accumulator, `jit_open_append_bin_y` for a `Y` slot — both **mutate nothing**) and then straight-line field byte stores.
No sequence here allocates and none raises, so none runs an interpreter step and no moving collection can interleave inside one.
**A shape the emitter cannot prove safe is refused at compile time**: its `*_shape` predicate answers `None`, the `try_inline_bs_*` entry answers `Ok(false)` having emitted **zero bytes**, and `emit/block.rs`'s arm falls through to `emit_bitstr(...)` — the family helper, i.e. exactly the tier's behaviour for every other `Bs*` variant.
**An addressable operand whose run-time value is wrong** (not a match context, a position outside the small range, a window too short, a subject form the extractor cannot read) branches to a local `fallback` label that runs exactly one interpreter step (`emit_step_and_continue`), so the process sees the family's own typed error and never a fabricated value.
The nine `emit/block.rs` arms sit **above** the `_ if bitstrings::claimed(instr) => emit_bitstr(...)` guard arm, because a `match` arm after a `_ if` guard arm is unreachable.
Ground truth is the interpreter's own family body — `crate::families::bitstrings::{matching, commands, raw}` — which is the in-tree oracle; this node adds no semantics of its own.
The tier's width/flag rule has **one** definition here (`integer.rs::field_width_supported`/`field_is_unsupported`), consulted by the shape predicates and by `emit/test/refusal_census.rs`, so the attribution cannot drift from the decision.

  The four label-carrying ones answer `Option<(Shape, (u32, u32))>` — the resolved fail target is part of the accepted shape — and the two start-match ones answer `Option<(StartMatchShape, MatchFail)>`.
- The **label-free** constructors `<Shape>::of(...)` (`TestTail2Shape::of`, `SkipBits2Shape::of`, `StartMatchShape::of`, `GetInteger2Shape::of`, `BsMatchShape::of`, `CreateBinShape::of`), which is what `emit/arith.rs::uses_y` reads: the block's Y gate must derive the same answer as the emitter without needing the label map.

- The shared machinery in `mod.rs`: `emit_ctx_guard` (the `is_boxed` + `HEADER_SUB_BITS` match-context guard, leaving the block base in `MOVE_SCRATCH`), `emit_fallback_and_done` (the `fast_done` jump + the `fallback` interpreter step + the `fast_done` binding), `emit_bit_extract` (a big-endian field out of `(base, bit_pos, num_bytes)`) and `emit_subject_view` (the readable `(base, bit_pos, num_bytes)` window of an **on-heap** `ErlHeapBits` subject; every other subject form jumps to `fallback`).

- The `#[cfg(test)]` per-shape instrument: `SHAPE_COUNT` = 9, the indices `SHAPE_GET_POSITION`/`SHAPE_SET_POSITION`/`SHAPE_TEST_TAIL2`/`SHAPE_SKIP_BITS2`/`SHAPE_START_MATCH3`/`SHAPE_START_MATCH4`/`SHAPE_GET_INTEGER2`/`SHAPE_BS_MATCH`/`SHAPE_CREATE_BIN`, and `inlined(shape) -> u64`, a **compile-time** count of how often a shape's sequence was emitted (an inlined shape calls no helper, so a run-time helper counter cannot see it).
- The two construction-shape caps, `create.rs`-private: `MAX_FIELDS = 16` fields and `MAX_TAIL_BYTES = 256` tail bytes, which bound the block's size and the `emit/compile.rs` `CREATE_BIN_PATH_BYTES` budget.
## The construction shape (`bs_create_bin { dst, segments }`) — `create.rs::try_inline_bs_create_bin`
The compiler's **linear accumulator** build: a `bs_create_bin` whose first segment is a `private_append` of the accumulator itself and whose remaining segments are whole-byte constant-width unsigned big-endian `integer` fields and `string` constants.
It is the binary-construction hot loop of every encoder, so the shape is lowered in place rather than left on the family loop.
The predicate `CreateBinShape::of(module, dst, segments)` is **label-free** (no `LabelMap`, no fail label), so `emit/regalloc.rs`'s four mirrors, `emit/arith.rs::uses_y`, the emitter and the refusal census derive one answer from one call.
Two shape facts come from the construction seam `families::bitstrings::create::seam`: `append_plan` answers *whether this instruction is that shape and what its fields are*, and `open_append` (behind `runtime.rs`'s two doors `jit_open_append_bin`/`jit_open_append_bin_y`, which share the private body `open_append_cursor`) answers *whether the accumulator has room and where its window's bytes start*.
`source` and every `Field::Bits` value resolve to a `Loc` — an in-bound x-register **or an addressable `Y` slot** (`Loc::of`, whose `Y` half is `operands.rs::y_addressable`) — so `uses_y()` is `source.is_y() || dst.is_y() || any Field::Bits value is a Y Loc`, and no `Y` slot is ever charged to `registers()`/`reads()`/`writes()`.
- Run-time guards, in order: (1) `dst`'s frame-window guard (`Loc::guard_store`), so a rejected destination writes nothing; (2) the `Y` accumulator's frame-window **guard only** (`Loc::guard_load` → `operands.rs::guard_y_read`), emitted before every write because the post-helper re-reads address `y_base + k*8` directly; (3) one guarded `Loc::load` plus one `is_small` guard per `Field::Bits` value — `and` with `IMMED1_MASK`, `cmp` to `SMALL_TAG` — because a bignum is legal in a wide field and a non-integer is the family's `badarg`, so a non-small value MUST deopt, and every such guard runs before any write; (4) the single positioned helper call — `jit_open_append_bin` for an x-register accumulator, `jit_open_append_bin_y` for a `Y` slot (`emit_positioned_helper_call`, so the pre-call spill happens and the instruction's own `(func, pc)` is published) — then `test rax, rax; jz fallback` — the **last** guard.
- **The accumulator crosses the block ABI as a slot index, never a word.** `emit/mod.rs::emit_helper_invoke` adds every argument as a `movabs` immediate, so no run-time term can be passed through it: the arm hands over the accumulator's **x-register index** or its **`Y` slot index** and the door follows from the `Loc` — `jit_open_append_bin` reads `machine.x`, `jit_open_append_bin_y` reads the slot through the bounds-checked `Machine::y` (`None ⇒ 0 ⇒ the same je fallback`, never an out-of-frame read). The two `extern "C"` wrappers share one private body (`runtime.rs::open_append_cursor`), so neither duplicates the seam query or the cursor arithmetic.
- **Every post-helper `Y` re-read is unguarded, and the once-up-front guard is what makes it sound.** The helper clobbers every caller-saved register, so a `Y` field value and a `Y` accumulator are re-derived after the call — four instructions each (`mov scratch,[rsp+RUNTIME_SLOT_DISP]` / `mov scratch,[scratch+RUNTIME_YBASE_DISP]` / `lea scratch,[scratch+y_offset(k)]` / `mov dst,[scratch]`), factored into `create.rs`'s `emit_y_slot_address`/`emit_y_slot_value`, with a `Y` field value re-read once per field rather than once per byte. They emit **no** second guard because the frame cannot move between the guard and them — the helper only reads machine state and runs no interpreter step — and that guard is nonetheless load-bearing, because those reads address `y_base + k*8` directly.
- Lowering after the helper: field byte stores at `cursor + field_offset`, where a `Field::Bits` field is `nbytes` stores of `sar_ri(value_re_read, 4 + 8*(nbytes-1-i))` then `mov_mr8`, and a `Field::ConstBytes` field is 8-byte `mov_ri` + `mov_mr` chunks then a `mov_ri` + `mov_mr8` byte tail.
  Every value is re-read after the helper (it clobbers every caller-saved register): an x-register from `machine.x`, a `Y` slot through its unguarded address re-derivation.
  Then the single window-end commit — `mov_mr` of `old_end + tail_bits` into `SUB_BITS_END_DISP` — emitted **inline**, and nothing after it may fail.
  Then the accumulator word (re-read the same way) into `dst` — its cache register, charged in `cacheable_writes`, or a `Y` slot whose address is likewise re-derived — and the in-block continue.
- Fallback: one interpreter step for the **whole** instruction. There is no fail arm — `bs_create_bin`'s `fail`-vs-`NO_FAIL` discrimination stays in the family.
- **The mutation order is the whole correctness argument.** The family's build performs exactly one mutation beyond the field bytes, C's `sb->end = new_position`; committing that word *before* the field stores would make the `fallback` (one whole-instruction interpreter step) append the tail a **second** time, because the window would already cover the bytes — a wrong result. So the commit is emitted last, and both append doors **mutate nothing** (`&Machine` throughout) so every guard that can reach `fallback` runs against the state the interpreter step will see. The payload bytes are the one thing written before the commit, and they are written at the same offsets with the same values on every attempt, so even a post-payload deopt re-produces them.
- **The extraction is arithmetic (`sar`), never logical (`shr`).** The family hands `push_byte_field` the small value *sign-extended* to 64 bits (`small_value(field) as u64`), and the raw word's bit 63 **is** the small value's sign bit, so `sar` reproduces `(value as i64 as u64).to_be_bytes()`. An 8-byte field's top byte has shift count `60`, where a logical shift's zero fill would reach the stored low byte and write `-1` as `0F FF FF FF FF FF FF FF`; a non-negative field has bit 63 clear, so the two shifts agree byte for byte and `sar` is unconditionally right. The count peaks at `4 + 8*7 = 60`. Pinned by `../../bs_create_bin_tests.rs`'s 64-bit rows.
- **A `string` segment's bytes are baked as immediates, never as a `StrT` pointer**: a cached module's string area may be a different allocation, so a table offset would read a stranger's bytes. The bytes are copied out of the module at compile time.
- Declined, each handled by `emit_bitstr` (a slower path, never a wrong result): anything `append_plan` answers `None` for (a plain or later-position append, an append unit not dividing 8, a `binary`/`float`/`utf*` segment, an `all` or runtime size, a `little`/`signed`/`native` flag, a field that is not a whole number of bytes or wider than a machine word, an unresolvable `StrT` window, a plan with no fields); an accumulator or field value `Loc::of` cannot address (a literal-area operand, an out-of-bound x-register index, a `Y` index the emitted `lea`'s `disp32` could not address); a width outside `8..=64` whole bytes; more than `MAX_FIELDS` fields or more than `MAX_TAIL_BYTES` tail bytes; a field list whose offsets do not sum to `tail_bits / 8`; and at run time a non-small field value, a `Y` destination or accumulator slot outside the current frame, or any `open_append` `None`.
## Constraints
- **Rule 0 — never a wrong result, only a slower one.** A shape that cannot be proven belongs on the family helper; the only two outcomes of a lowering are its exact value or an interpreter step.
- **No destination is written on a rejected path.** Every guard that can reject a destination write (`Loc::guard_store` for a `Y` destination, the match-context guard) is emitted **before** that write, so a rejection leaves the machine bit-identical to the pre-instruction state.
- **The commit order follows the shape.** A single-instruction shape (`bs_get_integer2`) commits the context `start` word **before** writing the destination, because `Dst` may be the context register itself — `beam_asm:filter_essentials/1` compiles `ctx == dst == x(3)`; the two context-only mutating shapes (`bs_set_position`, `bs_skip_bits2`) have no destination at all, so they cannot violate this order; `bs_match` commits `start` **once**, after its whole command list, and therefore accepts a destination that aliases the context slot only as its **last** command.
- **A `fallback` is a deopt, so every sequence must be re-execution idempotent.** `emit_step_and_continue` runs one interpreter step that re-executes the **whole** instruction with the same operands, so any guard that can reach `fallback` must run while the machine is still un-mutated: `bs_match` writes `start` exactly once on the accepted path and once on `fail_arm` (where `rsi` already holds the **last successful** command's position, which reproduces `families::bitstrings::matching::commands`' per-command `commit` on the fail path), and its predicate declines a **mid-list** `integer` whose destination is the context slot. The failure this closes: `<<_:2, Field:6>>` over a **1-byte** subject — the per-character body of `emqx_base62:decode/2`, `BsCreateBin` → `BsStartMatch4` fresh → `BsMatch [{ensure_exactly,8},{skip,2},{integer,2,[],6,1,Dst}]` — where a per-command commit made the post-`skip` deopt re-run `ensure_exactly 8` against an already-shortened window and reject a match the family accepts.
- **Only the eight match-context shapes are position-store-free.** None of them allocates or raises, so the position the driver parks on stays the block's, and a `fallback` step stores its own position through the normal deopt.
  The **construction** shape is the one exception: it emits `emit_positioned_helper_call` for the chosen append door (`jit_open_append_bin` or `jit_open_append_bin_y`), so the instruction's own `(func, pc)` is published before the call — the helper can be entered, and a `fallback` taken from its `test rax,rax` arm must park on this instruction rather than on the block entry.
- **`Y` operands are stack slots, never cached x-registers.** No shape's `registers()`/`reads()`/`writes()` includes a `Y` side; a `Y` operand is read and written through the guarded `emit/operands.rs` path, whose guard can only reject.
  Every `Y` **read** — the eight match-context shapes' destinations and the construction shape's accumulator and field values — goes through that same guarded read (`Loc::load`/`Loc::guard_load`).
  The construction shape is the one place a second, **unguarded** read of a `Y` slot follows a guarded one, and it is sound only because the guard was emitted up front and nothing between the two can move the frame (see the construction section).
- **Every byte read is bounded before it is read.** `emit_bit_extract`'s over-read guard (`j + W <= num_bytes`, or `j + 2 <= num_bytes` for the granular form) is emitted before the load it protects, and both loads are byte-wise and assembled most-significant byte first — an x86 multi-byte load is little-endian and would produce the byte-reversed value with no error surfaced.
- **The shape predicate is the single decision.** `emit/block.rs`'s arm, `emit/regalloc.rs`'s `cacheable_touches`/`cacheable_reads`/`cacheable_writes`/`is_inlined`, `emit/arith.rs::uses_y` and the refusal census all consult the same `*_shape` function, so a shape's emitted registers and its refusal cannot disagree with the code that runs.
- Errors are `beam_common::{Result, BeamError}` only; a raw `*mut Eterm` is never touched here — the emitters write bytes and the layout constants are the only view of the frozen words.
- Files stay near the ~1000-line guidance (`mod.rs` 570, `create.rs` 565, `bs_match.rs` 514, `start_match.rs` 401, `tail.rs` 356, `position.rs` 339, `integer.rs` 332, `tests.rs` 122); the per-shape suites are `../../bitstr_inline_tests.rs`, `../../bs_match_deopt_tests.rs` and `../../bs_create_bin_tests.rs`.
- Apache-2.0 header on every `.rs` (byte-exact; the repo license scanner enforces it).
## Routing Table
- `mod.rs` → the module docs, the layout constants and the field-flag bits, the `#[cfg(test)]` per-shape instrument (`SHAPE_COUNT`/`SHAPE_*`/`inlined`), the four shared emitters (`emit_ctx_guard`, `emit_fallback_and_done`, `emit_bit_extract`, `emit_subject_view`) and the `mod`/re-export block the callers resolve through.
- `position.rs` → `GetPositionShape`/`get_position_shape`/`try_inline_bs_get_position` (the `start` word as a `make_small`, with a `MAX_SMALL` guard that falls back to the family's whole-context snapshot) and `SetPositionShape`/`PosSource`/`set_position_shape`/`try_inline_bs_set_position` (a context's `start` word or a non-negative small integer, committed as one word store).
- `tail.rs` → `TestTail2Shape`/`test_tail2_shape`/`try_inline_bs_test_tail2` (the remaining-window compare) and `SkipBits2Shape`/`skip_bits2_shape`/`try_inline_bs_skip_bits2` (a constant `size * unit` skip with `start.checked_add(nbits)`).
- `start_match.rs` → `MatchFail`, `StartMatchShape`/`start_match3_shape`/`try_inline_bs_start_match3`, `start_match4_shape`/`try_inline_bs_start_match4` and the `emit_start_match` body both share (a context word is reused as a plain move; a miss goes to the resolved fail label, the interpreter step, or — for the `resume`/`no_fail` atom forms — the family).
- `integer.rs` → `GetInteger2Shape`/`get_integer2_shape`/`try_inline_bs_get_integer2` and the tier's width/flag rule `field_width_supported`/`field_is_unsupported`/`field_is_little`.
- `bs_match.rs` → `BsMatchCmd`/`BsMatchShape`/`bs_match_shape`/`try_inline_bs_match` over a fully static command list (the running position lives in `rsi`, `start` is written once per path), plus the decoders `bs_match_group_len`/`bs_match_static_size`/`bs_match_integer_flags` the refusal census walks lists with.
- `create.rs` → `CreateBinShape { source, dst, tail_bits, fields, field_offsets }`/`CreateBinShape::of`/`try_inline_bs_create_bin` — the private-append construction shape, its own `PreparedField::{Bits, Const}` pre-pass (`prepare_fields`, which runs *before* the first emitted byte so a value that stopped resolving emits nothing), the two `Y` re-derivation helpers `emit_y_slot_address`/`emit_y_slot_value`, and the `MAX_FIELDS`/`MAX_TAIL_BYTES` caps.
- `tests.rs` → the in-module unit suite (the flag bits against the family's own constants, the width/flag acceptance rule, an inlined `bs_get_integer2` agreeing with the interpreter, and an overrun field taking the fail label).
