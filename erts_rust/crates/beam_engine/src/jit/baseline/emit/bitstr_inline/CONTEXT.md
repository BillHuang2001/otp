# `baseline/emit/bitstr_inline` — the native bitstring shapes
## Intent
The baseline tier's native lowerings of the bitstring family's small, allocation-free matching shapes.
Eight shapes are lowered: `bs_get_position`, `bs_set_position`, `bs_test_tail2`, `bs_skip_bits2`, `bs_start_match3`, `bs_start_match4`, `bs_get_integer2` and a fully static `bs_match`.
Each is a straight-line guarded x86-64 sequence over the frozen `ErlSubBits` match-context words — `[thing_word, base_flags, start, end, orig]` (`erts/emulator/beam/erl_bits.h:60-82`) — that emits **no** helper call.
A sequence here never allocates and never raises, so it runs no interpreter step and no moving collection can interleave inside one.
**A shape the emitter cannot prove safe is refused at compile time**: its `*_shape` predicate answers `None`, the `try_inline_bs_*` entry answers `Ok(false)` having emitted **zero bytes**, and `emit/block.rs`'s arm falls through to `emit_bitstr(...)` — the family helper, i.e. exactly the tier's behaviour for every other `Bs*` variant.
**An addressable operand whose run-time value is wrong** (not a match context, a position outside the small range, a window too short, a subject form the extractor cannot read) branches to a local `fallback` label that runs exactly one interpreter step (`emit_step_and_continue`), so the process sees the family's own typed error and never a fabricated value.
The eight `emit/block.rs` arms sit **above** the `_ if bitstrings::claimed(instr) => emit_bitstr(...)` guard arm, because a `match` arm after a `_ if` guard arm is unreachable.
Ground truth is the interpreter's own family body — `crate::families::bitstrings::{matching, commands, raw}` — which is the in-tree oracle; this node adds no semantics of its own.
The tier's width/flag rule has **one** definition here (`integer.rs::field_width_supported`/`field_is_unsupported`), consulted by the shape predicates and by `emit/test/refusal_census.rs`, so the attribution cannot drift from the decision.
## API Surface
- The eight emitter entries, all `pub(in crate::jit::baseline::emit)` and all answering `Result<bool>`: `Ok(true)` = the whole guarded sequence was emitted, `Ok(false)` = **not a byte** was emitted (`try_inline_bs_get_position`, `try_inline_bs_set_position`, `try_inline_bs_test_tail2`, `try_inline_bs_skip_bits2`, `try_inline_bs_start_match3`, `try_inline_bs_start_match4`, `try_inline_bs_get_integer2`, `try_inline_bs_match`).
- The shape types, one per shape — `GetPositionShape`, `SetPositionShape` (with `PosSource::{Loc, Constant}`), `TestTail2Shape`, `SkipBits2Shape`, `StartMatchShape` (+ `MatchFail::{Resume, Label((u32, u32)), Family}`), `GetInteger2Shape`, `BsMatchShape` (+ `BsMatchCmd::{EnsureAtLeast, EnsureExactly, Skip, Integer}`) — each exposing `registers()`, `reads()`, `writes()` and `uses_y()` for the block's register cache and Y gate.
- The shape predicates, `pub(crate)`: `get_position_shape`, `set_position_shape`, `test_tail2_shape`, `skip_bits2_shape`, `start_match3_shape`, `start_match4_shape`, `get_integer2_shape`, `bs_match_shape`.
  The four label-carrying ones answer `Option<(Shape, (u32, u32))>` — the resolved fail target is part of the accepted shape — and the two start-match ones answer `Option<(StartMatchShape, MatchFail)>`.
- The **label-free** constructors `<Shape>::of(...)` (`TestTail2Shape::of`, `SkipBits2Shape::of`, `StartMatchShape::of`, `GetInteger2Shape::of`, `BsMatchShape::of`), which is what `emit/arith.rs::uses_y` reads: the block's Y gate must derive the same answer as the emitter without needing the label map.
- The rule and the decoders `pub(crate)`: `field_width_supported(nbits)`, `field_is_unsupported(flags)`, and — `#[cfg(test)]` — `field_is_little(flags)`, `bs_match_group_len`, `bs_match_static_size`, `bs_match_integer_flags`, `MatchFail`, all shared with `emit/test/refusal_census.rs`.
- The shared machinery in `mod.rs`: `emit_ctx_guard` (the `is_boxed` + `HEADER_SUB_BITS` match-context guard, leaving the block base in `MOVE_SCRATCH`), `emit_fallback_and_done` (the `fast_done` jump + the `fallback` interpreter step + the `fast_done` binding), `emit_bit_extract` (a big-endian field out of `(base, bit_pos, num_bytes)`) and `emit_subject_view` (a subject's readable `(base, bit_pos, num_bytes)`).
- The frozen layout constants the emitters read (`SUB_BITS_START_DISP`/`_END_DISP`/`_ORIG_DISP`/`_BASE_DISP`/`_BASE_MASK`, `HEADER_DISP`, `HEAP_BITS_SIZE_DISP`/`_DATA_DISP`, `SUB_BITS_HEADER`, `BIN_REF_HEADER`, `BITSTRING_TAG_MASK_WORD`, `HEAP_BITS_SUBTAG_WORD`, `NOT_BOXED_MASK`, `POINTER_MASK`, `IMMED1_MASK`, `SMALL_TAG`) and the five field-flag bits (`BSF_ALIGNED` 1, `BSF_LITTLE` 2, `BSF_SIGNED` 4, `BSF_EXACT` 8, `BSF_NATIVE` 16).
- The `#[cfg(test)]` per-shape instrument: `SHAPE_COUNT` = 8, the indices `SHAPE_GET_POSITION`/`SHAPE_SET_POSITION`/`SHAPE_TEST_TAIL2`/`SHAPE_SKIP_BITS2`/`SHAPE_START_MATCH3`/`SHAPE_START_MATCH4`/`SHAPE_GET_INTEGER2`/`SHAPE_BS_MATCH`, and `inlined(shape) -> u64`, a **compile-time** count of how often a shape's sequence was emitted (an inlined shape calls no helper, so a run-time helper counter cannot see it).
## Constraints
- **Rule 0 — never a wrong result, only a slower one.** A shape that cannot be proven belongs on the family helper; the only two outcomes of a lowering are its exact value or an interpreter step.
- **No destination is written on a rejected path.** Every guard that can reject a destination write (`Loc::guard_store` for a `Y` destination, the match-context guard) is emitted **before** that write, so a rejection leaves the machine bit-identical to the pre-instruction state.
- **The context `start` word is committed BEFORE the destination register is written**, because `Dst` may be the context register itself — `beam_asm:filter_essentials/1` compiles `ctx == dst == x(3)`. The two context-only mutating shapes (`bs_set_position`, `bs_skip_bits2`) have no destination at all, so they cannot violate this order.
- **No `emit_position_store` on any path here.** Nothing allocates and nothing raises, so the position the driver parks on stays the block's; a `fallback` step stores its own position through the normal deopt.
- **`Y` operands are stack slots, never cached x-registers.** No shape's `registers()`/`reads()`/`writes()` includes a `Y` side; a `Y` operand is read and written through the guarded `emit/operands.rs` path, whose guard can only reject.
- **Every byte read is bounded before it is read.** `emit_bit_extract`'s over-read guard (`j + W <= num_bytes`, or `j + 2 <= num_bytes` for the granular form) is emitted before the load it protects, and both loads are byte-wise and assembled most-significant byte first — an x86 multi-byte load is little-endian and would produce the byte-reversed value with no error surfaced.
- **The shape predicate is the single decision.** `emit/block.rs`'s arm, `emit/regalloc.rs`'s `cacheable_touches`/`cacheable_reads`/`cacheable_writes`/`is_inlined`, `emit/arith.rs::uses_y` and the refusal census all consult the same `*_shape` function, so a shape's emitted registers and its refusal cannot disagree with the code that runs.
- Errors are `beam_common::{Result, BeamError}` only; a raw `*mut Eterm` is never touched here — the emitters write bytes and the layout constants are the only view of the frozen words.
- Files stay near the ~1000-line guidance (`mod.rs` 580, `bs_match.rs` 485, `start_match.rs` 401, `tail.rs` 356, `position.rs` 339, `integer.rs` 332, `tests.rs` 122); the per-shape suites are `../../bitstr_inline_tests.rs`.
- Apache-2.0 header on every `.rs` (byte-exact; the repo license scanner enforces it).
## Routing Table
- `mod.rs` → the module docs, the layout constants and the field-flag bits, the `#[cfg(test)]` per-shape instrument (`SHAPE_COUNT`/`SHAPE_*`/`inlined`), the four shared emitters (`emit_ctx_guard`, `emit_fallback_and_done`, `emit_bit_extract`, `emit_subject_view`) and the `mod`/re-export block the callers resolve through.
- `position.rs` → `GetPositionShape`/`get_position_shape`/`try_inline_bs_get_position` (the `start` word as a `make_small`, with a `MAX_SMALL` guard that falls back to the family's whole-context snapshot) and `SetPositionShape`/`PosSource`/`set_position_shape`/`try_inline_bs_set_position` (a context's `start` word or a non-negative small integer, committed as one word store).
- `tail.rs` → `TestTail2Shape`/`test_tail2_shape`/`try_inline_bs_test_tail2` (the remaining-window compare) and `SkipBits2Shape`/`skip_bits2_shape`/`try_inline_bs_skip_bits2` (a constant `size * unit` skip with `start.checked_add(nbits)`).
- `start_match.rs` → `MatchFail`, `StartMatchShape`/`start_match3_shape`/`try_inline_bs_start_match3`, `start_match4_shape`/`try_inline_bs_start_match4` and the `emit_start_match` body both share (a context word is reused as a plain move; a miss goes to the resolved fail label, the interpreter step, or — for the `resume`/`no_fail` atom forms — the family).
- `integer.rs` → `GetInteger2Shape`/`get_integer2_shape`/`try_inline_bs_get_integer2` and the tier's width/flag rule `field_width_supported`/`field_is_unsupported`/`field_is_little`.
- `bs_match.rs` → `BsMatchCmd`/`BsMatchShape`/`bs_match_shape`/`try_inline_bs_match` over a fully static command list, plus the decoders `bs_match_group_len`/`bs_match_static_size`/`bs_match_integer_flags` the refusal census walks lists with.
- `tests.rs` → the in-module unit suite (the flag bits against the family's own constants, the width/flag acceptance rule, an inlined `bs_get_integer2` agreeing with the interpreter, and an overrun field taking the fail label).
