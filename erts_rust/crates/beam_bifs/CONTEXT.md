# `beam_bifs` — BIF Registry (`bif.tab` Surface) & Implementations

## Intent
Owns the **Built-In Function layer** (Stage 5, Part II §H): the `(module, function, arity)` → implementation registry plus the curated BIF surface, contract **Part I §A.7** — names/modules/arities reproduce `../../erts/emulator/beam/bif.tab`, none invented.
`beam_rt` adapts `BifRegistry` into the engine's `ExecutionContext::call_bif(module, function, arity, args) -> Result<Eterm>` seam (`../beam_engine/CONTEXT.md`).
Failure is **return-based** (`../CONTEXT.md` §3): `BadArg`/`BadArith`/`BadArity`/`SystemLimit`, plus `BeamError::Raise` for `error`/`exit`/`throw`/`nif_error`/`undef`/`unsupported_bif` — the engine turns that into a BEAM raise (exception tag in `x(0)`), never `setjmp`/`longjmp`.
**A typed error always beats a wrong result**: an unfaithful shape returns the typed gap, never a fabricated value.
Ground truth (read-only): `../../erts/emulator/beam/` (Routing Table files), `../../erts/emulator/sys/common/erl_float_short.cpp`, and the stdlib sources (`erlang.erl`/`erts_internal.erl`/`ets.erl`/`lists.erl`/`maps.erl`/`io_lib*.erl`/`unicode.erl`/`string.erl`).

## Roster census (authoritative; code-derived, pinned by `tests/roster_audit.rs`)
`bif.tab` declares **556** entries (`bif` 476, `ubif` 73, `hbif` 7), **0 missing**; `tests/bif_tab.rs` pins `IMPLEMENTED_DECLARED = 556`, `MISSING_DECLARED = 0`, `FAST_PATHS = 92`, `REGISTRY_SIZE = 648`.
`default_registry()` = `BifRegistry::with_core_bifs()` = **648 keys** = **556 declared + 92 stdlib fast paths** (`bif.tab`-undeclared exports: `lists` +40 over its 5 declared, 18 fun-taking; `maps` +12 over its 12; 8 `ets:*`; `io`/`io_lib`; `unicode`; `binary:referenced_byte_size/1`; the OTP-30 `pid_to_binary`/`ref_to_binary`/`port_to_binary`; `erlang:memory/0,1`; `erlang:convert_time_unit/3`; `erlang:fun_info/1`; the six `zlib` fast paths).
**Declared ≠ registered ≠ adopted.** Of the 556, **344 are unconditional in-crate** and **212 seam-backed** (one of the sixteen `BifContext` hooks, answering the typed `unsupported_bif` until a host installs it).
Of the 212, the seam that actually reaches a live provider in `beam_rt` is measured, not assumed: 172 are `RuntimeHooks` forwarders of which **36 are bridge-served, 42 are floor-shadowed and 94 are the typed gap** (the per-module split and the re-derivation are in `./src/runtime/CONTEXT.md`); all 40 remaining non-roster seam keys have their hook installed unconditionally or behind a `needs_*` gate that `beam_rt` honours.
**Never infer "served" from "registered": a key can be shadowed** — `beam_rt`'s native floor answers before the registry (`../beam_rt/src/native.rs::try_native`), so an in-crate implementation of a floor-shadowed key is dead code.
The 94-key gap is ranked and dispositioned in `./src/runtime/CONTEXT.md` § *Seam-key disposition*; the recommended first close is the 13-key `erl_debugger` family (constant `false` + 12 × `error:undef`).
The dedup **seam union is 242** = the 14 `hook_sets.rs` tables (246 raw keys − the 4 `LOCAL_FUN_IDENTITY ∩ LOCAL_NODE` keys); **30** union members are *undeclared* (`MEMORY_HOOK_BIFS` 2 ∪ `PORT_HOOK_BIFS` 4 ∪ `FUN_CALLER_BIFS` 24).
**209** keys are runtime-coupled under `src/runtime/` (`./src/runtime/CONTEXT.md`); there are **no dead registrations**.
`tests/roster_audit.rs` builds the union from the 14 public tables (never a hard-coded list) and asserts the partition (556 = 212 + 344), the union size, the per-table/per-module declared counts (25 modules) and every union member's `needs_*` predicate.

### The oracle cross-check behind "556 = 556" (the enumeration method)
`erlang:system_info(bif_list)` is **`badarg`** on OTP 29.1.1 / erts-17.1, so the roster cannot be enumerated that way.
The working enumeration: `code:all_available()` → `{ModuleName :: string(), File, Location}` (the name is a **string**, not an atom — `list_to_atom/1` it) → `beam_lib:chunks(File, [exports])` for the on-disk modules and `M:module_info(exports)` for the `preloaded` ones → keep the `(M, F, A)` for which `erlang:is_builtin(M, F, A)` is `true`.
On OTP 29.1.1 that yields **exactly 556 keys over exactly the same 25 modules** as `../../erts/emulator/beam/bif.tab`, i.e. **no missing declaration** and no invented one. The only asymmetries, all version- or instruction-related and none a gap:
- `erlang:apply/2`, `erlang:apply/3`, `erlang:yield/0` are reported `is_builtin` `true` but have **no `bif.tab` row** in either tree (they are implemented as instructions); `erlang:is_builtin/3` reproduces that trio explicitly (`src/runtime/erlang.rs`, `beam_common.c:2359-2385`).
- `erlang:pid_to_binary/1`, `erlang:ref_to_binary/1`, `erlang:port_to_binary/1` are OTP-30 additions: declared in this tree's `bif.tab:866-868`, absent (`undef`) on the OTP-29 oracle.
- the 29 quoted operator rows (`erlang:'+'/2` …) are invisible to an ASCII-only `exports` scan but are present and `is_builtin`-true on both sides.
Declared `bif.tab` keys per module (sums to 556): `erlang` 268 · `erts_internal` 71 · `ets` 51 · `math` 24 · `binary` 22 · `erts_debug` 20 · `erl_debugger` 13 · `maps` 12 · `os` 10 · `re` 8 · `records` 8 · `atomics`/`code`/`erl_ddll`/`persistent_term` 7 each · `lists` 5 · `prim_file` 4 · `unicode` 3 · `erts_literal_area_collector`/`erts_trace_cleaner` 2 each · `error_logger`/`file`/`io`/`net_kernel`/`string` 1 each.
Re-derive any split from the registry, never this prose.

## API Surface (FROZEN — `beam_rt` and tests design against this; additive-only changes)
Root + `beam_bifs::prelude::*`: `BifRegistry`/`BifFn`/`default_registry`; `BifContext` + its sixteen seams; `RecordDefinitionView`/`RecordFieldView`; `StdoutSink`/`NullSink`/`BytesSink`; `NifLoadError`; `raise_error`/`raise_exit`/`raise_throw`; `exit_reason_from_term`/`reason_term_from_exit_reason`; `TableRegistry`; the `hook_sets.rs` tables + `needs_*` predicates (`beam_bifs::bif_tab` is **not** in the prelude — validation tooling).
- **`registry.rs`** — `BifFn = fn(&mut BifContext<'_>, &[Eterm]) -> Result<Eterm>`; `BifRegistry` (nested `module → function → [(arity, BifFn)]` maps, so `lookup`/`contains` are allocation-free); `with_core_bifs()` = `default_registry()` (the 648-key roster); `register(...)` (replaces on repeat); `call(...)` (unknown key ⇒ `error::undefined_bif`, wrong arg count ⇒ `BadArity`); operator BIFs keep their *quoted* `bif.tab` names (`"+"`, `"=<"`, `"=:="`, `"!"`, `"++"`, `"not"`, …), and the per-module `register` calls run in a fixed order (`ets` wiring `ets::{matches,specs,extra,adopt,nav,reverse}`; `runtime` last).
- **`context.rs`** — `BifContext<'a>`, the one per-call context: `heap`/`atoms`/`self_pid`/`current_module`, one optional slot per seam, the constructors `int`/`atom`/`intern_atom`/`boolean`/`ok`/`make_float`/`tuple`/`cons`/`list`, `pub(crate) atom_and_heap` (the one borrow-table-and-heap route), the `ProcessInfoItem` enum and the `OutputSink` impls.
- **The sixteen optional seams** (each a `with_<seam>()` builder + accessor): `OutputSink` (`write_str`); `SendHook` (`send`); `AtomInterner` (`intern`); `FunCaller` (`call_fun`, plus the **defaulted** `call_fun_with_tables(fun, args, tables: Option<&mut TableRegistry>)`); `PortHook` (`open_port`/`close_port`/`port_info`); `DictHook` (`put`/`get`/`erase`/`get_keys`/`get_keys_for_value`, plus the defaulted `get_all`/`erase_all`); `PersistentTerm` (`get`/`put`/`erase`/`get_all`/`info`); `NifLoader` (`load_nif`); `RecordSource` (`record_definition`); `LocalNode` (`local_node`, plus the **defaulted** `local_node_hash(&mut self) -> Option<u32>`); `LocalFunIdentity` (`local_fun_identity`); `ModuleInfo` (`module_info`); `ProcessHook` (`whereis`/`register`/`unregister`/`registered`/`is_process_alive`/`processes`/`ports`/`process_info_keys`/`process_info_item`/`process_flag`/`group_leader`); `MemoryHook` (`memory`); `RuntimeHooks` (`call`); `ExportGate` (a **shared** `&'a dyn beam_codec::ExportGate`, `export_gate() -> Option<&'a dyn beam_codec::ExportGate>`) — the `[safe]` `binary_to_term/2` active-export predicate; with none installed the codec's check is skipped.
- **Every hook is defaulted** — with none installed the BIF answers the typed `unsupported_bif` gap (or a documented `Ok(None)`/fallback for a value-returning seam), **never** a fabricated empty list, `undefined`, `false`, zero or made-up count.
  Unset defaults: stdout for `io:format`; `unsupported_bif` for `send/2`/`"!"/2`/`send/3`, the port/dict/`PersistentTerm`/`ProcessHook`/`ModuleInfo` BIFs, `load_nif/2` and the fun-taking `lists`/`maps` BIFs **for a valid fun** (an invalid fun fails the clause guard first); `TermNotFound` for a new-atom BIF; `Ok(None)` for `record_definition`/`local_fun_identity`/`module_info`/`local_node_hash`; `(nonode@nohost, 0)` for `local_node`, else `None`.
- **`hook_sets.rs`** is the one home of the *seam-consumer* key sets: a `&'static` table per seam + an allocation-free `needs_*` predicate, re-exported at the root and in the prelude and `contains`-checked against the live registry.
  Sizes: `MEMORY_HOOK_BIFS` 2, `LOCAL_FUN_IDENTITY_BIFS` 4, `DICT_HOOK_BIFS` 7, `PROCESS_HOOK_BIFS` 11, `PORT_HOOK_BIFS` 4, `SEND_HOOK_BIFS` 4, `NIF_LOADER_BIFS` 1, `EXPORT_GATE_BIFS` 1, `RECORD_SOURCE_BIFS` 1, `MODULE_INFO_BIFS` 2, `PERSISTENT_TERM_BIFS` 7, `FUN_CALLER_BIFS` 24, `LOCAL_NODE_BIFS` 6, `RUNTIME_HOOKS_BIFS` **172** (asserted *equal* to `runtime::register`'s seam roster) — the 14 tables total **246** keys, dedup union **242**; the per-row counts and the aggregate are pinned by `the_module_doc_table_counts_match_the_tables`; `AtomInterner` deliberately has **no** table (its consumer set is unbounded); a host gates its seams through these, never a private copy.
- **`error.rs`** — `pub(crate)` `badarg`/`badarith`/`badarity`/`system_limit`/`undefined_bif`/`unsupported_bif`/`function_clause`; public `raise_error`/`raise_exit`/`raise_throw` over `beam_common::{Raise, ExitReason, ExceptionClass}`; `raise_term`/`raise_error_term` set `reason = ExitReason::Structured` with the term in the opaque `Raise::with_live_reason`/`LiveReason` slot (`as_atom()` stays `None`); `structured_error` builds `{Tag, Elements…}`, degrading to plain `badarg()` when `tag` cannot be interned.
- **`reason.rs`** — `exit_reason_from_term(term, &AtomTable) -> ExitReason` (`normal`/`shutdown`/`noproc`/`noconnection`/`killed`/`aborted` ⇒ their variants, any other atom ⇒ `Atom(name)`, non-atom/unknown ⇒ `Structured`); `reason_term_from_exit_reason` rejects `Structured` with `BadArg`; the `error/1,2,3`, `exit/1`, `throw/1`, `nif_error/1,2` BIFs run their argument through it — an **atom** reason keeps its exact `ExitReason` with **no** live slot, a **non-atom** one is `Structured` carrying the term.

## Constraints
- **Errors:** `beam_common::{Result, BeamError}` only, never a crate-local error enum, never `panic!`, never `unwrap()` on runtime data, never a wrong result.
  `BadArg`/`BadArith`/`BadArity`/`SystemLimit` for the argument/numeric/arity/size failures, the typed `unsupported_bif` **raise** for a capability gap — a capability gap is never reported as `badarg` (the argument is valid) and a `badarg` is never reported as a capability gap.
- **Never re-implement term order/size/hashing/construction.** Comparison ⇒ `beam_term::compare::{cmp, cmp_arith}`; hashing ⇒ `beam_term::hashing` (`term_hash` + the compatibility-pinned `make_hash`/`make_hash2`, no local copy); construction/inspection ⇒ `beam_term` accessors only (via `access.rs`); allocation ⇒ `beam_alloc::Heap`.
- **`unsafe` confinement.** The crate is `#![deny(unsafe_code)]`; the only two `#[allow(unsafe_code)]` modules are `src/access.rs` (term/int/float/bignum/bitstring/flatmap/native-record wrappers) and `src/calendar/localtime.rs` (the C time-library bridge); `unsafe_op_in_unsafe_fn` stays clean.
- **Deps (fixed by `Cargo.toml`):** `beam_common`, `beam_term`, `beam_alloc`, `beam_abi`, `beam_process`, `beam_message`, `beam_codec`, `beam_ets`, `beam_gc`, `libc`; **no `beam_loader` edge** (native-record definitions arrive through the `RecordSource` seam); a new dependency edge must be reported to `../`.
- **Files target ~1000 lines**, several exceeding that only via their in-module `#[cfg(test)]` suites; the printer and the `ets` bridge are **split by responsibility**.
- **Licensing Apache-2.0**; every `*.rs` carries the OTP `// %CopyrightBegin% … // %CopyrightEnd%` Apache-2.0 block.

## Notes for Agents
- **`access.rs` and `bits.rs` are PRIVATE** (`mod access;`/`mod bits;`); the cross-crate public surface is exactly the `pub use` list in `src/lib.rs` + `prelude`, with **no public term construction/destruction helper module**.
- **Two routes to destructure/construct boxed terms from safe Rust:** (a) the registered BIFs through `BifRegistry::call`; or (b) the sibling **safe** helpers `beam_engine::terms::*` / `beam_codec::term::*` (`make_float`/`make_big`/`make_flatmap`/`make_binary`/`make_bitstring`, `flatmap_parts`/`tuple_arity`/`tuple_element`/`cons_head`/`cons_tail`/`bitstring_bytes`/`float_value`); `BifContext` constructs floats, lists, tuples, cons, small ints and atoms safely but has **no** binary/bitstring, bignum, map or record constructor.
- **Oracle cross-checks back every hard algorithm.** Bare BIF names are **not** auto-imported under `-eval` (fully qualify); several probes need a **compiled module** (`erlc` on a fixture) — e.g. `erts_debug:flat_size/1` and `lists:sort/2`'s degenerate comparator; most "matches the oracle" unit tests are pinned-golden self-checks, and the integration files that shell out to system `erl` are **fail-loud**.
- **The recorded `erlang:list_to_existing_atom/1` divergence is NOT this crate's** (`../../tools/differential/divergences.md` misattributes it; `list_to_existing_atom_1` is a pure `BifContext::atom` lookup that never interns): on the `-eval` route the queried name is already in the runtime's ONE shared `AtomTable` because `beam_rt` eagerly interns every `{primLoad,…}` name of `bootstrap/bin/start.boot` (202 vs the oracle's 87) plus each loaded module's `Atoms` chunk (`../beam_rt/src/codeserver.rs`, `../beam_rt/src/boot/bootscript.rs`) — owner: `beam_rt` (`../beam_rt/src/boot/CONTEXT.md`), the divergence list's attribution owed a correction by the corpus owner.
- **`erlang:monotonic_time/0` is negative by design** (the ERTS epoch, `start_time` = `-576460752000000000`) — never re-introduce a "non-negative / process-relative / small" assumption, and use `div_euclid` (floor), not `/`, for unit brackets in time tests.
- **Build env:** sandbox `$HOME` is read-only — run cargo only via `erts_rust/scripts/cargo.sh … --manifest-path erts_rust/Cargo.toml -p beam_bifs`, and keep a `build --workspace` warning-free; CI is `.github/workflows/erts_rust.yml` (`../CONTEXT.md`).
- **Clippy state:** `cargo clippy -p beam_bifs --all-targets` **exits 0** (the deny-by-default `clippy::approx_constant` errors are gone: every golden float table that spells `3.14159`/`3.14` — values that are *not* `f64::consts::PI` — carries a smallest-enclosing-item `#[allow]` with a rationale, because the literal is the test's subject and substituting a constant would silently reframe the assertion). ~230 pre-existing *warnings* remain across ~37 files (`binary.rs`, `print/pretty*`, `lists.rs`, `re/*`, `too_many_arguments`, `very complex type`, and golden-float `excessive_precision` rows that must NOT be "fixed"); a blanket `-D warnings` pass is a separate workstream, so keep new code free of diagnostics instead of trying to zero the crate.

## Known Issues / typed capability gaps
**No known silent wrong *values*.** Every capability gap returns a typed `BeamError`, and every argument error matches the oracle's class; entries marked *divergence* are honest but not byte-identical.
### Divergences
- **`math:erf/1`/`erfc/1` are approximate** (`src/math.rs`): `erf` ≤ 4 ULP, `erfc` up to ~506 ULP — enough to change a `~w` shortest render; Rust has no `f64::erf`/`erfc` and glibc fdlibm is not Apache-2.0, so `tools/differential` does not pin the pair (it does pin the bit-exact `atanh/1`).
- **`erlang:display/1` writes a printable latin-1 char ≥ `16#80` as UTF-8, not the oracle's raw code unit** (≤ `16#7F` byte-identical; `io:format("~p",…)`/`display_string/2` unaffected); the fix is a byte-oriented `OutputSink` (`src/context.rs` + `beam_rt`), not a local one.
- **A `FunKind::Local` (`make_fun3`) fun is the typed `unsupported_bif` gap** in the printer and in `erlang:fun_to_list/1` (shared): its frozen descriptor is only `(uniq, index)`, so the module/name a `#Fun<…>` needs is not in the term and any text would be a silently-wrong result.
- **A non-small-integer `{chars_limit,N}` is rejected up front**, where the oracle fails only when arithmetic *reaches* the value; in-range small-integer limits are byte-identical.
- **`get_module_info`'s `exports`** lists loader-order exports, not the oracle's global export-table order (same set, order only — accepted at `../beam_rt/src/context/module_info.rs`); every other item matches.
- **`erts_internal:term_type/1` reports `export` for an `Imported` fun** where the oracle reports `fun` (yet `fun_info/2`'s `type` reports `local` for the same term).
- **Reject-where-the-oracle-succeeds / class mismatch:** `re:inspect/2` of a non-`namelist` item is the typed gap where the oracle raises `badarg`; `lists` of a non-list/improper argument raises `badarg` where the oracle raises `function_clause`/`case_clause`/`{bad_generator,_}`; no differential case covers these.
- **Node-identity ordering of a mixed internal/external `pid`/`port`/`ref` pair is only as faithful as the frozen layout allows** (`beam_term`'s, not this crate's): an external id whose node **name** equals the local one but whose `creation` differs is not ordered the way the oracle orders it (the M1 frozen term cannot see the oracle's node-pointer shortcut), and a mixed port pair at equal nodes follows `beam_term`'s documented total convention where the C compiles in an internal error.
### `[safe]` `EXPORT_EXT` over-accepts (accept-where-the-oracle-rejects)
- **A `[safe]` decode materializes an `EXPORT_EXT` for any `M:F/A`**, where C's `erts_decode_ext` requires an **active** export entry (`external.c:5032-5036` → `erts_active_export_entry`, `export.h:132-134`/`:175-183`) and otherwise `badarg`. Oracle-verified (OTP 29.1.1): `[safe]` accepts `lists:map/2` and `erlang:system_info/1`, and rejects `lists:map/3`, `lists:no_such_fun_xyz/1`, `erlang:no_such_bif_xyz/1` and an unloaded `mnesia:start/0` — a decode-created stub never satisfies the gate, so the correct predicate is `erlang:function_exported/3`'s, not "some export entry exists". The rule exists **only** on the `binary_to_term/2` route: `erts_decode_dist_ext` passes `flags = 0` (`external.c:1296`) and `NEW_FUN_EXT` has no safe arm (`external.c:5102-5176`) — neither may grow one.
- Both halves are wired — the codec's check site (`Decoder::with_export_gate`, reached through `decode_term_with_options_and_gate_used`) and this crate's seam (`BifContext::export_gate`, roster `EXPORT_GATE_BIFS`/`needs_export_gate`, consulted only by `decode_term_bif`, which falls back to `decode_term_with_options_used` byte-identically when no gate is installed). With no gate the check is skipped, so a runtime that installs none still over-accepts; the loaded-module registry that answers it is `beam_rt`'s to supply (Handoffs). The atom half is the codec's (`Decoder::safe`/`atom_term`).
- Adjacent, same owner: `decode_export` reads its module/function atoms through `Decoder::decode_node_atom`, which accepts the `NIL_EXT` internal-node marker in `[local]`/internal-node mode, while C's `EXPORT_EXT` uses plain `dec_atom` (`external.c:5014`, `:5017`; `NIL_EXT` is accepted only by `dec_sysname`) — so a `[local]`-wrapped `EXPORT_EXT` with `NIL_EXT` as its module is `badarg` on the oracle and materialized here.

### Environment-bounded values and bounded M1 leaks
- **`ets:all/0` answers only this crate's registry, ascending-id** (the oracle also lists the node's system tables, in no order) — the only `ets` value divergence.
- **Never-reclaimed registries (bounded M1 leak):** the compiled-pattern, `ets` continuation/compiled-spec and `atomics`/`counters` cell registries have no off-heap finalizer; the one *stale-word* hazard is the `ets` compiled-spec registry (`TableRegistry::insert_spec`/`spec_raw` store the caller's spec **`Eterm` verbatim** in a non-root registry that outlives the call), whose fix needs table-owned storage (a `beam_rt`/`beam_ets` change, Handoffs); the compiled-pattern registry deep-copies.
- **`time` native units are fixed (ns) and `monotonic_time/0,1` reports the ERTS monotonic epoch**: `ERTS_MONOTONIC_TIME_START_EXTERNAL + elapsed` = `-576460752000000000` ns plus the process-global `Instant` elapsed time (`erl_time.h:235-239,312-323`), i.e. a large **negative** reading inside `erlang:system_info(start_time)..(end_time)`; `/1` units are floored divisions of it, so `monotonic_time(U) == erlang:convert_time_unit(monotonic_time(), native, U)` for every accepted unit. `time_offset/0,1` is `system_time − monotonic_time`, hence a bignum carrying the `|epoch|` and satisfying `system_time() == monotonic_time() + time_offset()`. `unique_integer >= 0`; `time_unit` accepts the oracle's atom set and a **positive bignum** unit IS supported (`crate::time::{convert_unit_big,int_from_i128}`, floor semantics; past `BIG_ARITY_MAX` is `badarg`); **`error/2` ignores its second argument**, `nif_error/2`'s is crash-report metadata, `error/3` mirrors `error/2`; **`prim_*`** modules are otherwise unimplemented (only the three `os_bif.rs` `prim_file` helpers).
- **The printer cannot emit a `beam_term::ops: class: invalid boxed header subtag …` panic** — that is a corrupted/stale-term symptom (a flat-map render with a **non-atom** key, `../beam_rt/M1_GAPS.md`), not a printer input: no `src/print.rs`/`src/print/pretty/` path calls `beam_term::ops::class` or panics.

## Design Decisions
- **Stateful/global BIFs route through seams** — the crate owns neither global state nor OS machinery; the two seam-free families are the `atomics`/`counters` cells and the `erl_ddll` driver registry + error table, and a resource handle is an on-heap `Ref` thing over a module-local registry (`./src/runtime/CONTEXT.md`).
- **`function_clause` is a bare-atom raise**; `create`/`update` recognize the by-name atoms (`is_exported`, `'_'`) with a local helper, not fixed `AM_*` indices.
- **`self/0` reads `BifContext::self_pid`, not a global** (the engine is a pure state machine); a match spec's `self/0` is likewise resolved by a scoped `PidHook` install, never a global (`src/ets/self_hook.rs`'s `SpecEval` — `./src/ets/CONTEXT.md`).
- **`BifContext::heap()` is the caller's *destination* heap** — `beam_rt`'s pooled, never-reset `Runtime::bif_heaps` arena (`take_bif_heap`/`put_bif_heap`, default `1<<24` words) **outside** the GC-scanned spaces, so a result built there is unscanned, the runtime's unscanned-root valve *declines* such a collection, and heap exhaustion surfaces as `error:system_limit`; this crate never re-roots or copies down.
- **The fun-taking fast paths deliberately publish *no* GC roots** (measured): their live `Eterm`s in Rust locals cannot see a collection, because the latch is armed only for the slice's own machine (every nested run has `gc_armed == false`, `beam_rt/src/context/bridges.rs::FunBridge`) **and** the process is `relocation_exempt` for the whole run (`RuntimeContext::apply_fun`) — two independent refusals, so a stale-word hazard is a runtime-level store, not a BIF local (Handoffs).
- **The `ets` table handle is an on-heap `Ref` thing** (the M1 `tid()` substitution) for a normal table and the **name atom** for a `named_table`; a BIF takes the table out of the registry to release the `ctx.atoms()`/`ctx.tables()` borrow, and continuations/compiled specs are `Ref` things over registry keys likewise.

## Performance Notes (per-BIF-call hot path)
- **The registry lookup is allocation-free:** the nested maps hash borrowed `&str`s via `Borrow<str>` (no key built) and scan the 1–2 element arity `Vec`; a `beam_rt` dispatch reaches it twice (`contains`, then `call`→`lookup`), allocating nothing.
- **The comparison BIFs resolve the local node once per call, never per element:** `src/compare.rs::local_node_id` turns the `BifContext::local_node()` seam into a `Copy` `LocalNodeId` at the top of each BIF, and `cmp_arith_node`/`cmp_exact_node` consult it only for a pair `access::is_mixed_identity_pair` recognises — a small-int/atom/tuple comparison takes exactly the path it always did, and a `lists:sort/1` pays nothing per element where the accessor itself would cost an atom-table lookup or a hook call per comparison.
- **Small-integer arithmetic allocates nothing:** `erlang:'+'/2`, `'-'/2` and `'*'/2` take a machine-arithmetic fast path when **both** operands are small ints (`src/arith.rs::small_operand` → checked `/`/`-`/`*` → `int_from_machine`), materializing the result straight into an immediate — measured **0 alloc / 0 B** for `2+3`, `5-3`, `2*3`. The exact result is range-tested against `MIN_SMALL..=MAX_SMALL` **before** materialization and handed to the existing promotion seam otherwise, so bignum promotion is bit-identical (`MAX_SMALL+1`, `MAX_SMALL*2`, `MIN_SMALL-1` each cost the single widened `Big` allocation). `div`/`rem`/`abs`/the unary operators/the six bit operators deliberately stay on the `Big` path (their sign, two's-complement and `BIG_ARITY_MAX` reasoning is per-operator and none is hot); a mixed int/float operand still widens for the correctly-rounded `big_to_double`.
- **`list_to_binary/1` allocates one byte buffer and nothing else.** `src/binconv.rs::iolist_bytes` is a two-pass walk over **one** `WalkStack` (128 inline `Eterm` frames — 1 KiB of Rust stack — spilling to a `Vec` only past that depth, and the same stack serves both passes): `iolist_byte_len` validates *and* measures (`bits::byte_size`, no payload touch), then `append_iolist` fills a `Vec::with_capacity(total)`. The pre-pass's validation is authoritative for both passes (a term graph cannot change between them); the write pass keeps its `badarg` arms as typed-error guards, never silent skips. A binary element is copied straight in through the closure-scoped `access::with_binary_bytes`, so there is no per-element temporary. Measured by `tests/binconv_alloc.rs`: flat 64-element list **1 / 64**, mixed ints + binary + sublist **1 / 133**, `[]` **0 / 0**, a 200-deep nest 7 / 1025 (the one spill — inherent to an iterative walk of unbounded depth). `make_bin` → `access::make_heap_bitstring` → `Heap::alloc_words` then copies the bytes once into the on-heap `ErlHeapBits` on the caller's preallocated `BifContext::heap()` arena (no payload malloc, no `RefcBinary`/refcount). `iolist_to_binary/1` has its own walk in `src/binary.rs::collect_byte_unit`.
- **`length/1` allocates nothing and `list_to_tuple/1` allocates once.** `src/term_ops.rs::proper_list_len` is an allocation-free count walk (rejecting a non-list/improper tail), and `proper_list` is that count followed by one exact-capacity `Vec` fill — so `length/1` of 1000 elements is **0 / 0** and `list_to_tuple/1` of 100 is **1 / 800**. A rejected list allocates nothing (the failure precedes the buffer). Pinned by `tests/term_ops_alloc.rs`; `ctx.tuple`/`Heap::tuple` still copies the `Vec` into the tuple block on the context heap.
- **Every binary this crate *builds* stays on-heap, above the 64-byte `ERL_ONHEAP_BINARY_LIMIT` too** (`src/bits.rs` module doc, `src/access.rs::make_heap_bitstring`) — a documented placement-policy deviation from C (layout byte-identical), so a 256-byte `list_to_binary/1` result is an `ErlHeapBits` block, not an off-heap refcounted `RefcBinary`. `erlang:byte_size/1`/`bit_size/1`/`tuple_size/1` are allocation-free (a size-word read + `make_small`).
- **The `AtomTable` deep clone is copy-on-write** (`beam_rt`'s `CodeServer::atoms_mut`): only an interning call pays it.
- **The decode scratch revalidates in O(1) in the steady state:** the per-thread *exact image* of the caller's table (`DECODE_SCRATCH`'s `memo`) carries a `beam_term::AtomSnapshot` witness **taken from the shared table itself** (`DecodeScratch::witness`; a clone's fresh identity can never satisfy it), so `memo.len() == witness.len()` plus `shared.appended_since(witness)` proves index-identity with **zero** name reads, and only names appended since are copied.
  `prefix_matches` is the fallback, not the hot path: its Θ(atom-table) `name` reads run only for a replaced or shrunk instance, or a memo left **longer** than the shared table by a failed/`safe` decode, and a rejected memo is rebuilt with one `AtomTable::clone`, the witness refreshed either way.
- **`beam_rt/src/nif.rs` still revalidates its own decode memo index by index** (a private `prefix_matches`): the O(1) witness is **unadopted** there (Handoffs).
- **`term_to_binary/1` and `binary_to_term/1` allocation inventory** (one 5-key-map round trip): encode = **1** `Vec<u8>` — the `Encoder`'s own `Writer`, moved out verbatim by `Encoder::finish` because `term_encoder` writes the version byte into the writer before the body; `compressed_image` takes the image **by value** and hands the same buffer back on the compress-but-not-profitable revert — + the on-heap binary block (`beam_alloc` bump, O(size)); decode = 1 `Vec<u8>` (`access::binary_bytes`'s `to_vec` — still deliberately kept: the closure-scoped `access::with_binary_bytes` cannot serve the decode, whose bytes must outlive the closure because several BIFs return a term built from a slice of them (`binary_to_list/3`, `binary_part/3`), so `bytes_arg` documents why it copies) + 1 `Vec<(Eterm,Eterm)>` (the codec's map `pairs`) + the map block on the context heap; **no** per-call `HashMap`/`HashSet`/`String`/`format!`. Measured by `tests/binconv_alloc.rs` (counting `#[global_allocator]`): a 101-byte image encodes in **1 alloc / 128 B** and decodes in 4 / 285 B; a 4102-byte image encodes in **2 alloc / 4102 B** and decodes in 1 / 4102 B.
  `[local]` costs a **second** image buffer inside `beam_codec` (`Encoder::finish_local`/`finish_compressed` build a fresh `body.len()+6` `Writer`) — 4 alloc / 8241 B for the 4102-byte image, pinned by `tests/binconv_alloc.rs::the_local_option_still_costs_a_second_image_buffer_in_beam_codec`; the in-place fix is the `../beam_codec` handoff.

- **The `erl_ddll` in-crate namespace is complete but SHADOWED on the runtime path** — `../beam_rt/src/native/ddll.rs`'s floor claims `loaded_drivers/0`/`try_load/3`/`try_unload/2`/`info/2` over its own list and models no statically linked built-in, so the runtime answers `{ok,[]}`/`{error,not_loaded}`/`badarg` where the oracle and this crate answer `{ok,["tcp_inet","udp_inet","ram_file_drv"]}`/`{error,linked_in_driver}`/`true` (the two recorded A.7 divergences); fix by prepending the three statics there, or by not claiming those four keys (`./src/runtime/CONTEXT.md`).
  Two `spawn_driver` names have no driver at all: `open_port({spawn_driver,"udp_inet"|"ram_file_drv"})` is live in the oracle but `badarg` here, `../beam_io/src/driver.rs`'s `DriverRegistry::with_builtins` registering only `fd`/`efile`/`tcp_inet` and `../beam_rt/src/port.rs` resolving through `builtin_drivers.create` (`None` ⇒ `badarg`) — a `beam_io` addition.
- **The `[safe]` `EXPORT_EXT` active-export gate is waiting on the host's predicate.** The seam exists here — `BifContext::with_export_gate`/`export_gate`, roster `EXPORT_GATE_BIFS = [("erlang","binary_to_term",2)]` behind `needs_export_gate`, consulted only by `decode_term_bif` — so `beam_rt` must `impl beam_codec::ExportGate` over its code-server view and install it for the `binary_to_term/2` arm, gated through `needs_export_gate`. The predicate is `beam_rt`'s (`is_builtin(tab, registry, M, F, A) || code_server.module(M).is_some_and(|m| module_exports(m, F, A))` — the same predicate as `erlang:function_exported/3`, `src/context/runtime_hooks.rs:1538-1558`, with `CodeServer::module(&str) -> Option<&Module>` at `src/codeserver.rs:602`). Because the gate needs `&runtime.code_server` while `call_registered`'s fallback `FunBridge` arm holds `&mut Runtime`, it requires its **own** dispatch arm (next to `uses_record_source`, `src/context/calls.rs:547-557`), not a hook object on the existing one. Predicate caveat: the code server loads only the 22 `PRELOADED_MODULE_NAMES` (`src/boot.rs:61-84` — `lists` is not among them), so the strict form would turn the currently-agreeing `binary_to_term(term_to_binary(fun lists:map/2), [safe])` into `badarg`; the registry-inclusive variant (`|| registry.contains(M, F, A)`) is the recommended one — it still refuses every genuinely nonexistent MFA.
### Requested from siblings
- **`beam_term` — a checked port constructor.** `make_port_data` silently **masks** (`num & (1<<60 - 1)`), unlike `make_pid_data` (which leaks a carry); `try_make_pid_data` already exists but has no port twin. Add `pub const MAX_INTERNAL_PORT_NUMBER: u64 = (1 << PORT_DATA_SIZE) - 1` (the C's `ERTS_MAX_INTERNAL_PORT_NUMBER`, `erl_node_container_utils.h:250`) + `try_make_port_data(num) -> TermResult<u64>` with `TermError::PortIdOutOfRange` reconciling to `badarg` (`bif.c:4671`). Until it exists `src/port.rs` derives its own bound from `beam_term::PORT_DATA_SIZE` — that check is the **sole** guard against the mask, so a new port-encoding site must not skip it.
- **`beam_process` — checked encoder variants.** `ProcessId::to_eterm`/`PortId::to_eterm` are infallible over the unchecked/masking macros (`src/pid.rs:76-78`); a `ProcessId::try_new(number, serial)` / `PortId::try_new(number)` rejecting past `beam_term::{MAX_INTERNAL_PID_NUMBER,MAX_INTERNAL_PID_SERIAL}` / `MAX_INTERNAL_PORT_NUMBER` would make the invariant type-level instead of a call-site check. Do not change the current signatures — this crate takes the check from `beam_term` instead; `beam_process`'s own `src/CONTEXT.md` already records the debt.
- **`beam_rt`/`beam_io` — latent, not user-reachable masking.** `open_port/2` (`src/port.rs:79`) and the `ProcessHook` re-encode host-supplied `PortId`s, so a host that ever handed out a port number ≥ 2^60 would be silently masked into a wrong port. The port table is sequential today, so it cannot happen; a checked encoder (above) removes the hazard class.
- **`beam_ets` — interior statistics for `ets:info(T, stats)`** — `TableInfo` has no per-table counters (inserts/lookups/deletes), so that item is the typed gap; an additive `TableInfo::stats()` returning the oracle's `[{Item, Value}]` shape is the fix.
- **`beam_process` — `erlang:monitor/3` with a non-immediate tag** — the key is registered here but **shadowed** by `beam_rt`'s native floor (`native/process/monitor.rs`); `beam_process::check_tag` refuses a non-immediate tag (`{a,b}`, a binary) with `error::unsupported_tag`, while the oracle accepts any term and delivers `{{a,b}, Ref, process, P, Reason}`; closing needs `beam_process` to hold a deep-copied tag (a table-owned `TermStore` copy, like `Table::install_heir`) instead of a raw heap `Eterm`.
- **`beam_codec`** — already wired: `with_deterministic()` (by `binconv::encode_body`), the `EXPORT_EXT` **true** arity (`encode_fun_external`/`fun_true_arity_term`, so no >255 truncation) and the `with_local_ext()`/`finish_local(hash)` image; its only remaining gap is the reference-`id` representability one.
- **`beam_codec` — one duplicate image buffer left on the `[local]`/`compressed` paths.** `Encoder::finish_local(hash)` and `Encoder::finish_compressed(level)` (`beam_codec/src/encode/mod.rs`) each allocate a **new** `Writer` of `body.len()+6` and copy the body into it, so `term_to_binary(T,[local])` costs 4 alloc / 8241 B for a 4102-byte image (the plain path is down to 2 / 4102). An in-place `finish_local_in_place(hash)` / `finish_with_prefix(byte)` — write the 5-byte `[LOCAL_EXT][hash:4]` prologue into the writer the body was built in — would take it to ~2 alloc / 4134 B. Pinned today by `tests/binconv_alloc.rs::the_local_option_still_costs_a_second_image_buffer_in_beam_codec`, which must be lowered when the codec change lands.
- **`beam_common`/`beam_codec` — the ERTS monotonic-time interface constants are now duplicated.** `MONOTONIC_TIME_UNIT`/`MIN_SMALL`/`START_TIME`/`END_TIME` live in **both** `./src/time.rs` (needed here: `monotonic_time/0,1` reads the epoch) and `../beam_rt/src/info/time.rs` (`system_info(start_time|end_time)`); `beam_bifs` cannot depend on `beam_rt`, so the set is mirrored deliberately. Lifting it into `beam_common` (the LCA) is the fix owed by `../beam_common`.
