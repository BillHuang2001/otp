# `beam_rt` — M1 gap ledger, GC / heap model
The GC / heap-model half of the gap ledger in [`M1_GAPS.md`](M1_GAPS.md): the copying/immix collector's collection points and valves, the heap switches, the memory/statistics answers, the BIF-result copy-out, the scratch-heap lifetime class, the off-heap refc-binary reservoir, and what a *moving* engine heap would invalidate.
Every entry is a typed capability gap, a measured own-state difference or a documented hazard — never a wrong result.
## Memory and statistics
`erlang:statistics/1`'s container is re-homed onto the process's own **scanned** young heap on a process route like every other registered `Ok` container (`call_registered`'s `move_container = self.mailbox_table.is_some() || safe_to_rewind`), and is inert under the valve there.
Only the pool **rewind** stays `safe_to_rewind`-gated, so a non-rewindable call's tuple keeps its words on the unscanned pool — inert too unless it forwards a *scanned* word.
`DEFAULT_HEAP_WORDS` never causes a zero collection count: it sizes the runtime's **term-construction** heaps (BIF pool + `-eval` heap, `term_heap_words`), while a process nursery comes from `heap.policy()`'s `initial_heap_words`, so a fresh process's first `heap_size` already is the policy figure (*Heap switches*).
`erlang:memory/0,1` (`MemoryHook` seam) is adopted — nine oracle keys in oracle order with `total = processes + system`, every figure measured at construction; `memory/1` answers in the requested order, `badarg` for an unknown key.
`erlang:statistics/1` is partly served (`run_queue*`/`total_run_queue_lengths*`, `wall_clock`, `reductions`/`exact_reductions`, `context_switches`, `io`, and `garbage_collection` = `{GCs, WordsReclaimed, 0}` from the node-global cumulative counters), while `scheduler_wall_time(_all)` ⇒ `undefined` (no per-scheduler wall time is sampled, so the tuple list is never fabricated).
Gaps: `statistics/0`, the per-scheduler key family (`active_tasks*`/`total_active_tasks*`/`microstate_accounting`) and every other `RuntimeHooks` family; the nine-key construction and each served `memory`/`statistics` key: [`src/context/IMPLEMENTATION.md`](src/context/IMPLEMENTATION.md).

* **A tail loop of registered-BIF calls leaves the reported heap capacity flat.** `total_heap_size` reads **466 words** (`heap_size` 233) after 300 000 `integer_to_list/1` calls and the same 466 after 600 000, against 233/233 at N=1 — a copy-out result is re-homed onto the process heap and the pooled BIF region is rewound, so nothing is retained per call.
  The chain-sum machinery those figures come from is still what `process_info(P, total_heap_size)`, `erlang:memory(processes)` and `MaxHeapUsage::heap_words` (what `max_heap_size` compares, `beam_process/src/max_heap.rs`) report: `Nursery::grow`/`install` **retire** the space they replace and only `collapse` frees it (`beam_gc/src/nursery.rs`), so a growth that no collection follows lengthens the chain — the state `Process::collection_debt` names, settled by the next collecting door.
  That chain is **not** what an in-process `compile:file/2` retains on either route: the copy-out seam it needs is guarded by `mailbox_table.as_deref_mut()?` (`calls.rs:1490`) on the table-less `-eval` route, and on the `run <file>` route the compile's own work happens in nested runs whose context also carries `mailbox_table: None`/`gc_armed: false` (`src/context/bridges.rs`) — after a completed `cerl_clauses` compile the process reads `total_heap_size` **12 788** / `heap_size` **6 016** words (`run`) and **233** words (`-eval`).
  The compile's host memory is not a count of `heap_words`-sized reservations either: the `bif_heaps` pool is **depth-sized** (128 MiB for the outermost call, 1 MiB per nested level, self-tuned to `2 ×` an observed peak), so an in-process `cerl_clauses` compile ends at **6** regions / 133 MB of reservation against **5 397** words ever written (`BEAM_RT_MEM_TRACE=1`).
  Reservation is not RSS: a region is a lazy `mmap`, so that 133 MB is invisible in RSS and `erlang:memory(total)` stays blind to it (~5.8 MB after the compile) because those regions are `AllocType::Standard`.
  What does move RSS is written pages, measured on the `run` route with the tree's OTP-30 bootstrap compiler first on `-pa`: peak `VmHWM` **424 MB** (`cerl_clauses`, in-process) / **307 MB** (`orddict`), against **121 MB** for a bare boot.
  `inet_parse` (37 KB) completes only with the compiler **inside** the calling process (`no_spawn_compiler_process`): **7.81–8.22 GB** peak / ~17 s on the `-eval` route under the tree OTP-30 bootstrap compiler (`5 203–5 260 MB` / 12.1–12.6 s under the system OTP-29 one), while the default form (a spawned compiler child) outlives the 900 s blocking deadline; the `run` route never finishes it (`RUN_DEADLINE`, 60 s, exit 1 — 222 400 kB peak with the compiler in-process, 670 720 kB with the spawned child, 219 680 kB under the tree compiler).
  `dbg_iload` does not complete at all and reaches **813 MB** at the 25 s cap.
  Two mechanisms carry the multi-GB figures and both are still open: the `-eval` store's **retired chain** (4.63 GB, 59 % of the peak — fully written and resident) and the `beam_engine`-owned **arena** (2.85 GB of written pages over 16.18 GB reserved, 37 %) — the measured per-mapping split, both mechanisms' bound rule and the missing APIs are [`src/context/CONTEXT.md`](src/context/CONTEXT.md) *The bound …* + *Reclaim …*.
  The full attribution, the per-route table and the sound-vs-unsound reclaim list are [`src/context/CONTEXT.md`](src/context/CONTEXT.md) *Host-memory retention …* + *What is still unbounded …* + *Reclaim …*.

## GC / heap model
The unscanned-root descent is reached per heap-admission through the baseline JIT's `jit_test_heap`; `src/context/gc_walk.rs`'s per-walk visited table is near-optimal (1.25–1.45 probes/insert, ~5 grows over a whole `pool.erl` compile), so the remaining cost is its **invocation count** — plus the caller's per-edge `root_is_unscanned`/`for_each_child_slot` cost — which is `src/context/gc.rs` / `beam_engine` policy; the per-walk reset cannot become a cross-walk memo (heap addresses are reused after a collection) and the per-walk **allocation** is gone (`WalkScratch`). The per-pid latch is **closed only by the three whole-`RootSet` doors** on `ValveVerdict::foreign_free` — never by the park arm or a signal gate, which run for a process whose `x`/stack are unpublished (`gc_walk.rs`'s module docs).
`DEFAULT_HEAP_WORDS` is `1 << 24` (16 Mi words of lazily-mapped address space); term-construction heaps (BIF-heap pool + `-eval` heap) are bump arenas sized `config.heap_words`, `+hmax N` caps that capacity (`runtime.rs::term_heap_words`), and the `system_limit` translation runs after the copy engine's retry (`deliver_message` keys its heap-fragment fallback on a raw `AllocKind::Message`).
This crate does drive `beam_gc`: a *scheduled* process allocates on its own young heap and is collected at `ExecutionContext::ensure_heap`/`ensure_heap_full` (both share `collect_or_grow`; the latter is the full-root door `test_heap` reaches, armed by the slice's `BudgetContext`), pinned by `tests/gc_collect.rs` and reported by `Runtime::gc_stats()`; every other context no-ops on the unbounded arena.
Measured limit: a workload allocating only through `ExecutionContext::alloc_words` (no `test_heap` admission point, as real Cowboy `cowboy_bstr`-style code does) is not collected by *that* door — it grows the nursery and retries the bump (arena only when growth cannot reach it), and `Process::collection_debt` becomes a real collection at the *next* admission point — so on the **default** (native baseline) tier, which never pre-empts it, it reaches the term heap's hard limit with no collection at all (`collections: 0`; `heap_size` 3 074 841 / 29 485 327 words at N = 2 000 / 20 000).
The **reduction/yield-boundary door** (`src/context/gc/reds_door.rs`, from the runner's `Yield` arm) is the site that collects such a loop: it fires once per `REDS_BOUNDARY_MIN_REDS` (one `CONTEXT_REDS` slice) reductions over a nursery **seven eighths** occupied (the C's `ERTS_VBUMP_ALLOC_REDS` "allocation area exhausted" rule), so on the interpreter tier the no-admission shape collects once per pre-emption at the measured **17.8–18.0 reds/iteration** with `heap_size` converged at `young_target_words(401)` = **987** words (flat in N, one young space) instead of climbing; on the default (native-baseline) tier `etf_roundtrip`/`list_walk`/`slice:fib/1` complete inside one slice or never pre-empt at all, so no boundary is ever offered and the door contributes no collection to them.
Its cost on every other hot shape (per-call, ON vs OFF from one build) is [`src/runner/MEASURED.md`](src/runner/MEASURED.md) *The reduction/yield-boundary door's rationing on the hot shapes*; its pins (`src/runner/tests/{reds_door,boundary_cost}.rs`) are bands re-derived from measurement — a per-iteration reduction price of 16.997–17.004 reds (pinned inside 16..=18), `collections` (8, 84, 849) with `minor_gcs` (7, 81, 827) at N = 2 000 / 20 000 / 200 000, converged `heap_size` 2 586, `checks_per_call` 0.1949..0.1952 and a door rate of `checks/12..=checks/8` (390 checks / 44 door collections at N = 2 000, 1 952 / 196 at N = 10 000).
The door collects with `Process::collect_kind(CollectionKind::Minor)` (`src/context/gc/reds_door.rs`) and escalates to the escalating `Process::collect()` only when that minor reports typed heap exhaustion — `beam_gc::Collector::collect_minor` requires `mature.available_words() >= minor_room_words(roots)` up front and copies nothing otherwise, the room input being the **live** promotion (the nursery fill when it already fits, else `young_promotion_words`) — so escalate-only-on-cannot-fit is the rule and a cadence fullsweep no longer comes from the door ([`HANDOFF.md`](HANDOFF.md)).
Its per-call rates are the measured 3-row `MEASURED` table in `src/runner/tests/boundary_cost.rs`: 2.6522 / 2.6861 / 2.7109 collections and 0.1654 / 0.1632 / 0.1624 majors per call at N = 2 000/4 000/10 000 — flat in N, never a closed form.
A live float register is never left unrooted (the full-root doors publish the machine's live leading FR prefix, `Machine::fr_mark()`, so it lies inside the x file's pointer-bearing bound); a failed collection halts that process's collection, never the program.
**Retained-space caution:** `Process::grow_nursery` retires the space it replaces and only a real collection collapses that chain (`should_collect` ignores it), so a *genuinely* present foreign root **with** children keeps a process's capacity growing with its admission-point count (a `beam_process` policy matter — [`CONTEXT.md`](CONTEXT.md) *Known Issues*, the retired chain and its collapse rule); a container that forwards **no scanned word** is inert under the valve's two-fact rule (*GC-relocation hazards*), so a reference-minting loop runs at a flat `memory(processes)` of 6.91 MB across N = 20 000 / 200 000 / 1 000 000, against the oracle's 13.7 MB (`context/gc/refs_plateau.rs`).
Term-heap capacity stays the hard limit for anything not collected: both exhaustion classes become `error::system_limit` via `error::enforce_term_heap_limit` — `AllocKind::Heap` (the per-process bump allocator) and `AllocKind::Message` (the copy engine's, classified by `beam_message::is_message_heap_exhaustion`) → CLI `beam_rt: system_limit`, exit 1.
On-demand growth is adopted at the admission point and on the `beam_process` delivery path: `ensure_heap`/`ensure_heap_full` run `Process::ensure_capacity` when the policy can reach it (`HeapPolicy::next_words(need, 0) >= need`), plain `Process::collect` otherwise, so a `test_heap` request larger than the nursery is admitted by growing it in place (`tests/heap_growth.rs`) while a bounded `+hmax N` request it cannot reach is refused *before* collecting (the allocation falls back to the arena); `Process::deliver` grows the nursery the same way for a refused message copy, and a relocation-exempt receiver skips the retry entirely — a `receive`-parked receiver whose slice ended with a dead FR prefix is **not** exempt, because its park published its whole root set (`Registers::extra_roots` included, [`DESIGN_DECISIONS.md`](DESIGN_DECISIONS.md) *A park that skips root publication holds the relocation exemption*), so a delivery to it collects/grows and copies straight in.
Allocation outside an admission point uses bounded `beam_alloc::Heap` arenas whose regions never move while anything is executing (`BifContext::heap()`, `Runtime::eval_heap` — the store's words are relocated only by the region pass, at a site where nothing is running and with every root rewritten), so a BIF's *intermediate* terms are invisible to `roots`; a call overflowing such a heap raises `error::system_limit`. The one growth seam outside an admission point is the pooled BIF-heap frontier on the table-less (`-eval`/nested) route — `RuntimeContext::take_bif_frontier` retires a near-full region and continues in a larger one (capped only by an explicit `+hmax`) ([`src/context/IMPLEMENTATION.md`](src/context/IMPLEMENTATION.md) *The pooled BIF-heap frontier*).
The runtime drives `beam_gc` at **four** collection points, three of them heap-admission doors: `ensure_heap`/`ensure_heap_full`/`alloc_words_rooted` (`src/context/gc.rs`), reached only where *code* allocates (`test_heap`/`allocate_heap`), plus `src/context/gc/reds_door.rs::collect_at_reds_boundary` from the runner's `Yield` arm, which needs no request. A float-family module is collected over **only** at the two full-root doors — the narrow `ensure_heap` door hands over just an x prefix and the stack — a failed collection halts that process's collection rather than failing the program, the term-heap capacity is the hard limit for anything not collected, and on-demand growth is adopted at both doors and on the message-delivery path.
The **boundary door**'s gates are a reduction interval (`REDS_BOUNDARY_MIN_REDS`, at most one collection per pre-emption) and a **7/8** young-space occupancy watermark (the C's `ERTS_VBUMP_ALLOC_REDS` rule), and it publishes the machine's **whole** `RootSet` (`ALL_LIVE`), never the pointer prefix; it collects **young-only first** (`Process::collect_kind(CollectionKind::Minor)`) and escalates to the growing `Process::collect()` only on that minor's typed heap exhaustion, so cadence fullsweeps never come from the door; the native baseline driver charges **one** reduction each time control returns to its block loop (`set_current_code` then `reduce(1)` before the native `enter`), so a slice ends at a real **block exit** while an in-block BIF call and a linked tail `jmp` charge nothing — the fairness gap a GC one shares. Every fire count and per-N rate is a measured table, never a closed form: `src/runner/tests/{reds_door,boundary_cost,slice_charging}.rs` (*Scheduler / runner*, [`M1_GAPS.md`](M1_GAPS.md)).
The **unscanned-root valve**'s `context/gc.rs::root_is_unscanned` is a **two-fact** predicate — the word lies in an **unscanned** region (`word_region` → `WordRegion::{Scanned,Foreign}`) **and** it forwards at least one **scanned** pointer (`forwards_scanned_word` over `beam_term::layout::for_each_child_slot`) — so a foreign leaf or all-immediate block (a `make_ref/0` block, a ref/pid/port block, a float, a bitstring, the empty tuple, a tuple of immediates) is **inert**, as is a **null or unaligned** word (`dereferenceable_pointer`). The per-pid latch (`GcState::foreign`) is cleared only from the doors that hand the walk the machine's **whole `RootSet`** (`ensure_heap_full`, `alloc_words_rooted`, `reds_door`) and only on `ValveVerdict::foreign_free`; the narrow `ensure_heap`, the park arm, the off-heap prune and the `RuntimeCollectionGate` may **open** it but never clear it. Consequence: a loop holding one registered-BIF result container live still collects, because the copy-out puts the held container on the process's own **scanned** young heap (`tests/foreign_root_loop.rs`, whose law is the floor `(N + 4)/13` words per iteration).
**The `-eval` store's statement window** (`src/eval/scratch.rs`) bounds the store's own growth: the words a discarded top-level statement copied are rewound at the boundary **before** the next statement once the window reaches `MIN_WINDOW_WORDS` (1 024 words = 8 KiB) and a reachability descent from `Runtime::gather_off_heap_roots` plus the evaluator's `Env` finds no live word inside it (a `WindowProbe` sink compares *addresses*, so it dereferences nothing).
A **decline** keeps every word, one statement's worth each: a descent that cannot complete, a region replaced inside the window, a mark above the frontier, a reached word, a refused rewind, and any window a writer **pinned** — the runtime-lifetime writers (`Runtime::stabilize_term`/`stabilize_term_with`, `src/port/completion.rs::decode_caller_tag`), `apply_eval_closure`, and the two nested-run sites in `src/context/pump.rs`, which latch through `Runtime::pin_window_if_store_written` only when the run actually wrote on the store (a pinned window advances its mark, so it is a bounded leak of one statement, never a dangle).
Residuals: a single giant statement is inherently retained (its value *is* the result, and neither non-running site has a boundary inside it, so the peak it reaches stands), and the `-eval` action process is never enqueued — it has no scheduler pass, so no runner-outcome family can hold a word across a boundary there.
Figures and the OFF control: [`PERF.md`](PERF.md) *The `-eval` statement window*; the oracle cross-check of the three enumerated holder families: `tests/eval_region.rs`.
### The BIF-result copy-out and `ets_copy`'s peak RSS
`examples/perf_copy_out.rs` splits `binary_to_term/1`'s **508 ns** on the published `etf_roundtrip` payload as prologue **82** + `beam_codec` decode **218** + **copy-out 149** + residual **59** (`term_to_binary/1` is 252; the copier alone 142 ≈ **2.1 ns per copied word**) — a *second* full traversal the C runtime never pays, because its BIFs allocate on the process heap via `HAlloc`. Eliding it means handing the pooled region over as a **scanned nursery space** (`Nursery`'s space/retired chain and `collapse`, `HeapSpace`/carrier ownership, `Runtime::{take,put}_bif_heap`) — **owner `beam_gc` + `beam_process`**, a cross-crate handoff.
`RuntimeContext::call_registered` (`src/context/calls.rs`) moves an `Ok` container result **unconditionally on a process route** (`move_container = self.mailbox_table.is_some() || safe_to_rewind`) onto that process's own **scanned** young heap (`move_result_into_process_heap`, landing the copy in `x(0)`): the pooled 16 MiB `Runtime::bif_heaps` (`Target::Foreign`) is unscanned and a later call's rewind recycles it, so a heap object storing such a result (`compile:debug_info_chunk/1` holding `lists:filter/2`'s output in a tuple) would dangle.
A reference-holding graph is never moved and never rewound (`graph_holds_reference`): a reference's identity is its address, so a copy would no longer be the timer `erlang:cancel_timer/1,2` names — `badarg`, hence a `kernel_sup` crash. The walk that decides it is hash-free and allocation-free (an inline worklist, no visited set, a visit budget), ≈1.0 µs cheaper per container-returning BIF than the `HashSet` walk it replaced — [`../PERF_DISPATCH.md`](PERF_DISPATCH.md) *WALK-CUT*.
The pool **rewind** to the call's pre-offset is the gated half — `safe_to_rewind` (no escaping `DispatchHooks` arm, no reentrant fun-applying call) **and** a succeeded move — so an `Err` result (a raise's live reason term points into this heap), a non-rewindable call and a failed re-home never rewind, the last because its words are still handed out.
The **table-less (`-eval`) route keeps the move gated** on `safe_to_rewind`, into the runtime-lifetime store: no collector exists there and a non-rewindable call never rewinds, so an unconditional move would only duplicate the result into an arena a long in-process compile exhausts.
The rule is load-bearing, not defensive: a container result left on the recyclable pool while Erlang code stores it in a heap object becomes a dangling boxed argument at the next rewind (the `invalid boxed header subtag`/SIGSEGV class), which is the shape `selfhost/in_process_compile_file_no_spawn_deterministic` exercises.
Residuals: a NIF result is not copied out (deliberate M1); an escaping `DispatchHooks` arm and an error result leave their container on the pool; the `-eval` store only grows; a nested run (`RuntimeContext::run_export`) never collects.
The copy-out cannot collect (a BIF call leaves the machine's x registers / Erlang stack unpublished), so the nursery is grown through the no-relocation `Process::grow_nursery` seam — **predictively** from the learned `RuntimeContext::copy_out_hint` (the copier's own reported shortfall, spent through `RuntimeContext::hint_grow_gate` against a margin whenever the young space's available words fall below it) whenever the young space cannot hold it, and on a refusal for the first copy of each context or a graph bigger than the hint — and the retired young space is retained; hence `copy_bound/ets_copy`'s peak RSS growing linearly in N on the **default** tier, where neither `alloc_words_rooted` nor the boundary door collects (the interpreter tier's does). Inside a **relocation-exempt** window (a nested run, whose machine roots are unpublished by construction) the growth itself is declined — a young-space replacement needs a complete root set — so that copy-out is **stabilized** onto the never-reset store instead: refuse ⇒ stabilize, never dangle. The measured mechanism, the figures and the refuted leads (the ETS adopt path, the caller-module float guard) are [`../PERF_DISPATCH.md`](PERF_DISPATCH.md) *`ets_copy` peak RSS*.
A `hint == 0` reproduces the refusal path exactly, and that path stays behind it (a fresh empty destination table per attempt, the permanent-non-exhaustion short-circuit, `safe_to_rewind`, and the `max_heap_words` ceiling's bare-shortfall fallback).
The hint's residual: it is per `RuntimeContext`, so the first copy-out of each context still learns by refusing — **754 refusals per 250 000 ops ≈ 13 ns/op (0.4 %)**, down from 83 067 (0.332/op) to 0.003/op on the paired A/B; removing it needs a `Runtime`-owned pid→hint map with cleanup on process death. What it bought: `copy_bound/ets_copy` **0.605×** on a paired A/B — [`PERF_HISTORY.md`](PERF_HISTORY.md) *The copy-out's learned size hint*.
Closing any of it needs a `beam_process` seam that can shrink/reuse retired young space or a `beam_engine`/runner seam that publishes the driving machine's roots at a BIF call so the copy-out can collect — sibling-crate changes, reported not worked around; the readable-word guard, the `grow_nursery` hint and the six `src/context/tests.rs` pins: [`src/context/IMPLEMENTATION.md`](src/context/IMPLEMENTATION.md) *Per-call cost of a registered call* / *The table-less arm and its store*.
### The scratch-heap lifetime and the dangling-boxed-argument class
`erlang:system_info/1` hands its value **off** the reset-per-call scratch region: `Runtime::system_info`/`format_with` rewind it through `Runtime::reset_info_scratch`, which refuses to recycle a region a live term may occupy (`Runtime::info_heap_tainted`, a one-way taint valve latched when a relocation is refused), and the one value handed to Erlang code is relocated by `RuntimeContext::relocate_off_scratch` through the copy-out machinery above — onto the running process's scanned young heap, or the never-reset store on the table-less `-eval` route. A retained `system_info/1` value is therefore sound, pinned oracle-cross-checked by `tests/system_info_retained.rs` (the `epp:predef_macros/2` shape: `A = system_info(version), system_info(allocated_areas), system_info(machine)` answers `"17.1"`, and the `otp_release` form runs instead of dying).
The class it closes — `beam_term::ops::class`'s `invalid boxed header subtag` abort (`../beam_term/src/ops.rs:194`, reached through `compare::cmp_impl`) — is a **dangling boxed argument**, never a `beam_term` classifier gap: the word read at `*boxed_val(x)` is a non-literal cons-cell pointer, and no header constructor can produce one (`_make_header` = `(size<<6)+subtag`, every subtag `…00`; ERTS reserves only `0xF`, `../../erts/emulator/beam/erl_term.h:149,308`).
Two rules close it, both load-bearing: **a live heap serves its own builds** — `alloc_words_rooted`'s unarmed arm (every nested run) serves the running process's **scanned** young heap, because `beam_gc`'s copier leaves a `Target::Foreign` container verbatim *without descending*, so a container built in the unscanned arena keeps a stale child word once a collection relocates its young-heap children — and **a young-space replacement needs a complete root set** (`src/context/gc.rs::may_replace_young_space`, the single predicate every growth site consults).
Pins: `src/context/gc.rs`'s unarmed-arm case and `src/context/calls/exempt_window_tests.rs` (node level), `tests/nested_run_allocation_heap.rs` (the profile-independent structural pin: a nested run's container is young-resident, survives a growth and relocates with its children intact) and `tests/path_length_independence.rs` (the real in-process `compile:file/2` route at 18 staging path lengths — the *manifestation* is address-layout dependent, so the length sweep, not a single invocation, is what pins it).
The mechanism in prose and the falsified suspects: [`src/context/CONTEXT.md`](src/context/CONTEXT.md) *A term that outlives its heap*.
The differential sweep has **no per-case timeout** (`../tools/differential/src/runtime.rs:661-712`; `Observation.exit_code: None` = signal, `compare.rs:280-284,391-395`), so a `signal` verdict is still a real abnormal child exit and must be investigated as a fresh lifetime defect rather than dismissed as flake.
### The engine's off-heap refc-binary reservoir and the escaped-payload store
`Machine::refc_binaries` holds the payloads a process's compiled bitstring code built (they live outside every heap the collector owns), and the runner's `Yield` arm reclaims every payload its just-published roots no longer reach once the process's **binary virtual heap** triggers — the C's `off_heap.overhead > bin_vheap_sz`, re-sized after a prune by `next_vheap_size` — through `src/context/gc/bin_vheap.rs` (`reclaim` + the per-process `OffHeapReclaimer`, held on the engine as `ProcessEngine::off_heap`).
The release moves nothing (a retained payload keeps its `data_ptr`), and the **prune-only** valve `gc_walk.rs::reaches_an_unscanned_word` is strictly stronger than the collection one, because a `BinRef.val` names an off-heap payload no relocation moves.
A reachable **reference** does not trip that valve: an internal reference (M1's `[header,node,serial,id]` and the C's `[header,w1,w2]`) and the external form are childless leaves whose payload words are packed `u32`s or a bare id — never a term reference, never a `BinRef.val` — so one live monitor ref, timer ref or unnamed-ETS tid in a process's root set does not keep its dead payloads alive (`gc_walk.rs::is_inert_reference`).
Every other gate is a **decline** — the residual is a **leak, never a dangle**: the `Wait` arm runs no prune at all, a declined prune keeps every payload it was asked about, and a parked process's reservoir is therefore released at its next pre-emption or with its machine — which a dead process's engine loses at the scheduler pass boundary, so no reservoir outlives the process that built it ([`src/runner/CONTEXT.md`](src/runner/CONTEXT.md) *Known Issues / Handoffs*).
The binary-vheap budget **gates** a prune and never *causes* one: `OffHeapReclaimer::triggered()` is consulted by the prune and nothing reads it to ask for a collection, so one slice's dead off-heap garbage is bounded by the **slice length**, never by the configured budget.
The cadence is therefore the **pre-emption** cadence, and that is the loop's bounding invariant — one pre-emption window: the call site is the runner's `Yield` arm (`src/runner/run.rs:1443`, whose gate `parked_published` is `!is_relocation_exempt()`), and the prune there releases the **whole** reservoir (every dead handle, `AllocType::Binary` live 0 afterwards, `retained_by_shared` 0) — verified across 20 payload-building shapes over ≥100 parks each (a plain `<<0:N/8>>` build, `binary:copy/2`, `base64:encode/1`, `list_to_binary/1`, a sub-binary, a `bs_init_writable` accumulator, ETS- and dict-held payloads, reference/monitor/tid-holding roots, the fun-loop and a dense sampler).
`erlang:memory(binary)` sampled at a **fixed iteration offset** is a slice **phase**, not a leak: with a ~999-iteration park interval a read every 1 000 iterations answers 1, 2, 3, … payloads for ever and resets at rung 999 — the same total work sampled at a different offset shifts the whole column.
The phase-immune reading is a **dense** sample (a read after *every* iteration, folded to `{min, max, trough}`): at 1 024-byte payloads over N = 20 000 and 45 parks, `min` 0, trough **1 072** (one payload) and peak **475 968** (444 payloads = one pre-emption window), element-for-element identical for the shapes holding a live reference.
That window is a count of iterations, so the envelope's **bytes are payload-size proportional**: the same fixture peaks at 475 968 / 930 624 / 1 839 936 / 29 119 296 for 1 024/2 048/4 096/65 536-byte payloads (≈444 payloads each), where the oracle on the identical source stays at 20 176 / 23 176 / 33 504 / 213 776 — its trigger *causes* a collection, this runtime's only gates a prune, so the oracle's envelope is its bin-vheap budget and this one's is a slice. Flat in N at every size (a capacity residual, not a leak); the repair is an end-the-slice-on-the-trigger seam in `runner`/`beam_engine`.
`tests/off_heap_reclaim.rs` asserts exactly those two terms and derives both from them.
**A slice that never pre-empts reaches no prune at all**, and that is the one unbounded shape: a self-recursive tail call with a BIF-free body — the measured shape is `u1(N, B, K) -> _ = <<0:(B*8)>>, u1(N-1, B, K)` — is linked into ONE natively executed block chain, so it is priced ~once and reaches no refusing seam, the native (default) tier charging a reduction on a non-tail call only (a tail BIF dispatch or `call_ext` only *spends*).
The process's off-heap refc-binary reservoir therefore accumulates unpruned, because the host's prune is **park-gated** and this process never parks: measured `erlang:memory(binary)` = exactly `N × (payload + 48)` — **21 440 000** bytes at N = 20 000 with 1 KiB payloads and **0 parks** (`{min 1 072, max 21 440 000, trough -1, parks 0}`), 82 880 000 at 4 KiB, the same `N × (payload + 48)`.
Both figures are this tree's, at the default native tier with `BEAM_RT_TIER` unset, on a shared host: the counters are what is meaningful, never the wall time.
`BEAM_RT_TIER=interpreter` bounds it: every instruction is charged there, so the process really pre-empts and its reading falls instead of growing (the exact park count and peak are the fixture's own, not a documented invariant).
The fix seam is a reduction charge on the linked tail-exit path in [`../beam_engine`](../beam_engine) — `jit/baseline/runtime.rs`'s tail exit with `emit/link.rs`'s and `emit/block.rs`'s emitted jump arms — a sibling crate, hence recorded here and not fixed here.
The envelope is one **slice**, so its bytes are payload-size proportional — a capacity residual, not a leak: what one slice builds, one prune releases at the process's next pre-emption, or its machine's drop takes it.
A `receive`/`Wait`-parked process legitimately does not prune (its roots are unpublished), so its reservoir is retained while it is parked — by design, bounded by what one slice built, and released with the machine its process's death drops at the scheduler pass boundary ([`src/runner/CONTEXT.md`](src/runner/CONTEXT.md) *Known Issues / Handoffs*).
A **fun-loop** body reaches the reservoir through the in-machine `lists:foldl/3` path (`prefers_module_code_for_fun_apply`), and its `erlang:memory(binary)` reading is the same slice phase: N = 1 000 answers every payload the run built (`4 144 000` at 4 KiB) because the run ends before its first pre-emption, and N = 2 000/3 000/4 000 answer `N − last park` (measured 1 995 → 0 payloads, 1 996 → 1, 1 997 → 2, 3 000 → ~1 005).
A fun applied from the **`-eval`** route takes the registered `lists:foldl/3` fast path into a nested run instead, whose payloads the nested epilogue adopts into the escaped-payload store: that envelope is a converged capacity of one `STORE_BUDGET_WORDS` worth — `377 104` bytes at 4 096-byte payloads (91 payloads) and `389 136` at 1 024-byte ones — flat in N from 500 to 5 000.
The A/B is the contract: with the prune's call site disabled the same fixture answers exactly `N × (4096 + 48)` (`4 144 000` at N = 1 000 → `41 440 000` at N = 10 000, the 10× step adding 37 296 000 bytes), and `retain(6000, 4096, 65536)` is the anti-dangle half — a 64 KiB payload surviving ~6 000 prunes **byte-exact** (md5, `=:=` a fresh rebuild, both ends) while the churn around it is released.
Mechanism, valves and residuals: [`src/context/CONTEXT.md`](src/context/CONTEXT.md) *The off-heap refc-binary reclamation*; the call site and its per-slice cost: [`src/runner/CONTEXT.md`](src/runner/CONTEXT.md) *The off-heap refc-binary reclamation at the slice boundary*. Its end-to-end effect on a real workload — `emqx_base62:encode/1` on the unmodified EMQX module, `PRE-FIX` vs `POST-FIX` vs the oracle — is [`src/runner/MEASURED.md`](src/runner/MEASURED.md) *The off-heap reclamation end to end*.
`erlang:memory(binary)` is `binary_alloc`'s live bytes, so it reflects a release; `erlang:process_info(P, binary)` is the per-process roster assembled in `src/native/memory/mod.rs` and rendered by `src/native/memory/binary.rs` as the deduped union of the target's engine reservoir (`RunnerState::reservoir_at` for another pid, the in-machine BIF door for the executing one) and the PCB's own off-heap table (empty in production), in the oracle's `{Ptr, Size, Refc}` tuple shape (`[]` when it holds none).
The runtime's **escaped-payload store** (`Runtime::eval_off_heap`) is PRUNED at two points (`src/eval/reclaim.rs`): the `-eval` driver's depth-0 statement boundaries (`Evaluator::run_top_level` → `prune_escaped`) and the nested-run epilogue (`src/context/pump.rs::adopt_nested_payloads` → `Runtime::prune_off_heap`) that adopts a finished nested `Machine`'s handles.
Because no eval root lies in a scanned region, the prune is its own region-agnostic reachability walk over an explicitly published root list; term regions are handed over as **word images** and only pattern-scanned for `HEADER_BIN_REF`, because a region interleaves heap objects with the raw payload bytes of on-heap binaries (pushing its words as roots dereferences `0x0101_0101_0101_0100`).
Refusal is **doubt ⇒ decline** (an exhausted `WALK_VISIT_BUDGET`, an unknown boxed header) plus a `refcount() > 1` belt; the trigger is O(1) — the handle count against the last observation, with the verdict (not only the count) cached — which is what makes the per-lambda-application cost one integer compare.
The nested point prunes only the handles **its own run adopted**: sweeping the whole store there corrupts live data (measured: `lists:map/2` over 200 engine-built 40 000-byte binaries returns 190 corrupted elements, 0 when scoped), because a payload an earlier run built can already stand on an enclosing suspended machine's stack — so such a payload is retained for the store's lifetime, a bounded leak.
The store is not pid-attributable, so no per-pid `Process::set_off_heap_binary_bytes` figure is published from it.

* **A nested fun application's nursery chain is bounded by the debt gate.** A host BIF that applies a fun argument (`lists:foldl/3`, `maps:map/2`, …) enters the runtime through `FunBridge::nested` → `apply_fun` → a **second machine** whose context is `gc_armed == false` while the pid is marked relocation-exempt (`src/context/runtime_context.rs` `with_relocation_exempt`, entered at `src/context/pump.rs` `run_export_blocking`/`run_label_blocking` and `src/context/execution.rs`'s `apply_fun`), so no collapsing collection door can fire for the duration while that run's registered-BIF copy-outs keep growing the young heap through the **no-collect** `Process::grow_nursery` seam.
  `src/context/execution.rs`'s `resolve_external_call` therefore declines the `lists`/`maps` fun-applying keys while the calling process carries collection debt (`nests_a_fun_apply` ∧ `calling_process_has_collection_debt`, the roster being `beam_bifs::needs_fun_caller`), so the call is entered **in-machine on the caller's own armed machine** and the debt door collapses the chain as it grows.
  The two `ets` folds deliberately stay on the synchronous path (their bodies reach a gated `lists` key internally), and a table-less (`-eval`) context reads no debt, so the gate is inert there.
  Measured with `BEAM_RT_GC_TRACE=1` over a 25 s `dbg_iload` window on the `run` route: `chain_spaces` peaks at **6** with **17 121 896** chain-summed used words and peak `VmHWM` **813 MB** (system OTP-29 compiler), or **10** / **37 189 421** words and **690 MB** (tree OTP-30 bootstrap compiler).
  What the gate does **not** cover: a nested run entered while the caller owes no debt, a key outside the `lists`/`maps` roster, and a fun applied from a context with no process table.

* **The two long-lived term regions are reclaimed wherever nothing is executing; a single in-flight statement still pays the full peak.** `src/runtime/reclaim.rs` runs **one** reachability descent at two non-running sites (the `-eval` statement boundary and the driver exit) and does three things with it: rewind the arena above its mark (`MinimalContext::heap_rewind`, Tier A), drop a whole-dead `eval_heap_retired` region (its `Heap::drop` frees the carrier), and evacuate a partially live one into one fresh region through `beam_gc`'s `Relocator` behind a **post-move verification** that keeps every source on a hit. Measured on a single `inet_parse` compile: the retired chain **19 → 1** regions (603 193 147 words reclaimed) and 11 arena regions recovered; on a **two**-compile `-eval` the peak falls **3.07 GB (−19.0 %)** (16 175 512 → 13 109 804 kB), and that win is the arena rewind while the store chain stays additive there (`store_compactions=0`, the move refused by the verification).
  What is **not** bounded is the peak *inside* one in-process `compile:file/2` (8 197 644 kB for `inet_parse`, unchanged within 0.03 %): the store still retains the copy-out volume since the last reclaimable boundary — region-capped at `MAX_EVAL_HEAP_WORDS` (256 MiB) but not chain-capped, because a retired region is full and never rewritable — and `arena_words` reads **1 328 036 633** (10.6 GB of cumulative demand over 7 045 408 calls) on that run. Peak RSS is therefore ≈ copy-out volume + arena written pages + O(1) — 4.63 + 2.85 + 0.23 GB for `inet_parse`.
  The binding holder is the **in-flight machine stack together with the evaluator's Rust-frame root set**: `Runtime::in_machine` is a single slot (`runtime.rs:652`), a parent machine's `&mut` is a Rust local of `src/context/pump.rs`, and the driver's `Env` is a `HashMap` in another frame — no `&mut` reachability exists for either at any of the three Phase-2 points, so `with_relocation_exempt` stays. Detail: [`src/eval/CONTEXT.md`](src/eval/CONTEXT.md) *Phase-2 blocker*, [`src/runtime/CONTEXT.md`](src/runtime/CONTEXT.md) *The region-reclaim pass*, [`src/context/CONTEXT.md`](src/context/CONTEXT.md) *Reclaim …*.

* **The `-eval` store's written volume, not the region pass, is what one long statement costs in RSS.** A single `-eval` statement that compiles a module in process writes ~27 MB into `Runtime::eval_heap` (the 128 MiB seed region): `smaps` at the peak shows the store's one mapping (`size=131 080 kB`) holding **26 916–27 988 kB** of written pages, while under the pre-window binary the same mapping never rises above ~2 MB. The writer is the exempt-window stabilize arm of the process-route copy-out (`src/context/calls.rs` → `src/context/pool.rs`, which copies the graph into the store because a relocation-exempt window may not replace the young space), not `src/runtime/reclaim.rs`: the pass's own A/B switch (`BEAM_RT_REGION_RECLAIM=off|arena|nomove|store-whole|store|all`) moves peak RSS by at most **580 kB** on that workload, and its exit counters read `store_compactions=0 store_regions_dropped=0 store_move_declines=0` with `arena_reclaims=2` releasing 285 words. Per-writer detail and the ranked sound options for a future fix: [`src/context/CONTEXT.md`](src/context/CONTEXT.md).

* **The store's written prefix inside one statement is bounded by the statement's copy-out volume, not by program history.** Nothing may rewind it mid-statement — `pin_window_if_store_written` latches the statement window with a transient pin on the first store write and the window's rewind refuses, and a region pass is refused while a machine is in flight — so the two reclaim points are the next top-level statement (`Site::Boundary`) and the driver exit (`Site::Exit`). Growth is by copy-out shortfall (doubling, clamped by `MAX_EVAL_HEAP_WORDS = 1<<25` words per region); a measured single-statement compile writes ~27 MB of a 128 MiB seed and replaces no region (`eval_heap_retired_regions=0`). The figure is therefore a peak that the following boundary gives back, and it does not grow with the number of statements already run.

* **A user process's nursery is live-set sized and flat in N** (not a gap; the rules are `beam_gc`'s `HeapPolicy::young_minor_target_words`/`young_target_words`, one × the round's own `GcStats::words_copied` plus the reserve, and the shrink/keep band around it): `reds_boundary`'s boundary `heap_size` is a flat **987** = `young_target_words(401)` = `next_words(2·401+64)` on both tiers — a fixed point of both the fullsweep and the minor keep rule — the interpreter control reads the **233**-word floor (`young_minor_target_words(0)`), and `churn` holds a flat **2 585**-word nursery (the last growth-table entry below the `YOUNG_SHRINK_FACTOR` ceiling `8 × 376`) and a flat **6 772**-word mature region at every N — [`src/runner/MEASURED.md`](src/runner/MEASURED.md).

* **The runtime drives `beam_gc` at four collection points, three of them heap-admission doors** (`src/context/gc.rs` + `src/context/gc/reds_door.rs`); a failed collection halts that process's collection, never the program — `M1_GAPS_GC.md` *GC / heap model*.
  The **boundary door** is what makes a BIF-only loop collectable, and on the **default (native-baseline) tier it is unreachable** — a fairness defect as much as a GC one, and through it a memory one: a loop that spends no reduction never reaches a park, so the off-heap reservoir also grows `N × (payload + 48)` (see *The engine's off-heap refc-binary reservoir* above).

* **The unscanned-root valve declines a collection only where relocation is genuinely unsafe** — `context/gc.rs::root_is_unscanned` is a **two-fact** predicate, so a foreign leaf, an all-immediate block or a null/unaligned word is inert — `M1_GAPS_GC.md` *GC / heap model*.
  Consequence: a reference-minting loop keeps `erlang:memory(processes)` flat, and a loop holding one registered-BIF result container live still collects.

## Heap switches
`+hms`/`+hmbs` set `beam_gc::HeapPolicy` rounded through the growth table (`+hms 1000`→1598), `+hmax*` the `max_heap_size` map; a malformed value is exit 2 where the oracle exits 1.
`beam_gc::HeapPolicy::initial_heap_words` is the single authority for a new process's initial nursery: both collector constructors install it, so `+hms`/`+hmbs` and `spawn_opt`'s `min_heap_size` size the heap a fresh process really gets, and `process_info(P, heap_size)` reports it (`beam_rt/tests/min_heap_size.rs` is the oracle-cross-checked table: `+hms 100` → 142 words, `+hms 4000` → 4185, the 233-word floor by default).
`+hmax N` reaches the policy the process actually runs under on **every** route: the CLI value (`src/cli.rs` → `HeapOptions::set_max_heap_words` → `RuntimeConfig::heap`) is cloned by `Runtime::default_spawn_policy`/the boot-time spawns (`src/runtime.rs`) into the `beam_process::Process` the collector owns, and `spawn_opt`'s `{max_heap_size,…}` is installed by `SpawnOptions::policy` (`src/native/process.rs`) on the same field; `Runtime::new`'s `EmulatorDefault`/`Immix` resolution rewrites only `initial_mature_words`. Both are observable through `process_info(P, max_heap_size)` and `system_info(max_heap_size)`.
`+hmax N` is a **cumulative** ceiling on the nursery chain: `beam_gc::Nursery::grow` may only claim the budget the chain has left (`max_heap_words - capacity_words()`), refuses a request that budget cannot reach with the typed heap exhaustion, and re-adopts an empty retired space instead of appending a fresh one ([`../../beam_gc/CONTEXT.md`](../beam_gc/CONTEXT.md); pins `beam_gc/tests/{nursery_chain,nursery_growth_retention}.rs`).
Residual: a refused growth is served from the pointer-stable arena instead of raising the C's `system_limit` — both unrooted growth seams (`src/context/gc.rs::alloc_words`, `src/context/calls.rs::move_result_into_process_heap`) fall through to `Runtime::alloc_words` when `Process::grow_nursery` declines, so the block sits outside the heap accounting the verdict is computed from ([`HANDOFF.md`](HANDOFF.md)); and `max_heap_words == 0` is every unpoliced route's default and means "no upper bound", so an unbounded policy still grows with its admission-point count; the ceiling holds only while growth is the sole way capacity moves.
`process_flag(max_heap_size, Map|Int)` and `spawn_opt`'s `{max_heap_size, Map}` share **one** parser (`src/native/process.rs::max_heap_setting` building `src/config.rs::MaxHeapSetting`, re-exported from `beam_process::{MaxHeapFlags, MaxHeapSetting}`): a bare integer or a map with a mandatory `size` and the three boolean keys `kill`/`error_logger`/`include_shared_binaries`, the previous setting answered as the oracle's four-key map in the C's construction order (`error_logger, include_shared_binaries, kill, size`, `erl_gc.c:3807-3822`), and the `badarg` matrix — a missing `size`, a `size` below the process's **own** `min_heap_words` (`MaxHeapSetting::violates_floor`), a non-integer/negative/float `size`, a non-boolean flag, a non-map value — leaving the previous setting untouched.
Both routes install the **whole** setting, flags included, on the PCB (`Process::set_max_heap_size`); it is caller-scoped (a child spawned later is born with the default map) and read back by `process_info(P, max_heap_size)`/`garbage_collection` (`tests/max_heap_flag.rs`).
The bound **is enforced** on the **prospective post-collection capacity**, never occupancy: every collection/admission and `serve_gc_request` drains the verdict (`src/context/gc.rs::drain_max_heap_verdict` — the ERTS `=ERROR REPORT====` block on **stderr**, exactly **once** per killing breach, printed before the `Kill` is latched, the C's report-then-kill order, `erl_gc.c:3744-3803`), and `src/runner/run.rs::take_max_heap_kill` turns the latch into `RunOutcome::Exit(<bare atom killed>)` on the returned/wait/err/yield paths, so the ordinary `terminate` propagates `{'EXIT',Pid,killed}`/`'DOWN' … killed` (`tests/max_heap_enforce.rs`).
Residuals, pinned there: (a) the report is on **stderr** with the bare `=ERROR REPORT====` header where the oracle's `logger` writes to **stdout** with `==== <timestamp> ===`; (b) 7 of the C's 9 fields (`GC Info`, `Stacktrace` omitted, never fabricated); (c) a latched `Kill` is performed at the runner's **next slice boundary**, so the victim may run the rest of its slice; (d) the `-eval` action process is never enqueued, so a verdict latched there is never drained; (f) `include_shared_binaries`' off-heap figure is the **last pre-emption's** surviving payload bytes, published by the `Yield`-arm reclamation (`Process::set_off_heap_binary_bytes`, `src/context/gc/bin_vheap.rs:463`): a slice's freshly built payloads are charged only after its pre-emption, a process that has not pruned yet falls back to the PCB's own roster-derived sum (empty in production, so nothing), and once the override exists it **replaces** that sum rather than adding to it — the C's `bin_old_vheap` term is absent too, so the charge is a small under-estimate, never an over-estimate (`beam_process::MaxHeapUsage::charged_words`).
The pins are `tests/max_heap_enforce.rs` (12 oracle-cross-checked cases), `tests/max_heap_flag.rs` and the fixture `tests/fixtures/maxheap.{erl,beam}`.
`+hmax` bounds user processes, never the runtime's own boot processes: `Runtime::system_spawn_policy`/`default_spawn_policy` hand boot-spawned processes an uncapped policy (`max_heap_words = 0`) inside the `Runtime::booting` window around the boot-script drive, while the action process and `erlang:spawn*` children keep the user bound; a genuine over-cap allocation on the `-eval` route therefore reports as `beam_rt: system_limit` (exit 1), never as a boot server's death behind `unsupported: opcode: run: process blocked` (exit 3). Residual: at `+hmax ≤ 1024` the boot's own **term construction** still fails and `src/boot/drive.rs` maps it to `blocked()` → exit 3 (a `src/boot/`-owned mapping).

* **`erlang:memory/0,1` are adopted**; **`erlang:statistics/1` is partly served** (its `garbage_collection` key carries the real node-global counters), the per-scheduler/msacc keys and `statistics/0` being typed gaps; **the heap switches are modelled** through `beam_gc::HeapPolicy`, and `+hmax N` **is** a cumulative ceiling on the nursery chain — a growth may only claim `max_heap_words - capacity_words()`, the verdict compares the chain sum (`beam_process::MaxHeapUsage::heap_words`), and an over-cap allocation answers `error:system_limit` (measured: a 833 259-word live term under `+hmax 50000` exits 1, the same term under `+hmax 1000000` succeeds at `total_heap_size` 833 259) — `M1_GAPS_GC.md` *Heap switches*.
  Residual: a growth **refused** at an unrooted seam (`context/gc.rs::alloc_words`, `context/calls.rs::move_result_into_process_heap`) falls through to the pointer-stable arena instead of raising, so that block sits outside the heap accounting the verdict is computed from.

* **`max_heap_size` is ENFORCED, end to end** — `src/native/process.rs::max_heap_setting` serves `process_flag` and `spawn_opt`, a breach is the ERTS `=ERROR REPORT====` block plus a `Kill` latch, and the check is on the **prospective post-collection capacity**, never occupancy; the residuals are the stderr-only report and **7 of the C's 9 fields**.

* **`+hmax` bounds user processes, never the runtime's own boot processes** — `Runtime::system_spawn_policy`/`default_spawn_policy` hand boot-spawned processes an uncapped policy inside the `Runtime::booting` window, so an over-cap `-eval` allocation is `beam_rt: system_limit` (exit 1); at `+hmax ≤ 1024` the boot's own term construction still fails and is mapped to `blocked()` → exit 3.

## GC-relocation hazards (what a MOVING engine heap would invalidate)
The engine's `alloc_words` seam prefers the running process's own young heap (a capacity refusal met by a no-relocation `Process::grow_nursery` and one retry) and falls back to the pointer-stable `Runtime::arena`, so what a moving heap would dangle is a raw `Eterm` pinned **outside** the GC root set.
`Process::regs` is a snapshot, not a live mirror: `publish_roots_full`/`scatter_roots_full` (`context/gc.rs`) copy the x file's **pointer-bearing prefix**, the whole stack and the FR prefix (bounded by `fr_count`) at every full-root admission point (`gc::ensure_heap_full`/`alloc_words_rooted`, bound `gc.rs::machine_root_bounds`) or at a **publishing** (non-exempt) park, so a collection reaches x/stack/FR and nothing else — a Rust local or an un-published `Machine` field is not in the root set, and the engine's `live` count is ignored.
The admission-point collection *is* the publish/collect/scatter hand-off (safe); the delivery- and signal-driven ones are refused for a receiver with unpublished roots (`Process::has_unpublished_roots()` = `relocation_exempt || state == Running`, `beam_process/src/process.rs:1439`, tested *first* by every caller — a refused delivery becomes a heap-fragment delivery, a refused ptab signal goes to `enqueue_fragment_signal`), `beam_rt` adds `delivery_collection_is_unsound`, and `RuntimeCollectionGate` declines a woken-but-not-yet-resumed (`Runnable`) receiver; an **exempt** park (a live FR prefix) publishes nothing and its `serve_gc_request` is declined, while every other park — a dead-FR `Wait` park included — publishes first and then serves. The `Yield` arm is exempt exactly when the slice ended with a live FR prefix (`fr_count > 0`), and the residual exposure is a live term outside `process.regs` — hence `relocation_exempt` (`self()`, the wrapped `-eval`/nested-`Machine` scope, the live-FR-prefix park window).
A collection is also DECLINED when a root — or a container the scanned graph reaches — lies in an unscanned region (the unscanned-root valve): `RuntimeContext::call_registered` hands `beam_bifs` the pooled 16 MiB `Runtime::bif_heaps` and `gc::alloc_words`'s fallback hands blocks out of `Runtime::arena`, both `Target::Foreign` (`beam_gc/src/copy.rs` leaves a pointer outside `FromSet` as-is). Its two phases (`root_is_unscanned`, then the `gc_walk.rs` descent under the per-pid latch) span the published machine roots plus the process's own root-set containers (`Process::collect`'s registers, mailbox, dict, saved arguments, pending signals); a refusal needs *both* an unscanned region (`gc::word_region` → `WordRegion::Foreign`) and a child the collector would follow or update (`forwards_scanned_word` → `word_is_scanned`), so a reference, pid, port, float, bitstring, empty tuple and an all-immediate block are inert. The latch's raison d'être is `Runtime::bif_heaps` — a container a registered BIF built there (`erlang:'++'/2`, `lists:append/2`, `lists:reverse/1`, `lists:filter/2`, `ctx.tuple(…)`) writes the caller's heap `Eterm`s verbatim, so its holder is reachable only through the unrewound paths (a NIF result, an escaping `DispatchHooks` arm, the `-eval`/nested shape, an error result) at depth ≥ 2 below a scanned root; `beam_rt run w.beam w:f9/0` renders the oracle's exact `~w` at exit 0.
All five decline sites (the three admission points, `Runtime::serve_gc_request`, `delivery_collection_is_unsound`) answer `Ok(())`, and both allocation doors try the owner's young heap first (a non-forced refusal `grow_nursery`s and retries the bump) **before** the valve and the arena, leaving the `Process::collection_debt` the next admission point's occupancy test turns into a real collection.
