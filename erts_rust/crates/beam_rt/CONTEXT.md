# `beam_rt` — Rust ERTS Runtime Binary (boot · CLI · `system_info` · runtime glue)
## Intent
Top of the workspace: produces the `beam_rt` executable and is the only crate that sees the whole picture — `main.rs`, the **CLI flag surface** (**A.6**), **`system_info/1`** + `erlang:process_info/2` (**A.8**) and the **boot sequence** (`src/boot/{preloaded,bootscript,drive}.rs`), which loads the 22 preloaded `.beam`s for real and runs the real `bootstrap/bin/start.boot` to completion (`{progress,started}`, **A.7**); every CLI action but `-version` drives that script, so a silent action exits **0** with empty stdout and stderr.
It owns the **concrete `ExecutionContext` glue** onto the real `beam_bifs` registry / `beam_alloc` heap / `beam_process` pid — every optional `beam_bifs` host seam, the **A.4 runtime side of ports**, the **runtime-local timer deadlines** (`src/timers.rs`) and the **distributed node** (A.5: `src/dist/` accepting, `src/dist_client/` connecting).
The vertical slice runs on a test-only `MinimalContext` at the engine level (`../beam_engine/tests/slice.rs`) and through this crate's real glue (`Runtime::run_function`/`run_module`).
It depends on **all** other crates (wave 6) but **calls down, never duplicates**: BIFs → `beam_bifs`, terms/heaps → `beam_term`/`beam_alloc`, pids → `beam_process`, modules → `beam_loader`, execution → `beam_engine`.
## API Surface (FROZEN — the runtime façade; re-exported at the crate root and `beam_rt::prelude::*`)

### `dist.rs` + `dist/` / `dist_client/` — the distributed node (A.5, phases 1–2)
`DistNode` is the *accepting* half and `DistClient` the *connecting* half, sharing **one** node→link table (`src/dist/links.rs::DistLinks`), so a locally-initiated `link`/`monitor`/`exit` or a plain send reaches a peer **whichever side opened the socket** (`Runtime::dist_ensure_link`).
Both inbound drives drain every link; `LocalDeliverySink` routes the message and route-free receipt families through `ProcessTable`'s inbound primitives (`Runtime::inbound_delivery`, default **ON**), peer loss fires `noconnection`, and `Runtime::dist_connect` is idempotent — the `epmd` publish, the native `net_kernel` service (a peer's `net_adm:ping/1` answers `pong`) and the event-vs-control ordering rules are [`src/dist/CONTEXT.md`](src/dist/CONTEXT.md), with `tests/dist_two_vm{,_rust,_wire}.rs` the evidence.
### `nif.rs` — the live NIF libraries (A.3), and `build.rs`
`NifEntry{lib, host: Box<NifHostImpl<'static>>}` (**field order is load-bearing**: `lib`'s `Drop` runs the C `unload` against the erased host pointer) and the `!Send` `NifRegistry` (`lookup` is **the dispatch predicate**) are `Runtime`'s `Rc<RefCell<..>>`; a NIF **call** runs on a **fresh** per-call `NifHostImpl` sharing the runtime's **one** `Rc<AtomTable>`, and `src/nif/thread_env.rs` is the process-independent host a NIF-created thread runs on.
`build.rs` emits `-Wl,--export-dynamic`, which with `NifRegistry::new`'s anchor publishes the `enif_*` symbols a real NIF's `dlopen` needs — [`src/nif/CONTEXT.md`](src/nif/CONTEXT.md) holds the eleven `NifHostServices` hooks, the `enif_schedule_nif` trampoline, the `enif_select` STOP verdicts and the private-atom-space hub; `M1_GAPS_A3_NIF.md`.

### `malloc.rs` — the allocator's free-page release
`beam_rt::release_free_pages()` is the crate's single allocator lever (`libc::malloc_trim(0)` on glibc, an honest `None` elsewhere), called **once per process** from `src/main.rs` after the `"beam_rt-driver"` thread is joined — the only quiescent *and* complete point; the runtime is not torn down at exit (`ExitTeardown::Leak`), so it finds a largely **live** arena, returns little, and peak `VmHWM` is untouched. `BEAM_RT_MEM_TRACE` prints one `[rt-mem] heap-release=…` line and nothing depends on it — [`src/CONTEXT.md`](src/CONTEXT.md) § *The allocator's free-page release*.
### Observability (`trace.rs` · `seq_trace.rs` · `crash_dump.rs` · `perf_map.rs`, contract A.8)
Process tracing, sequential tracing, the ERTS-format crash-dump writer behind `erlang:halt`, the `+JPperf` JIT symbol map, the **execution-trace seam** (`call`/`return_from`/`return_to` over `ExecutionContext::trace_event`, on both execution paths) and the three per-call harnesses (`examples/perf_{bif,call,copy_out}.rs`) — [`TRACE_AND_PERF.md`](TRACE_AND_PERF.md).
Env-gated, silent-unless-set diagnostics live in `src/debug_trace.rs`: `BEAM_RT_BOOT_TRACE`, `BEAM_RT_DEBUG_DIST`, `BEAM_RT_MEM_TRACE`, `BEAM_RT_GC_TRACE`, `BEAM_RT_STORE_TRACE`, plus `BEAM_RT_TIER` (the execution tier) and `BEAM_RT_REGION_RECLAIM` (the region-reclaim A/B kill switch, `src/runtime/reclaim.rs` — it can disable an *action*, never a correctness guard); nothing depends on any of them and nothing may emit unconditionally.
### Crash reports (`src/report.rs`) — an abnormal termination's `=ERROR REPORT====` block
One report per process that dies from an uncaught **`error`-class** exception (an uncaught `throw` is promoted to `{nocatch, V}` and reports too), on **stdout**, in the default handler's shape: the `logger_formatter` legacy header, `Error in process <Pid> with exit value:` and the `{Reason, Stacktrace}` term — rendered by the one `io:format/2` `~p` printer (`Runtime::format_term`), never a second formatter.
Silent, matching the oracle: an `exit`-class reason (`exit/1`, `normal`, `shutdown`, `killed` — hence every link/monitor/`exit/2` propagation), a trapped `{'EXIT', …}`, a normal return, and the process the `run` action is itself waiting for. The one call site is the runner's termination arm, and the stacktrace is the raise's own (`beam_common::StackTrace`).
Honest gaps: the header timestamp is **UTC** (the oracle renders local time), no `logger`/`logger:add_handler`/`logger_formatter` configuration, no SASL `supervisor`/`gen_server` reports, no `erlang:error/2,3` arglist (`error_info`) form and no `max_heap_size` report.
## Host-side term stores — owned copy vs raw word (the rule that makes a store sound)
- **A raw `Eterm` in a long-lived slot roots nothing** — a term that can outlive one instruction (a raise reason, a pending reply, a parked value, a cache entry) must be copied onto a scanned heap or explicitly stabilized before its owner's heap can be relocated.
- **A pointer-stable, never-scanned store (the arena, `alloc_words`) is the wrong home for a container whose children live on a scanned young heap** — the copier gives it `Target::Foreign` and never descends.
- **An exempted heap-growth or move must "refuse ⇒ stabilize, never dangle"** — the refusing path first makes its roots publishable.
- **An `Rc` lease held across an allocation is a deep-copy or a dangling hazard, never a cheap borrow.**
A stored term is self-contained only if it went through `Runtime::stabilize_term`/`try_stabilize_escaped` (a copy onto the `eval_heap` + `eval_off_heap` store), was built on the pointer-stable arena, or was built on a pooled BIF heap the escaping `runtime_hooks` arm froze; every store is **pointer-pinned**, so the one thing it cannot survive is a **move** of the region it names — done only where nothing is executing (`-eval` statement boundary, driver exit), rewriting every stored word through `Runtime::for_each_root_family_mut`.
Roster and per-site inventory: [`DESIGN_DECISIONS.md`](DESIGN_DECISIONS.md) *Host-side term stores*, `src/context/IMPLEMENTATION.md`, [`src/runtime/roots/CONTEXT.md`](src/runtime/roots/CONTEXT.md).
## The runtime-native BIF floor (`src/native/`)
The floor serves the BIFs that are really *runtime* operations, from `RuntimeContext::try_native`, **before** the `beam_bifs` registry, and **a native entry never duplicates a registry one** (an unclaimed call returns `None`).
`native::FLOOR_MODULES` (14 names) gates only `resolve_external_call`, **not** `call_bif`'s dispatch: a registry key in a floor module's namespace that `try_native` has no arm for still reaches the registry (`os:env/0` is `beam_bifs`'s — the floor owns only `os:cmd/1`).
The floor owns the process/name/global-state arms (`ProcessBridge`'s only implementation), `process_info/1,2`, the monitor/demonitor/alias family with the reference-alias send destination (`src/context/alias.rs::alias_destination`, on **both** send seams) and `garbage_collect/0,1` — **no BIF may collect**, so a servable call records **one** request through `Runtime::request_gc` and answers the oracle's `true`; **`code:*` reads and the code path are the floor over `CodeServer`**, whose expected-module-name check ends every name-based load (`M1_GAPS.md` *The code store*) — [`src/native/CONTEXT.md`](src/native/CONTEXT.md) is the dispatch table.
`Runtime::new` starts exactly **four** runtime-owned, never-enqueued `Runnable` processes (the `init` stand-in `<0.0.0>`, the root process `<0.1.0>` every action runs as, the `erl_prim_loader` stand-in and the `file_server_2` service). `call_bif`'s last resort is the **`erlang` namespace**: a **known** unimplemented OTP `erlang` BIF raises `unsupported: erlang:X/Y` (exit 3), any other unclaimed name the bare `error::undef` — `src/error.rs`'s `KNOWN_UNIMPLEMENTED_ERLANG_BIFS` and its drift guard are the authority.
## The local-call seam (a module's own non-exported NIF stubs, A.7)
`ExecutionContext::resolve_local_call` (`src/context/execution.rs`) serves **two** built-in key sets — `prim_tty`'s 20 stub keys and `zlib`'s 19 stream keys — from a module-name equality short circuit plus a fixed `(name, arity)` table test, so the predicate allocates nothing and touches neither the registry nor a hash (the same predicate `beam_bifs::serves_local_call` exposes).
`src/context/apply.rs` (`served_local_key` + `apply_non_local_fun`) serves the **record-held** shape of the same two sets: `zlib.erl` stores `fun deflate_nif/4` in a `#zlib_opts{}` record and applies it, so `deflate/3`, `inflate/3` and `safeInflate/2` reach the served key through a local `make_fun3` closure instead of entering its `erlang:nif_error(undef)` body.
The `zlib` stream API is live end to end through the real preloaded `zlib.beam`, oracle-identical at every level 0–9 — `tests/zlib_{stream_levels,stream_dict,guards,one_shot}.rs`; mechanics in [`src/context/CONTEXT.md`](src/context/CONTEXT.md) § *The in-machine call and fun seams*.
## CLI subset (contract A.6 — anything else is *unsupported*, exit 3)
| Switch | Meaning in `beam_rt` |
|---|---|
| `-version`/`--version`, `-noshell` | the version banner (OTP `30.0-rc0` / erts `17.1`, exit 0); head-less run |
| `-eval Expr` | evaluate a general Erlang expression, print **nothing**; one trailing `.` tolerated and stripped |
| `-s Mod Func [Args…]` / `-run Mod Func [Args…]` | apply `Mod:Func(a, b, …)` — arguments **atoms** for `-s` (result **discarded**), plain **strings** (char lists) for `-run` |
| `-pa Dir` / `-pz Dir` | add `Dir` to the code path: `-pa` **heads** it (the group reversed), `-pz` **tails** it (behind the system `ebin`s), on **every** route |
| `+S Total[:Online]` | scheduler counts → `emulator.schedulers` / `online_schedulers`; `+SP`/`+SDcpu`/`+SDPcpu`/`+SDio` validated and preserved verbatim (inert here) |
| `+A`/`+P`/`+Q`/`+t`/`+e` | async-pool size (preserved verbatim), max processes, max ports (`system_info(port_limit)` = `min(next_power_of_two(request), 134217727)`, default `65536`), atom/ETS table sizes |
| `+sbt`/`+K`/`+hms`/`+hmbs`/`+hmax`… | scheduler bind type and kernel-poll switch preserved verbatim (an unknown bind type is the C's rejection); GC heap policy (`beam_gc::HeapPolicy`), visible in `garbage_collection` |
| `-extra …` / `-- …` | **ends the switch surface**: the rest are plain arguments (`RuntimeConfig::plain_args`) `init:get_plain_arguments/0` answers as a list of strings, split per **entity** (`ERL_OTP30_FLAGS`/`ERL_AFLAGS`/argv/`ERL_FLAGS`/`ERL_ZFLAGS`) at that entity's first `-extra` |
| `-name N` / `-sname N` | node name → `emulator.node_name`; **makes the runtime a distributed node** (A.5) |
| `run <file.beam> <mod>:<func>/<arity> [arg…]` | **the vertical slice**: run the file as a **scheduled process** via `Runtime::run_scheduled` (a blocking driver/port round trip resumes on a later pass) and **print** the result (`~w~n`), bounded by `RUN_DEADLINE` |
The per-switch grammar — a value **attached or separate** (`+S1` ≡ `+S 1`, `+A0`, `+sbtu`, the form the real `erlc` passes), `-smp`, the `+Q`-vs-`ERL_MAX_PORTS` routing, the `run` action's `ARG` token kinds and the `ERL_*FLAGS` merge — is [`src/cli/CONTEXT.md`](src/cli/CONTEXT.md) § *The supported switches (the exact A.6 surface)*.
* **The OTP `erlc` C front end drives this runtime unchanged** (A.6+A.9): `ERLC_EMULATOR=<beam_rt> erlc +deterministic -o <D> <src>` (argv `+S1 +sbtu +A0 +P 65536 +Q 1024 -noinput -mode minimal -boot no_dot_erlang -s erl_compile compile_cmdline -extra …`) exits 0 with a byte-identical artifact — `tests/erlc_emulator.rs`, `tests/cli_compact_flags.rs`, `tests/cli_plain_args.rs`.
* **Every command-line flag is an init argument** (the source of `init:get_argument/1`) and an unmodeled switch is **data, not a usage error**.
### The `-eval` route (`src/eval/`)
`-eval` walks the Erlang **expression** subset `erl_eval`-style — arithmetic/comparison/**bit operators** dispatched to the **`beam_bifs` `erlang` registry**, binary segments accumulating a bit buffer — and anything outside the grammar is a **capability gap** (exit 3).
`receive … after T` scans the running process's **real mailbox** and spends its window across the scheduler, one port-poller pass and the timer heap, delivering that round's port messages into the mailbox before any clause is tried (mechanics in [`src/eval/CONTEXT.md`](src/eval/CONTEXT.md)).
A closure `erlang:spawn/1` starts is **compiled** into a synthetic `beam_rt_eval_fun_<index>` module, so the child is an ordinary `ProcessEntry::Export` that really parks on a bare `receive` or a `timer:sleep/1`, with the result — a decline included — cached per closure index.
**Fun application has an explicit nesting budget, because the walker's continuation is the native Rust stack** (`src/eval/nesting.rs`, counters per thread): the tree walker up to `WALK_APPLY_LIMIT = 512`, the **compile route** past it (so `F = fun G(0) -> done; G(N) -> G(N - 1) end, F(50000)` ⇒ `done` on the BEAM process stack), and the typed `unsupported: eval fun application: …` gap once `COMPILED_APPLICATION_LIMIT = 16` compiled applications are already nested; an application inside a scheduler slice **cannot** compile and refuses instead of pumping — `tests/eval_deep_recursion.rs` (value, exit 0, never a signal; the refusal is exit 3 with one `unsupported:` line and empty stdout), mechanics in [`src/eval/CONTEXT.md`](src/eval/CONTEXT.md).
## Exit codes & the `unsupported` signal (the stable external contract)
The CLI exposes **four** exit codes; the differential harness keys off this mapping.
| Code | Meaning | stderr |
|---|---|---|
| `0` (`EXIT_OK`) | success | — |
| `1` (`EXIT_RUNTIME`) | genuine runtime error (a load/execute/BIF failure: a missing `.beam`, an `undef`, BIF `system_limit`) | `beam_rt: <error>` |
| `2` (`EXIT_USAGE`) | malformed invocation (a missing operand of a *known* switch, a malformed `run` spec or numeric value, a stray positional) | `beam_rt: <error>` then [`USAGE`] |
| `3` (`EXIT_UNSUPPORTED`) | **unsupported** — a Milestone-1 capability gap | one line: `beam_rt: unsupported: <reason>` (no [`USAGE`]) |
`unsupported` is emitted for a `-eval` expression outside the grammar, a BEAM-engine gap (`unsupported_opcode…`) or a `beam_bifs` gap (`unsupported_bif`), and is **uncatchable** (`src/eval/mod.rs`'s `flow_from_error` turns it into `Flow::Fatal`, like `halt`); the convention is a raise whose **reason atom starts with `unsupported`**, of which `src/error.rs::unsupported_reason` is the **single** interpreter, classified through `ExitReason::as_atom`.
**A raise's reason atom is named in the shared atom table before the raise leaves the call**, or the engine cannot land it in a catch frame: `src/context/calls.rs::name_raise_reason_atom` appends it (append-only, at `PendingInterner`'s predicted index) on the **one** funnel every failed call passes — **every** atom-reasoned error is named, a capability gap included, so `try … catch` in compiled code observes a typed gap instead of dying (`tests/gap_raise_catch.rs`, [`src/context/calls/CONTEXT.md`](src/context/calls/CONTEXT.md)).
A raise may carry an opaque live reason whose payload is an `Eterm` (`beam_common::Raise`); `src/error.rs::live_reason_term` renders it through `format_term_w`, so `beam_rt run <m>.beam m:badkey/0` → `beam_rt: error {badkey,nokey}` (exit 1).
## `system_info/1` keys (contract A.8)
`InfoValue` is the heap-free source of truth; `Runtime::system_info` materializes it on the scratch heap, and strings are Erlang **strings** (lists of char small-ints) exactly as the oracle returns them.
The surface answers **88** supported keys plus the two tuple keys (`{allocator, A}`, `{wordsize, internal|external}` via `info::tuple_key_value`; an unknown word/tag is `badarg`) and `error:badarg` for any other key; the **27** oracle-accepted keys naming facilities this runtime lacks raise the honest `unsupported` gap, and the two sets partition the oracle's 115 accepted names, so a `badarg` where the oracle answers a value can only come from an unclassified key (`tests/system_info_oracle.rs`) — **the per-key catalog is [`src/info/CONTEXT.md`](src/info/CONTEXT.md)**.
## Constraints
* **Errors are return-based** (`../CONTEXT.md` §3): every fallible API returns `beam_common::Result`, with no crate-local error enum, no `panic!`/`unwrap()` on runtime data and no silent swallowing; CLI diagnostics are `BeamError::Raise(Raise::error(ExitReason::atom(msg)))`, never stderr writes.
* **`#![deny(unsafe_code)]` with ten per-module exemptions**, each carrying a `// SAFETY:` rationale at every site — `src/malloc.rs`, `src/nif.rs`, `src/context/{calls,gc,gc_walk}.rs` + `src/context/gc/{bin_vheap,valve_bounds}.rs`, `src/eval/reclaim.rs` + `src/eval/reclaim/resource.rs`, `src/runtime/reclaim/compact.rs` (the store move).
* **Call down, never duplicate** — the `~p` printer is **not** re-implemented; `format.rs` drives the registered `io:format/2` BIF.
* **A live `[BinRef][ErlSubBits]` pair must always have a live payload owner** — reachable from `Machine::refc_binaries()` or `Runtime::eval_off_heap`; the transport copy resolves one of them into its **source** table so the destination **shares** the handle, and a source table that cannot name the owner makes `beam_message`'s copier **flatten** the window into a fresh on-heap bitstring as the **last resort** only.
* **A live heap serves its own builds; the arena is a refusal-only fallback** — `alloc_words_rooted` serves a nested run from the running process's scanned young heap, the arena answers only a table-less context or a refused growth, and inside a relocation-exempt window the copy-out `stabilize`s instead of growing — [`DESIGN_DECISIONS.md`](DESIGN_DECISIONS.md) *A live heap serves its own builds*.
* **A park that skips root publication holds the relocation exemption** — the three runner park arms take the one `park_window` entry point and write the flag in both directions, so the halves are un-splittable — [`DESIGN_DECISIONS.md`](DESIGN_DECISIONS.md) *A park that skips root publication*.
### The one process-lifetime `Rc<AtomTable>` lease
**Never hold a `CodeServer::atoms_handle()` across an intern** — [`src/codeserver.rs`](src/codeserver.rs) states the rule on the method: `atoms_mut()` is `Rc::make_mut` and `intern_pending()` falls back to it, so an `Rc<AtomTable>` clone held **across** an intern deep-clones the whole shared table and drops the copy; only **one** holder lives long enough to matter — a loaded NIF library's stored host (`src/nif.rs`'s `NifEntry::host`, alive for the process's life). A `{spawn_driver, Name}` port's host is **not** a holder: its view is a **lease** on the `DriverAtoms` record, opened by `PortRegistry::open_driver_atom_view` only for the window in which driver code runs and closed (re-entrant, depth-counted) by `close_driver_atom_view`; a built-in-driver or `{spawn, …}` port creates **no** host, so the code server is sole owner there.
The two production sites taking a handle — `src/native/prim_file.rs::build_descriptor`, `src/native/net/nodes.rs::nodes_2` — **intern first and take the handle last**; the port delivery builders intern **through `src/port.rs`'s `intern_reuse`** (look the name up on the shared table first, reach `atoms_mut()` only for a genuine miss); and the borrow-workaround clones (`src/eval/mod.rs`, `src/native/memory/mod.rs`, `src/native/erts_internal.rs`) each wrap a `make_map` that takes `&AtomTable` and cannot intern. `RuntimeContext` itself holds **no** atom handle — [`src/context/CONTEXT.md`](src/context/CONTEXT.md) § *The atom namespace and the one lifetime `Rc<AtomTable>` lease*.

## Known Issues / Intentional M1 gaps
**Every gap is a typed capability gap or a measured own-state difference — never a wrong result.** The full catalogue is the ledger index: **`G`** = [`M1_GAPS.md`](M1_GAPS.md), **`GC`** = [`M1_GAPS_GC.md`](M1_GAPS_GC.md), **`A3`** = [`M1_GAPS_A3_NIF.md`](M1_GAPS_A3_NIF.md), **`A4`** = [`M1_GAPS_A4_PORTS.md`](M1_GAPS_A4_PORTS.md); each line names the section that owns the detail.
* **Own state and introspection** — `processes/0`, `ports/0`, `port_count`, the boot-set size and `process_info` contents differ by construction (`process_info/1,2` answers ~28 items, `port_info(P, monitors)` stays `[]`, `minor_gcs` is `minor_since_major`, `process_info(P, binary)` is the reservoir ∪ PCB union while `erlang:memory(binary)` is separate, and a non-byte-aligned `-eval` result cannot be printed) — G *Own-state answers*, *`process_info/1,2`*, *Printing*.
* **Process control and ETS** — a named port monitor, a remote `{Name,Node}` monitor, `spawn_opt` options and `exit/2,3` on an internal port/foreign ref are gaps; ETS owner tracking and owner-death reclamation are live on both install routes, the table-alias boundary being a witness `Cell<bool>` rather than an address check — G *Process-control families*, *ETS ownership*.
* **Scheduler and tier** — single-threaded; a BIF-and-call-only loop ends no slice on the native tier (fairness) and a BIF-nested fun application is unbounded in depth (the driver thread's stack is the bound). `JitMode::Auto` is **phase-scoped**: the interpreter during the boot phase, the native baseline tier in steady state, a boot-entered process upgraded one-way at its first post-boot slice boundary, a pinned `BEAM_RT_TIER` phase-independent; the pre-emption residual (a natively linked tail loop with no allocation never pre-empts) is steady-state only and an upgraded process's code ranges are absent from `+JPperf` (diagnostic-only) — G *Scheduler / runner*, *Execution tier*, [`src/runner/CONTEXT.md`](src/runner/CONTEXT.md).
* **Funs and `-eval`** — a compiled module's local fun encodes faithfully while an `erl_eval` lambda stays `unsupported: local_fun` (A3, G *Funs*); `-eval` fun application compiles past 512 walked applications and otherwise answers the typed `unsupported` gap ([`src/eval/CONTEXT.md`](src/eval/CONTEXT.md)).
* **A.3 (NIF)** — every residual is a typed refusal or a deferred answer (plain callback-visible host state, a private-atom-space thread host, `nif_yield_point` a fairness no-op) — A3, [`src/nif/CONTEXT.md`](src/nif/CONTEXT.md); **A.4 (ports)** — message shapes and driver-port behaviour match the oracle, while a registered port name, a remote port term, an unreachable remote destination and a send to a non-alias reference (typed `badarg`; the C drops it) stay gaps — A4, [`src/port/CONTEXT.md`](src/port/CONTEXT.md).
* **A.5 (distribution)** — no Erlang-level distribution subtree, every inbound family is a route-free receipt, and an inbound `DOP_SEND_SENDER*` does not pass the wire sender pid as the mailbox `from` — G *Distribution and the node*.
* **GC / heap model** — four collection points (three heap-admission doors); the unscanned-root valve declines only where relocation is genuinely unsafe; the engine's refc-binary reservoir is pruned at every pre-emption and `Runtime::eval_off_heap` at two points, every gate a decline so the residual is a **leak, never a dangle**; `+hmax` is a cumulative ceiling over user processes only and `max_heap_size` is **ENFORCED** — GC *GC / heap model*, *The BIF-result copy-out*, *Heap switches*.
* **Printing, `eunit` and boot** — `io` output has **three** routes (the direct sink for the runtime's own stream, OTP's own `io.erl` in-machine for any other **live** leader so nothing can double-send, a synchronous file device) over a **14**-key floor, so `eunit:test/1` runs OTP's own framework: `jsone` **90/90** and `bbmustache` **79/79**, stdout and stderr byte-identical to the OTP 29.1.2 oracle; an `io` output call on a live non-stream device inside a **scheduler slice** is the typed `unsupported` gap and a nested run that may not pump folds a file-device round trip to `blocked` — G *Printing*, [`src/context/io_route.rs`](src/context/io_route.rs).
* **Boot (A.7/A.9), timers, CLI** — a booted runtime leaves a live `logger_server` registered as `logger`, the script's tail blockers are served in-node, and a process entry naming a host-served MFA is dispatched rather than entered; self-hosted compilation runs in ONE invocation via the committed OTP-30 bootstrap compiler with a documented >60 KB residual (G *A.7/A.9 boot*, *A.9 E0*, [`EPP_SCHEDULED_ROUTE_FINDINGS.md`](EPP_SCHEDULED_ROUTE_FINDINGS.md)). `send_after/3`/`cancel_timer/1` and the `RuntimeHooksBridge` timer/link/module keys are floor capabilities while `system_info/2` and the other `RuntimeHooks` keys are gaps (G *Timers*); `-smp disable`, `-setcookie` and the `-eval`/`-s`/`-run` subsets diverge ([`src/cli/CONTEXT.md`](src/cli/CONTEXT.md)), flag-driven `system_info` literals report their defaults (G *Placeholders*), and two load-sensitive failure shapes exist, both real exit codes, never a signal (G *Scheduler / runner*, *A.7/A.9 boot*).

## The scheduled path and the per-BIF-call cost (read before optimizing the glue)
`run_scheduled` (the CLI `run` action) drives **one** target pid after `boot_if_needed`, at `RUN_STEP_SLICES` passes per round and `DEFAULT_REDUCTIONS_PER_SLICE = CONTEXT_REDS`, so per-pass costs amortize while **per-slice** costs dominate a long run; the per-pass/slice/round inventory and the per-workload cost are [`src/runtime/CONTEXT.md`](src/runtime/CONTEXT.md) *The scheduled path's fixed costs* and [`src/runner/MEASURED.md`](src/runner/MEASURED.md).
A registered BIF call is priced in retired instructions, of which this crate owns the routing (`call_bif`'s NIF → floor → memo draw), the `BifContext` build and arm selection and the tail — the rest is `beam_engine`'s dispatch or the BIF body, and the ranked levers with their owners are [`PERF_DISPATCH.md`](PERF_DISPATCH.md) *The per-BIF-call cost, on one page*.
A handle built in `call_registered` must be gated by a `uses_*` predicate, and the `DispatchMemo` hit path, not the prologue, is what to price (`examples/perf_call.rs`).

## Startup footprint (a minimal `-noshell -eval 'halt().'` boot) — where the bytes are
The bare boot peaks at **48.53 MiB** `VmHWM` (5 interleaved randomized rounds), **0.817×** a contemporaneous 59.45 MiB oracle boot and within 0.05 MiB of the 48.48 MiB `BEAM_RT_TIER=interpreter` ceiling — the phase rule captures 99.3 % of the interpreter-boot advantage while steady state keeps the native tier — with boot wall/CPU p50 122.8 ms / 116.1 ms against the oracle's 133.7 / 128.1 (`new`/`oracle` paired medians 0.910 wall, 0.903 CPU).
**Capacity is not RSS**: the peak is allocator-visible (massif: 35.3 MiB heap + 1.6 MiB overhead), ≈24 MiB of it `beam_loader`'s lowering and ≈3.1 MiB the atom store's copy-on-write shard generations, while both 128 MiB boot reservations sit at 12–48 kB resident; **the 99 `.beam` files a bare boot opens (5.80 MiB) retain ≈20 MiB of IR**, 70 executing at least once and 29 never (≈5.8 MiB of dead IR).
The per-tier instrument table, the massif site decomposition, the per-workload peak-RSS rows, the `90.1`/`128.9` MiB compute-run correction and every caveat are **[`STARTUP_COST.md`](STARTUP_COST.md)** § *Where the bytes are — the minimal-run footprint*, § *Instructions are not time* and § *The 99 boot-loaded modules and what they retain*, with the loader's side in [`../beam_loader/CONTEXT.md`](../beam_loader/CONTEXT.md); `tests/rss_bound.rs` bounds the boot peak at 120 MiB.
