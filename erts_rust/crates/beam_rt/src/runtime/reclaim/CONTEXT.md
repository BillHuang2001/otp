# `beam_rt::runtime::reclaim` — the region-reclaim pass over the runtime's two long-lived term regions

## Intent
This node reclaims the runtime's two never-freed term regions: `Runtime::eval_heap` plus its `eval_heap_retired` chain (the `-eval`/table-less copy-out **store**) and `Runtime::arena` (the engine `MinimalContext` every table-less `alloc_words` lands on).
Both are **pointer-stable** by contract — a word handed to a caller stays valid until this pass releases it — and neither reclaims anything on its own: the store retains the total volume it ever copied (a replaced region is full, never rewritable, never dropped), and the arena is a ladder of reservations that only grows.
**One descent, two mechanisms, two sites.**
The sites are the two instants at which the runtime is not executing — the domain `Runtime::for_each_root_mut`/`for_each_root_family_mut` claims completeness over (`../roots.rs` *Completeness — this walk is whole only at a non-running site*): the `-eval` statement boundary (`crate::eval::scratch::boundary`) and the driver exit (`crate::eval::scratch::close`, once the driver's result or raise is known).
`open` runs from `scratch::open`, a pass from those two functions and `close(runtime)` from `scratch::close`; with the pre-existing diagnostic `Runtime::set_eval_scratch_enabled(false)` no window opens and neither site exists.
**The corpus** is that mutable door's families (23 `RootFamily` variants; `RootFamily::in_value_gather` is the machine-checkable record of which ones the read-only value gather deliberately skips) plus the caller's own live terms plus `RunnerState::for_each_error_live_reason`.
The caller's terms are the evaluator's `Env` at a boundary and, at the exit, those plus the driver's result — or a raise's reason and its stacktrace head — lent as **the `outcome` fields themselves** through `&mut outcome`, never as a copy: a copied slot is rewritten by the move while the word actually handed back still names the dropped region.
`for_each_error_live_reason` is the one family with a reader and no writable slot: for a drop it is just another live word (a region holding one is retained by evidence), while the move refuses a region such a word **stands in** (`R_REASON`).

## API Surface
### Phase 1a — whole-region actions, no word moves (`../reclaim.rs`)
A pass asks the term graph one question, once: does any reachable term stand inside a candidate range?
The ranges are the store's current region above this window's cursor `[cursor.store_mark, used)`, every retired region at or after `open`'s baseline, and every arena region at or above `open`'s mark (the marked one only above `mark.used`, since older regions the window never wrote are excluded).
The descent is `crate::eval::reclaim::walk` — the one walker — with `src/eval/scratch.rs::RangeProbe` as its sink, and it stops as soon as every range has an answer (`Reach::done`).
Three independent actions follow:
- **a whole-dead retired region is dropped** from `eval_heap_retired` (its `Heap::drop` frees the carrier, so RSS really falls) → `store_regions_dropped` + `store_words_reclaimed`.
- **a whole-dead prefix of the CURRENT region is `reset_to(cursor.store_mark)`**, never `reset_to(0)`: words below the cursor were written before this window opened, by a run whose root set this pass cannot enumerate.
- **the arena is rewound** through **Tier A** `MinimalContext::heap_rewind(cursor.arena_mark)` → `arena_reclaims`/`arena_words_reclaimed`/`arena_regions_dropped`; a rewind bumps the mark epoch, so the cursor takes the new frontier or every later pass would refuse.
Tier A rather than Tier B's `reclaim_heap`, whose fallible `forward` closure leaves the arena **empty** with the epoch bumped and the caller's roots abandoned on `Err`, whereas `ContextHeap::rewind` validates the epoch, the region index, the base address and the used figure **before** it mutates anything — so a refusal leaves the arena byte-identical and is a decline, never an error.
`raise_store_floor` adopts the statement window's own mark (which only ever advances) as the current-region floor when the two name the same region, because a reset below it would make the next boundary refuse.
A pass with no candidate range costs one `Vec` size test and no descent; a window that wrote nothing returns before the descent and records nothing.
### Phase 1b — the evacuation move (`./compact.rs`)
The shape Phase 1a cannot touch is the **interleaved** one: a statement binding several values keeps one live word in each of several otherwise-dead regions, so each is partially live and retained whole.
The move evacuates every word reachable from the mutable corpus out of the current region's candidate prefix and every retired candidate into **one** fresh region, then drops the sources.
It is attempted only when the candidates hold at least `MIN_WINDOW_WORDS` (1 024 words = 8 KiB, `../../eval/scratch.rs`), at least **half** of those words are dead by the exact `beam_gc::live_words_in` count over the same corpus, and at least one carrier would be released (`mark != 0 && retired.is_empty()` is `Skipped`, since a fresh region releasing nothing is a pure loss).
Below a threshold the outcome is `MoveOutcome::Skipped` silently and the whole-region action still runs; above it a refusal is recorded on the move's own counters.
The copy is `beam_gc`'s `FromSet::from_ranges` + `LinearDest::new` + `Relocator::new_checked` + `forward_roots` over the families then the caller's slots, then `complete()`.
The destination's capacity is the store's own growth policy — `Runtime::eval_store_growth_capacity(current, need.max(EVAL_STORE_GROWTH_FLOOR))`, which keeps the current capacity when it already serves the live set, else doubles, floored at `EVAL_STORE_GROWTH_FLOOR` = 256 words and capped at `MAX_EVAL_HEAP_WORDS` = 1 << 25 (256 MiB) — then clamped by the total capacity of the regions it replaces, so a multi-region collapse always releases memory.
**Headroom is a correctness property of a move, not a tuning knob:** `eval_heap` has exactly one growing consumer (the table-less copy-out's `grow_eval_heap`) while every other writer — `Runtime::stabilize_term`, the evaluator's own term building, the native floor's `make_binary` replies — copies into the region it finds and answers the typed `error:system_limit` when it is short, so a destination sized from the live set alone would shrink every other writer's bound (`../../context/CONTEXT.md` *The store's writers that do not grow*).
The win is collapsing N regions into one, never making that one small: a capacity-sized region is a lazy `mmap` that costs address space, not RSS.
### The move's completeness precondition
`beam_gc`'s relocator writes a forwarding marker into every source object it copies, so "undo the copy" is not achievable once a word moved.
The sequence is: build the destination fresh; forward the corpus and run the destination scan; re-run the **value** descent from the forwarded roots over the OLD ranges with the same walker and sink the whole-region pass uses; then **any hit — or a copy that failed mid-way — keeps BOTH**.
`MoveOutcome::KeptBoth` installs the destination as a *retired* region (so the copies its rewritten roots name stay valid and `Runtime::for_each_off_heap_region` still visits them) and leaves every source installed, so nothing dangles; only **zero** hits commit.
A post-copy refusal deliberately does **not** fall through to the whole-region drop: `pass` matches `Moved | KeptBoth => {}` against `Skipped | Declined => store_action(…)`, because the descent that just failed is known incomplete for exactly those ranges; the next pass re-classifies on fresh evidence.
A hit is **attributed** (`verification_owner` → `MoveHitOwner`, published as `store_move_hit`): the caller's `MoveSlots` re-walked one at a time, then the corpus family by family through `Runtime::for_each_root_family_mut` (a cheap whole-slice reject, then one walk per root of a hitting slice), naming `CallerSlot(i)`, `Family(family, slot)` or `OutsideCorpus` — the last being itself a finding, since no slot of the corpus reproduces the hit.
Attribution runs only on the hit path, so its cost (up to one extra descent per corpus slot) is never paid by a move that verifies clean.
The stated residual: an object the corpus *did* enumerate moved, so its old address holds a forwarding marker a missed holder would read; and a **boxed** object whose source word was forwarded holds the `BOXED` marker in its header, which no value descent can read — a miss the check structurally cannot report (a cons cell, whose two words are readable, is the shape it can).
The commit installs the destination as `eval_heap`, `reset_to(mark)`s the old current region (dropping it when `mark == 0`, else pushing it onto `eval_heap_retired`), removes every retired candidate descending, rebuilds the select registrations' `(fd, handle)` index (`PortRegistry::rekey_select_handles`), advances the pass's cursor and stops the statement window (a mark is an offset into the region it was taken in).
`Runtime::references`, `Timers::refs` and `TimerWakes::deliver` need no re-keying for a structural reason: each key is minted on a **pooled BIF heap**, a different region family from the store, so a pooled-BIF-heap word can never be one of the move's keys.
A `[BinRef][ErlSubBits]` pair moves as an ordinary block — the relocator classifies the `BinRef` as a leaf and `BinRef::val` is not a heap word — so the payload pointer is carried verbatim and the destination is covered by `Runtime::for_each_off_heap_region`; a move can neither orphan nor over-release a payload.
### The decline lists
The move's list, headed by `R_PIN`: it declines whenever the statement window is **lifetime-pinned** (`scratch::pin`/`PinKind::Lifetime`, latched by `Runtime::stabilize_term`/`stabilize_term_with` and `src/port/completion.rs::decode_caller_tag`), at **both** sites — such a store may name a word in a region a move would evacuate and has no writable slot, so doubt is a refusal.
That refusal is **empirically necessary, not conservative-by-accident**: with the pin check bypassed the post-move verification HITS on `cerl_clauses` and the compile then fails with `system_limit`.
The other `R_*` constants are `R_SWITCH` (the move bit is off), `R_REASON` (a recorded raise's live reason word stands inside a candidate range), `R_DEST_ALLOC`/`R_DEST_CEILING`, `R_CAPACITY` (the `Relocator::new_checked` preflight, refused **before** the first word moves), `R_COPY`/`R_SCAN`, `R_ENUMERATION` and `R_VERIFY_HIT`.
The whole-region pass keeps its own list on `reclaim_declines`/`reclaim_last_decline`, decided by `refusal` and the walk: the switch off, no region-reclaim window, the caller's `withheld` reason, `Runtime::in_machine` set, no statement window, a window pin **at the boundary only** (of either kind — at the exit the corpus is complete, so no pin declines the drop), the walk's own `Walk::reason`, a refused arena rewind, a refused store reset, and an incomplete root enumeration.
Doubt is always a refusal: a walk that cannot finish (visit budget, unknown boxed header), a machine in flight, a refused rewind — each leaves both regions exactly as they were.
A **recorded raise is not doubt**: its live reason is pushed into the value root set, so its region is retained by the same descent as any other live word.
### Counters and the switch
`Runtime::region_reclaim_stats` publishes `RegionReclaimStats`: `store_compactions`, `store_words_reclaimed`, `store_regions_dropped`, `store_move_declines`, `store_move_last_decline`, `store_move_hit` (with the `store_move_hit_family`/`store_move_hit_slot` accessors), `arena_reclaims`, `arena_words_reclaimed`, `arena_regions_dropped`, `reclaim_declines`, `reclaim_last_decline` and `reclaim_last_decline_header`.
Every field is a monotone counter of decisions taken; none is a sampled figure and none is adjusted by reading it.
`reclaim_last_decline_header` carries the **raw header word** when the most recent refusal was the walk's unknown-boxed-header verdict (`Walk::UnknownHeader`, `../../eval/reclaim.rs`); `decline()` clears it so a later refusal cannot leave a stale header beside its own reason, and the field is where the pass's own descent and the move's post-move verification meet while each keeps its own reason pair.
`RegionReclaimConfig` has three independent bits (`store`, `store_move`, `arena`), all on by default, latched in `Runtime::new` from `BEAM_RT_REGION_RECLAIM` (`all`/`store`/`store-whole`/`arena`/`nomove`/`off`, anything unrecognised meaning the default) and settable with `Runtime::set_region_reclaim_enabled`; the latched form is readable through `Runtime::region_reclaim_enabled()` so a pin asserting a post-reclaim shape reads the state instead of assuming the default.
**The switch can only disable an action, never a guard**: every decline is decided independently of it, and it changes no rooting, no liveness and no reclamation-timing decision anywhere else.

## The measured record
### Where the retention is, and what the pass costs
`src/runtime/arena_census.rs` answers *which* requests produce `RetentionStats::arena_words`, switched on by `BEAM_RT_ARENA_TRACE` only (latched into `Runtime::arena_census` at `Runtime::new`, so with the variable unset `Runtime::alloc_words` pays one `bool` test and `Runtime::arena_census_report` is `None`).
It folds a power-of-two size histogram, the ten largest single requests with a `Backtrace::force_capture` each, a non-overlapping inside/outside-**nested-run-window** word split, and a per-window word-delta histogram.
The state is a thread-local (`arena_census::STATE`) and the `Window` guard (`Window::enter`, closed by `Drop`) records only the depth and the entry mark, so it never borrows the runtime; `crate::cli::run` prints the report as `[rt-arena]` lines at the retention line's own point.
Release builds keep line tables (`[profile.release] debug = 1`), the census drops its own frames and `TOP_FRAMES = 12` remain per request.
Measured on a tree-compiler `inet_parse` compile (~23 s, peak `VmHWM` 8 222 904 kB): `arena_words=1 328 040 786` over 7 047 134 calls, mean 188.5 words/call, with 70.13 % of the calls carrying 0.94 % of the words and 69.67 % of the words riding in 101 367 requests of 4 097–16 384 words (mean 11 442).
Its window stats: `opened=350 135 outermost=2 537 inside_words=1 328 038 935 (100.00 %) outside_words=1 851`, and one window's delta is 1 328 036 719 words (77.74 % of the summed deltas) — the compile is a single outermost nested run whose inner windows average ~2 074 words.
Control on `cerl_clauses.erl` (~1.2 s, peak 409 140 kB): `arena_words=5 133 613` over 414 946 calls, mean 12.4, with `513..=1024` at 28.84 %, `17..=32` at 19.02 % and `33..=64` at 14.06 %; `opened=24 556 outermost=2 537 inside_words=5 131 750 (99.96 %)`, again one window of 5 129 534 words.
Both modules' ten largest requests are the same site — `families::maps::write::put_map_assoc` → `terms::map::make_map_rooted` → `terms::slack::reserve` → `Machine::alloc_rooted` → `RuntimeContext::alloc_words_rooted` → `Runtime::alloc_words` — the largest a single 58 293-word (466 kB) / 3 168-word (25 kB) map reservation, and `inet_parse`'s retained top ten descend by exactly 21 words per step (one growing map rebuilt with one pair fewer).
So the demand is the engine's ordinary term-build reservation on the route with no process heap, and the census observes only — it implies no allocation-policy change.
**Where the A.9 compile peak's RSS actually lives.** The same route peaks at `VmHWM` 7.81 GB (7 811/7 813/7 813 MB over three runs, ~17 s, `VmSize` 21.16 GB), and `mallinfo2()` makes glibc **not** the consumer: peak in-use 0.185 GB / system 0.187 GB, flat from ~1.4 s, ≤ 2.5 % of the peak.
The remainder is this crate's own `mmap` carriers, split by mapping as the `-eval` store's retired region chain ≈ **4.6 GB** + arena written pages ≈ **2.85 GB** + pooled BIF heap (1.2 MB written of 169 MB reserved) + JIT code / file-backed / stacks ≈ **0.12 GB**.
The store is identified by shape and census agreement: 19 mappings of exactly 256 MiB (18) and 128 MiB (1) with `Rss ≥ 90 %` of `Size`, against `eval_heap_retired_regions=19` / `eval_heap_retired_words=620 756 992` (4.63 GB) — and a region is retired only once `grow_eval_heap` ran out of room in it, so it is ~fully written (the current 256 MiB region is the partially written one).
The arena is the opposite signature: the doubling chain 8192/4096/2048/1024/512/256/128/64/32/16 MB at `Rss ≪ Size` (8192→499 MB, 4096→1114 MB, 2048→666 MB, 1024→306 MB, 512→148 MB), i.e. 16.18 GB of reservation carrying 2.85 GB of written pages.
So `arena_words` is an **address-space upper bound** whose looseness is reservation, not residence, and the peak is 60 % `-eval` store / 37 % arena / 2.4 % glibc / 1 % other.
Both per-mapping figures are what the run **reached before the exit-site pass ran**; neither is a permanent floor.
Method (both probes external — no runtime code took part): `/proc/<pid>/smaps` snapshotted at RSS thresholds with every anonymous VMA classified by content read from `/proc/<pid>/mem` (a `beam_alloc` mseg segment by the header magic `0x4D53454700000001` at `payload − 64`, scanning the first 512 KiB because a super-aligned payload can sit far past its mapping base; a glibc non-main arena by `heap_info.size == 0x4000000` at the 64 MiB-aligned base; a glibc mmap chunk by the `IS_MMAPPED` size word), plus an `LD_PRELOAD` shim sampling `mallinfo2()` every 20 ms that writes one `<SHIM_OUT>.<pid>` file per process (`beam_rt` forks a child that would otherwise overwrite the parent's figures).

### A/B: the pass on a **two-statement** `-eval`
A single-statement `-eval` cannot show the pass's win — its peak is reached before either site runs — so two compiles in ONE expression put a statement boundary between them.
Release build, `BEAM_RT_MEM_TRACE=1`, three `-pa` flags written separately and relative to the repo root, before the `-eval`; peak `VmHWM` polled from `/proc/<pid>/status` every 20 ms; the two-statement form is `A = compile:file(...), B = compile:file(...), ok.`

| workload | pass | peak `VmHWM` | wall |
|---|---|---|---|
| bare `-eval 'halt(0).'` | on / off | 118 776 / 118 596 kB | 0.5 / 0.5 s |
| `inet_parse` (1 statement) | on / off | 8 197 644 / 8 195 536 kB | 17.2 / 17.2 s |
| `inet_parse` (**2 statements**) | on | **13 109 804 kB** | 33.3 s |
| `inet_parse` (**2 statements**) | off | **16 175 512 kB** | 33.3 s |
| `cerl_clauses` (1 statement) | on / off | 382 688 / 382 484 kB | 0.9 / 1.1 s |
| `cerl_clauses` (**2 statements**) | on / off | 520 312 / 554 088 kB | 1.4 / 1.5 s |

The `off` column is `BEAM_RT_REGION_RECLAIM=off` on the same binary; the bare-boot row's `+0.2 %` is noise.
Three separate 1-statement `inet_parse` runs read 8 195 536 / 8 195 764 / 8 197 644 kB — stable to 0.05 % — so a disagreement of a few hundred MB between two runs of the same commit is a property of the host: re-measure rather than trust a lone figure.
The pass is therefore worth **−3.07 GB (−19.0 %)** on the two-statement `inet_parse` run and **−33.8 MB (−6.1 %)** on `cerl_clauses`, and **nothing measurable** on a single statement — what the two-site design implies.
The win there is the **arena rewind**, not the store: `arena_reclaims=1`, `arena_words_reclaimed=1 328 035 267`, `arena_regions_dropped=11` (`inet_parse`) / 5 129 490 and 3 (`cerl_clauses`), while `store_compactions=0` and `store_regions_dropped=0`, so the store chain stays **additive** (`eval_heap_retired_regions` **38** with the pass on against **37** with it off, because the refused move keeps its destination on top of every source).
On the single-statement run the whole-region half does the work: `store_words_reclaimed=603 193 147`, `store_regions_dropped=18`, `eval_heap_retired_regions` **19 → 1**.
The pass releases **address space** too: on the same two-statement case peak `VmSize` reads **43 996 436 kB with the pass off against 27 415 256 kB with it on** (−16.6 GB, −37.7 %) — the whole-region drops plus the arena rewind releasing their carriers' reservations, and **not** the destination, since no move happened and a capacity-sized destination releases nothing of its own.
The single-statement case's peak is unchanged in **both** dimensions because it is reached before either site runs.
Residual, stated honestly: the two-statement run ends with **one more** retired region than the pass-off run, because a refused move retains its destination; a run retains a region for one of two measured reasons — `R_PIN` on the single-statement route, `R_VERIFY_HIT` on the two-statement one.
**Which rows are re-measurable today.** The small workloads reproduce (bare boot 113 MB, `cerl_clauses` one statement 264 MB, eight compiles in one comprehensible statement 277 MB) against a pre-pass build, with the pass inside ±4 MB on each.
The **`inet_parse` rows are not reproducible on a shared host**: with `code:which(compile)` verified in the same `-eval`, an `inet_parse.erl` compile through this route did not finish inside 10 min in either mode (`no_copt` not inside 4 min); `lib/stdlib/src/io_lib_fread.erl` (16.6 KB) is the largest source that completes, at ~125 s / 312 MB.
Compile wall time per source is wildly workload-dependent here (`cerl_clauses` 13.8 KB → ~5 s, `io_lib_fread` 16.6 KB → ~125 s, `inet_parse` 37 KB → >10 min), so a row is comparable only against a row of the same source on the same host; the **counters** (`arena_words`, `eval_heap_retired_regions`, `store_*`, `arena_*`) are the host-independent evidence.
The `-pa` paths are load-bearing: `erts_rust/bootstrap/lib/…` does not exist, and a wrong `-pa` path is **not** an error — it is silently ignored, the compile runs under the **system** compiler, and roughly every figure halves (`inet_parse` 1 statement 8 197 680 → 5 260 252 kB; 2 statements 13 167 032 → 7 598 340 kB, off 16 174 884 → 10 297 484 kB; `cerl_clauses` 382 764 → 308 500 kB).
So a figure from this section is valid only once `code:which(compile)` names `bootstrap/lib/compiler/ebin/compile.beam` — print it in the same `-eval` rather than assuming it.
### What refuses the move — a parked machine's used extent, not a dictionary slot
`store_move_hit` names `Family(ProcessDict, 1)` on the two-statement workload, and **that attribution is a measurement artifact**: `attribute_hit` reports the first corpus candidate whose descent records a range hit, while the `BEAM_RT_STORE_TRACE=1` diagnostic re-descends every slot and shows that the dictionary slot's own graph names **no** word inside a candidate range.
What actually refuses is the walk meeting an unparseable word reachable from exactly **two** corpus slots — `RootFamily::ParkedMachine[0]` and `[8]`, both holding the same word (**2** of 4 039 slots).
`RunnerState::for_each_engine_census` names the holder's state: **31 engines, 31 in a park window** — `parked = Some(..)`, `parked_published = false`, `live_x = 12`, `stack = 9`, `fr = 0`, `roots = 21` — the idle blocked set, whose machines the corpus over-approximates and walks in full, and the refusing words are `#Ref`-shaped residue at the **edge of the used extent** whose address a released region once occupied.
The corpus **over-approximates** a parked machine (the live `x` prefix, the whole Erlang stack, the FR prefix — **no frame liveness map**), so a dead word in that extent coinciding with a candidate range refuses the move for the whole pass; that is the Phase-2 blocker in measured form, and it is not a missing writable slot, because a parked machine's roots *are* in the corpus and *are* forwarded (`RunnerState::for_each_parked_machine_root_mut`).
An "any engine is parked ⇒ decline the move" gate is **not** the fix and was reverted: every engine is parked at a statement boundary, so it made `store_compactions` structurally unreachable while buying no soundness the post-move verification does not already give — that verification is the gate, and its refusal outcome is `MoveOutcome::KeptBoth`.
The **unknown boxed header** refusal is the same shape rather than an untaught subtag: `reclaim_last_decline_header & TAG_HEADER_MASK` is `0x0a`/`0x2a`, and `0x2a & 0b11 == 0b10` is `TAG_PRIMARY_BOXED` — a boxed **term** word where an object header belongs, reachable from the same released-region residue — and no legal `beam_term::tag::BoxedTag` is ≡ 2 (mod 4), so no child enumeration can be taught for it: dereferencing further would treat a pointer as an object, a memory-safety defect rather than an approximation.
`Walk::UnknownHeader` therefore carries the header **and** the leading term word so the finding stays diagnosable.
### Artifact identity, and the Phase-2 blocker
`compile:file(Src,[no_spawn_compiler_process,deterministic,no_docs,{outdir,…}])` under this runtime with the **system** compiler is byte-identical to `erl -noshell -eval` of the same call for both sources — md5 `3d63fbbf42aaed6d4f6e1aef82ba3a64` (`inet_parse`) and `94cb59a617a3ec1afda0750118dde54b` (`cerl_clauses`), all 14 chunks including `Dbgi`.
The **tree**-compiler route has no comparable oracle artifact (the system emulator cannot load the OTP-30 compiler: `Use of opcode 193; this emulator supports only up to 191`), so the guard test `a_real_stdlib_module_compiles_in_process_like_the_oracle` covers that route — green with the move **on** and with `BEAM_RT_REGION_RECLAIM=nomove`.
Both mechanisms are non-running-site mechanisms, so the single-compile **peak** is not bounded by them, and each candidate in-run point is blocked (itemised in `../../eval/CONTEXT.md` *Phase 2*):
- **POINT A, the nested-run exit** (`../../context/pump.rs`, `run_export_blocking`/`run_label_blocking`, inside `with_relocation_exempt`): the current machine is a `&mut` local only between `machine_pool::acquire` and `machine_pool::release`, `RuntimeContext` holds no machine field, and `Runtime::in_machine` is one position slot — so a parent machine's roots are reachable from nowhere.
- **POINT B, the store-growth point** (`grow_eval_heap` / `ContextHeap::alloc`): no machine, no stable slots, and for a move provably too late (the words it retires already hold the live ones).
- **POINT C, the engine heap-admission safepoint** (`../../context/gc.rs`): the machine's roots arrive as arguments and `ensure_heap` returns `Ok(())` at `armed == false`, which is every nested machine.
The binding holder is the **in-flight machine stack together with the evaluator's Rust-frame root set** (the `Env` is a `HashMap<String, Eterm>` whose other terms are locals in another frame), no `&mut` reachability exists for either at any of the three points, and a raise's live reason has no mutable accessor in `beam_common` — so the exile window (`with_relocation_exempt`) stays.
### What the pass does *not* cost
A peak-RSS sweep on the single-compile `-eval` route (one statement compiling `lib/stdlib/src/io_lib_fread.erl` in process, ~125 s, `/proc/<pid>/status` every 20 ms) pins both the actions and the accounting:

| `BEAM_RT_REGION_RECLAIM` | peak VmHWM | peak VmSize |
|---|---|---|
| `off` | 319 952 kB | 995 052 kB |
| `arena` | 319 696 kB | 995 052 kB |
| `nomove` | 319 468 kB | 993 616 kB |
| `store-whole` | 320 048 kB | 993 616 kB |
| `store` | 319 564 kB | 995 632 kB |
| `all` (default) | 319 516 kB | 993 908 kB |
| pre-window binary (no pass at all) | 290 668 kB | 1 019 892 kB |

The whole spread across the six modes is **580 kB**: no action of this pass accounts for the ~29 MB gap and the switch cannot recover it.
The exit `[rt-mem]` line agrees — `store_compactions=0`, `store_regions_dropped=0`, `store_move_declines=0`, `eval_heap_retired_regions=0`, `eval_heap_words=16777216` (the seed's *capacity*, unchanged at boot and exit), `arena_reclaims=2` releasing 275–285 words.
The store move does not even *attempt* there rather than declining: its threshold refuses to install a fresh region without releasing one, and the window opens on a store whose current region already has a non-zero below-cursor prefix and whose retired chain inside the window is empty — so no destination is built, and a declined move's residual (the pages its copy wrote, never its capacity) is not part of that route's peak either.
Two facts a measurement of this pass must keep in mind: `RetentionStats::eval_heap_words` is `Heap::capacity()`, **blind to `used()`** — the written prefix, which is what costs RSS, is read through `Runtime::eval_region_stats()` (`EvalRegionStats { used, capacity, retired_regions, retired_capacity_words, opens, resets, declines, last_decline, words_reclaimed, reclaim }`) — and `RetentionStats::arena_words` is **cumulative demand**, so a rewind does not lower it (the arena's live figure is `beam_engine::ContextHeapStats::live_words`).

## Constraints
- Return-based errors and no silent swallowing (`../../CONTEXT.md` §3): every refusal is a recorded `&'static str`, never a partial action; no `panic!`/`unwrap()` on runtime data.
- `unsafe` is confined to the two explicitly-marked modules — `compact.rs` (the `beam_gc` `FromSet`/`LinearDest` primitive construction, where the caller's own completeness claim is the safety contract) and `holder.rs` (the one `beam_term::layout::for_each_child_slot` call) — each site carrying its `// SAFETY:` rationale; `reclaim.rs` and `tests.rs` are safe Rust.
- **A refusal must never leave a dangling word**: the only irreversible act is dropping a source region, and the post-move verification gates exactly that; every other refusal leaves both regions byte-identical.
- **The switch may only disable an action, never a guard**, and no behaviour outside the two sites may depend on the pass.
- `BEAM_RT_STORE_TRACE` (`./holder.rs`, prefix `[rt-store]`) and the `[rt-mem]` line are diagnostics: nothing depends on them, and every entry point returns on its first cached-`OnceLock` test when the variable is unset.
- Files target ~1000 lines: `compact.rs` 876, `holder.rs` 781, `tests.rs` 1 273 (a single test-only suite, so the target does not bind it), `reclaim.rs` 1 017.

## Test Strategy
`./tests.rs` is the crate's region-reclaim suite (**11** `#[test]`s) and drives the **safe** doors only — `beam_alloc::Heap::cons` to write onto the store, `Runtime::alloc_words` for the arena, and an address comparison over `Runtime::for_each_off_heap_region` to read a word back (never a dereference), so a wrongly dropped region shows up as a *missing* address rather than a use-after-free.
What each class pins:
- `a_live_value_in_a_retired_region_is_retained_while_a_dead_one_is_dropped` — the whole-region drop on evidence, and the retained region's word still readable.
- `the_switch_off_leaves_the_retired_chain_alone` — `RegionReclaimConfig::OFF` records the switch decline and moves nothing.
- `every_decline_records_its_reason_and_touches_nothing` — the decline list one case per runtime (each decline is independent, so a shared runtime would hide one).
- `the_arena_rewind_fires_when_nothing_reachable_stands_above_the_mark_and_is_declined_when_something_does` — both sides of Tier A.
- `the_current_region_is_reset_to_the_windows_mark_and_a_rooted_word_blocks_it` — the `reset_to(cursor)` half.
- `the_move_collapses_several_partially_live_regions_into_one_and_every_value_reads_back` and `the_move_off_leaves_a_partially_live_region_whole_and_the_move_on_collapses_it` — the interleaved shape, the move's thresholds and the on/off contrast.
- `a_move_keeps_the_stores_capacity_and_the_headroom_the_growth_policy_gave_it` — the destination is sized by the growth policy, not the live set (a live-set-sized destination is four orders of magnitude smaller and the next `stabilize_term` would fail with `system_limit`).
- `a_forced_verification_hit_keeps_every_region_and_the_next_pass_classifies_them_on_evidence` — `MoveOutcome::KeptBoth` via `compact::tripwire`, plus the counters.
- `the_diagnostic_classifies_a_word_against_the_candidate_ranges_it_stands_in` — `holder::locate`'s classification both ways (a fixed address check, so the process-global stderr channel stays out of it).
- `a_refc_binary_value_survives_the_exit_site_move_byte_exactly` — a 4 096-byte off-heap payload read back byte-for-byte through `beam_term::refc::refc_bytes`, with `store_compactions == 1` proving the move ran.
Cross-node: `../../eval/scratch.rs` owns the statement window whose mark is the pass's floor (its own latch, `PinKind`, `MIN_WINDOW_WORDS` and reset reasons), and `../../runner/CONTEXT.md` owns the parked-machine root family, the engine census and the `RunnerState` that carries a recorded raise's live reason.
Run the suite with `erts_rust/scripts/cargo.sh test --manifest-path erts_rust/Cargo.toml -p beam_rt reclaim` (a bare `cargo` from the repo root cannot resolve the workspace manifest; the sandbox `$HOME` is read-only, hence the wrapper).

## Known Issues
- **A stale doc reference in `reclaim.rs`'s module docs.** The *completeness precondition* item 3 describes a `note_lifetime_store` that "registers the region with this pass" (no reset for the rest of its life as the current region, and every region retired from then on excluded from the candidates, both lifted by the next growth). No such function exists in the crate: what actually exists is `WindowState::pinned` declining the whole-region pass **at the boundary** and `WindowState::lifetime_pinned` declining the move (`R_PIN`) at both sites.
- **A store region can be retained for a reason the counters name only indirectly** — `R_PIN` (single-statement route) and `R_VERIFY_HIT` (two-statement route) are the two measured ones; `store_move_hit`'s `Family(...)` attribution can be a measurement artifact while the real refusal is a `ParkedMachine` slot whose extent the corpus cannot prove dead (see *What refuses the move*).
- **`kept_both` retains the destination's capacity**, so a refused move is additive in address space until a later pass reclassifies it.
- **Both mechanisms are non-running-site mechanisms**: an in-run peak is not bounded by them, and the Phase-2 points are blocked (see *Artifact identity, and the Phase-2 blocker*).
