# `beam_rt::context::gc` — measurements

## Intent
Every measured figure behind [`CONTEXT.md`](CONTEXT.md)'s conclusions, with the recipe that re-derives it.
`CONTEXT.md` states what is true; this file states how it was priced.
Measurement discipline (instructions on a pinned P-core, paired and alternating rounds, a from-source rebuild as the control, wall clock and sample shares as different units) is [`../../../STARTUP_COST.md`](../../../STARTUP_COST.md) *Measurement discipline*.
No test in this workspace compares any figure here against the oracle.

## The allocation doors and the bump fast arm
- `bench_gc_bound:churn/1 20000`, `taskset -c 2 perf stat -e instructions:u`, `cpu_core/instructions` only (this hybrid CPU emits two PMU instances): control **26.447 G**, new **26.015 G** = **−1.63 %** raw, **−1.82 %** on the marginal (`N=20000` minus `N=0` ≈ 2.68 G), spread 0.04 % over 3 alternating reps.
  A rebuild of the control from its own source is bit-identical, so there is no two-build spread to subtract.
- Of that delta: the door split alone ≈ −0.46 %, the constant-length `plain_bump` fill ≈ −0.8 %, the inlined forced-request peek ≈ −0.4 %.
- `gc_process_enabled` runs **46 979** times from this module per `churn` run, where consulting the valves per rooted allocation makes it 31 052 161 — and it takes no profile samples (1.08 % of self time in that shape), so the read is off the per-term path.
- The admission count is unchanged at 15.5 M `ensure_heap_full` + 15.5 M `alloc_words_rooted` (mean 2 words per rooted allocation), and the fast arm serves **99.9999 %** of them.
- The run retires **6.15 %** fewer instructions and its paired wall-clock median over 5 alternating rounds is **5.27 %** lower (band 0.943–0.974).
- Foreign workloads: instructions retired **2.78 %** lower on `emqx_base62:decode`, **0.13 %** lower on `cowboy_bstr:to_lower` and `capitalize_token`.
- **The collection cadence is unchanged**: `churn` at N = 20 000 reports `erlang:statistics(garbage_collection)` `{48013, 37294061, 0}` on both binaries, and the `BEAM_RT_PERF_COUNTERS=1` per-key dispatch table is identical key for key.
- The hot/cold split costs one extra `ProcessTable::get_mut` on a path taken once per ~331 admissions — not measurable — and the gate order is verdict-identical because every continuation re-read is a pure read of the same instant.
- `plain_bump`'s fill is a constant-length slice for `n ∈ {1,2,3}`: the runtime-length copy lowered to a **GOT-indirect `memcpy@GLIBC` call** on the per-cons-cell (`n == 2`) path, which `perf annotate` attributed **1.95 %** of a whole `churn` run to (`non_null.rs:1650`).
- The forced-request peek stays inlined: an `#[inline(never)] fn forced_gc_request` costs **26.22 G against 26.01 G** (+0.78 %), because `gc_request_pending` is one `is_empty` load after inlining and a call is dearer than the spills it avoids.
- `#[inline(always)]` on the three `ExecutionContext` heap-door impls was rejected: **26.04 G against 26.01 G** (+0.1 %) — the bloat in `runner/budget.rs`'s impl outweighs the trampoline it removes. Plain `#[inline]` (a 6-instruction trampoline) ships.
- **Remaining per-admission cost, by owner** (all outside this node, each a report rather than a fix):
  - `beam_alloc::Heap::tuple` → `bump::tuple_at` is ~27 instructions of the reservation (1.63 % of the `churn` run); a safe `Heap` primitive writing the header and placeholders and returning the base without a `Result<Eterm>` would bound it.
  - `ProcessTable::get_mut` is probed twice per admission (`imul $0x23e0` + serial + state test, ~8–10 instructions each); the raw-pointer memo that would halve it needs an eleventh `unsafe` exemption and is refused.
  - The doors' own 7-push/pop frame (~14 instructions) stays because the inlined `gc_request_pending` puts a SipHash `contains` in the same body; splitting `../runtime/gc.rs::gc_request_pending` into a cheap `is_empty` inline plus an outlined `contains` would remove it (≈0.8 % bound), but that file is a sibling and must not be edited from here.

## The unscanned-root valve — cost, census and aggregate widths
- `BEAM_RT_VALVE_TRACE=1` is a release-mode census, silent and free when unset: one `OnceLock<bool>` read hoisted per walk into `Walk::traced`/`Visited::traced` and threaded down as a `bool`, so no per-word path probes the environment.
  It prints one `[rt-valve]` block on stderr at driver-thread exit: per-door call/descent/counts, a per-pid block (admissions, needs, capacities, chain/available/occupancy/debt, grew-vs-collapse-only, words copied and reclaimed, descents ÷ collections), the `Visited` insert/revisit/probe/grow figures, the seeded root words by family and the tier1/tier2 split. It never changes a verdict.
- Measured on the **minimal run** (`beam_rt -noshell -eval 'halt().'`, whose whole cost is the boot drive; `-version` drives no boot script and reaches 0 calls):
  - A descent is paid only where a collection can follow: admissions are exactly **1:1** with collections, and the walks are issued by `EnsureHeapFull`, `CollectionGate` and `RedsBoundary`; `EnsureHeap`, `AllocWordsRooted` and both prunes issue **none**.
  - The cost is the descent, not the seeding: the root families seed a few tens of thousands of words (**96.9 %** of them registers) where a single descent walks thousands of objects.
  - Boot trigger mix: of **384** collections **145 are occupancy-driven and 239 `collection_debt`-driven**, **225 of 384** leave the nursery capacity unchanged, the mean free-word figure at admission is **1 963**, and `<0.10.0>` owns **355** of the 384. The full per-door and per-pid block is [`../../../STARTUP_COST.md`](../../../STARTUP_COST.md) *Boot collection accounting*.
- An ablation turning the descent off (`descend = false`) measures **−26 %** of retired instructions; three verdict-neutral landings (the mature-first ordered exact test, the hoisted `Geometry`, moving `Visited`'s room-making path out of the insert hot path) took the run **−3.17 %** (paired 5 rounds, spread 0.14 pp, noise floor ±0.1 %).
- Sampled self time for this node on the new binary is **≈17.5 %**, of which the walk proper is `Visited::insert` 8.6 / `Walk::seed` 4.0 / `Walk::run` 2.0; `word_region` no longer reaches the 0.5 % line. Quote the instruction delta, not the sample share.
- `Walk::seed`'s 4.0 % share is not seeding — it is `Walk::expand` inlined at the seed loop's call site. An ablation of the seed loop's per-word classification (`#[inline(never)]` duplicate behind `black_box`) sizes the honest phase-1 work at **≤1.36 M instructions = 0.069 %** of the run, ~14× below the noise floor. Machine roots are 18 741 words of a 40 833-word phase-1 total. Do not optimise the seed loop.
- `Visited::insert` is memory-bound, not instruction-bound: `perf annotate` puts **59 %** of its samples on the single slot-array load of a random probe into the 16-byte-slot table, at **1.045 probes/insert**. Right-sizing the table back down was measured and rejected — it halved cache traffic but raised probes/insert 1.42→1.69, costing ~9 % more cycles end to end.
- `tier2_share` on the boot workload is **0.968** (1 847 171 of 1 909 104 classified container words fall through to the exact tests, and 1 847 133 of those are found *scanned*), which is why the mature-first order and the single-space shortcut shape the exact half.
- On the in-process compile path the descent costs **~30×** the collector: `perf record` over a `string.erl` compile puts `gc_walk::Visited::insert` 1.65 % + `Walk::seed` 0.25 % + `Walk::run` 0.12 % ≈ **2 %** of cycles against the copier's **0.06 %**.
- `perf record` on 24× in-process `compile:file("lib/stdlib/src/ordsets.erl")` (`taskset -c 3`): `Nursery::contains` self time **0.043 %** of samples (the exact tests the reject removes are 0.06 points of it), `root_is_unscanned` **0.39 %**, `Walk::run` **0.44 %** — the skipped tests and the added bounds read are the same order, so the filter is neutral here.
  On a reference-minting `send`/`receive` loop at 200 000 iterations the filter is **0.3 % fewer instructions** and **0.5 % fewer branches**, and no sample moved a valve decision.
- **Aggregate widths** (`valve_bounds.rs::the_aggregate_span_is_reported_for_both_collector_kinds`, which prints and never asserts — the absolute width is allocator-placement fact, so the shape generalizes and the count does not): a fresh process spans on the order of **500** words on the copying strategy (233-word nursery + 233-word mature + the gap) and low thousands with `BEAM_RT_GC_STRATEGY=immix` (a 2048-word mark-region mature at a different offset); a growth that retires a nursery space widens both, and a collection resizes and relocates the space.
  A representative run printed 469 / 2284 fresh, 848 / 2662 grown, 3489 / 5304 collected (copying / immix). The interval is **not monotone**, which is why the bounds are re-read rather than cached.
- The valve's decisions are pinned unchanged: `valve_bounds.rs::the_two_tier_test_matches_the_exact_predicate_in_every_region` (against the pre-filter expression kept verbatim as the oracle) and `the_filter_does_not_change_a_collections_counters`.
  End to end, 8 in-process compiles of `ordsets.erl` report `erlang:statistics(garbage_collection)` `{63, 1268177, 0}` and `{heap_size, 233}`/`{total_heap_size, 466}` on both binaries.
- Per-walk memoisation across walks is unsound, not merely unmeasured: a heap address is reused as soon as its object is collected, so the generation-stamped reset is what makes reusing the table free.
- The four `../../runner/` collection/reduction pins belong to that node: their per-iteration prices are `beam_engine`'s charge stream (a same-module local call priced once per distinct callee per run from the engine's memoized local-call verdict), so a change there moves them and leaves every valve figure here untouched.

## The in-process `compile:file/2` path
Release profile, `beam_rt -noshell -eval` running `compile:file(Src,[no_spawn_compiler_process,deterministic,no_docs,{outdir,D}])`, each source compiled **twice** back to back: baseline, and with `+hms 20000000` (a floor large enough that no collection runs at all — `erlang:statistics(garbage_collection)` then reports `{0,0,0}`).

| source | baseline wall | `{collections, reclaimed, 0}` | collections above the boot floor | `+hms` wall | GC's wall share |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `lib/stdlib/src/ordsets.erl` (13 585 B) | 577 ms | `{562, 969 321, 0}` | 61 | 565 ms | ≤ 2.1 % |
| `lib/stdlib/src/maps.erl` (37 745 B) | 3 041 ms | `{612, 1 475 440, 0}` | 111 | 3 056 ms | ≈ 0 (the `+hms` row is slower) |
| `lib/stdlib/src/string.erl` (103 287 B) | 23 831 ms | `{852, 2 259 646, 0}` | 351 | 25 316 ms | ≈ 0 (the `+hms` row is slower) |

- The **boot floor is `{501, 713 680, 0}`** (an `-eval` that compiles nothing), so only 61/111/351 of the reported collections belong to the compile itself — the column is a run total, not a compile figure.
- The collector's own cycles are **0.06 %** of the run (`perf record -F 997`, 28 852 samples: `beam_gc::copy::Copier::forward_one` 0.01 %, `Nursery::contains` 0.02 % + 0.03 %; no `beam_gc` collect symbol reaches 0.01 %), and a pre-sized 20 M-word nursery costs page faults, zeroing and cache.
- "852 collections × a 175 000-word live set" overstates the copying: `GcStats::words_reclaimed` is survivor-adjusted, so a round's input is `words_copied + words_reclaimed`, and `collect_minor` ends in `Nursery::collapse` → `HeapPolicy::young_after_collection(capacity, stats.words_copied)` — the target is re-derived from the round's own young live set and converges.
- The large `erlang:memory(processes_used)` figure a compile ends on (376 KB for `string.erl`) is the mature **capacity** a fullsweep sized at `mature_target_words` (≈2 × the whole-heap live set), never a per-collection copy volume.
- The `-eval` body is an ordinary spawned child under `Runtime::default_spawn_policy()`, so `+hms`/`+hbm*`/`+hmax` do reach the process that runs the compile (`{0,0,0}` is that proof), and a small-floor A/B (`+hms` 10 000 / 100 000 / 1 000 000) moves the same compile by <2 %.
- **Where the compile's time actually is** (each owner another node — escalate there, do not fix here): `beam_engine::interpreter::build_label_map` **25.2 %** (the whole-module `LabelMap` cache misses on nearly every freshly compiled module), `<RuntimeContext>::call_registered` **14.0 %**, libc allocation churn **≈19 %** (`_int_malloc` 6.7 %, `_int_free_create_chunk` 3.6 %, `cfree` 2.8 %, `malloc` 2.3 %, `unlink_chunk` 2.1 %, `memset` 1.4 %), `beam_term::map::write_map` **6.7 %** plus its canonical-order `Vec` sort 3.2 %, `beam_term::compare::cmp_impl` **3.0 %**.
- **Recipe**: the two-run A/B *is* the measurement — `… -noshell -eval 'T0 = erlang:monotonic_time(millisecond), {ok,_} = compile:file(SRC, […, {outdir, D}]), io:format("~p ~p~n", [erlang:monotonic_time(millisecond) - T0, erlang:statistics(garbage_collection)]), init:stop().'` with and without `+hms 20000000`, then `perf record -F 997` + `perf report -i <data> --stdio -s symbol` for the cycle split. There is no in-tree collection tracer and none is needed.

## The exempt-window reach on the compile route
- The only space replacements firing inside the route's exempt windows are the two copy-out sites (`../calls.rs::hint_grow_gate` 58×, the post-refusal growth 12×).
- The **rooted** allocation seam's refusal is unreachable in every measured run of that route; the **unrooted** seam's is reached **exactly once per compile** (a 15-word request against a 233-word young space with 10 free, falling through to the arena).
- No collection runs inside an exempt window: 1 143 `ensure_heap_full` + 19 `reds_boundary` collections all report `exempt=false`.

## Why the boot's collection count is a shrink/grow oscillation
The minimal run takes **384 collections, 100 % of them on the scheduled route** (the `-eval`/`-version` routes are structurally inert — table-less contexts never reach a door).
- Every `[rt-heap] copy-out grow` firing is `<0.10.0>`'s with `chain_spaces=1`: `hint_grow_gate` grows before a copy-out that would not fit, and `Process::grow_nursery` **retires** the replaced space, so `collection_debt()` becomes true.
- The next admission door collects; `Nursery::collapse` → `HeapPolicy::young_after_collection` keeps a capacity inside its band, and the growth table re-rounds each replacement space. One boot's observed ladder is `2 586 → 376 → 986 → 376 → 986` — an oscillation, not a working set — and **355** turns of the loop run on one pid.
- `beam_gc` owns the fix (see [`CONTEXT.md`](CONTEXT.md) *What the doors do NOT decide* for why no `beam_rt`-side lever is sound). The landed C floor (`MINOR_SHRINK_FLOOR_WORDS = 3 000`) trims the count by **−1.2 %** of the run ([`../../../STARTUP_COST.md`](../../../STARTUP_COST.md) §(b)).
- **The `+hms` surrogate sweep** (`taskset -c 2`, `cpu_core/instructions:u`, 5 alternating paired rounds per row, control spread ±0.15 %, new ±0.05 %, load 0.04–0.21; `VmHWM` from `RUSAGE_CHILDREN`) floors the same quantity the C rule preserves — the nursery target — through a different mechanism:

| `+hms` | collections | (debt-driven) | words copied | words reclaimed | instructions | Δ | `VmHWM` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| none (that round's control) | **427** | 262 | 128 316 | 661 006 | 1 980.5 M | — | 116.3 MB |
| 1 000 | **91** | 50 | 66 721 | 689 104 | 1 714.8 M | **−13.48 %** | 114.0 MB |
| 3 000 | **41** | 16 | 62 353 | 684 134 | 1 674.8 M | **−15.44 %** | 113.1 MB |
| 8 000 | **13** | 5 | 45 900 | 634 739 | 1 644.6 M | **−16.96 %** | 114.0 MB |

- The surrogate's **−13.5…−17 %** belongs to the raised minimum heap alone and is never the floor's own value. What it establishes is the direction: less copying (128 316 → 62 353 words), essentially unchanged reclamation and a slightly **lower** `VmHWM`, so less copying means less memory here. Cycles at 3 000: 736.9 M → 672.3 M (−8.8 %).
- The 1 000 row has the landed rule's dynamics (a grown capacity is **kept** — 2 586 words — because the gate moves to 8 000); the 3 000 row its ceiling.
- **Blast radius of the floor, by band**: a capacity ≤ 1 864 words was never shrinkable, so every figure below it stands — `233` (`../../runner/tests/slice_charging.rs`), `843` (`../../../tests/foreign_root_loop.rs`, `../../runner/tests.rs`'s `heap_growth` control), `2 586` (`../../runner/tests/reds_door.rs`), and the differential corpus' only by-value case (`{heap_size,233}`/`{total_heap_size,233}` for a fresh spawn).
  The widened band is **(1 864, 3 000]**, exactly where `../../runner/tests/boundary_cost.rs`'s `MEASURED_YOUNG_CAPACITY = 2 585` sits: that row still runs at `peak_young_capacity: 2_585` and the ratio-based `../tests.rs::the_copy_bound_ping_pong_grows_the_nursery_instead_of_declining_the_valve` is green.

## The `heap_growth` cadence (a door-independent fixed point)
`../../runner/tests.rs::the_heap_growth_workloads_per_call_counters_are_flat_in_n` is the pure engine-allocation shape and its cadence is `collections == 1 + N / K`, with `K` derived from the row's own measured peak capacity.
- The 401-word `test_heap` request fits the settled **843**-word nursery twice (`2 × 401 = 802`), so `K == 2` and the counts are **2 501 / 5 001 / 12 501 / 25 001 / 50 001** at N = 5 000 / 10 000 / 25 000 / 50 000 / 100 000, with `peak_young_spaces == 1`, `debt_boundaries == 0` and `major` **0** on the rows staying on 843 (the rows that take their one fullsweep settle on **987**, carrying the same `K == 2`).
- The `+ 1` is the collection the run takes before its first iteration — a fresh process starts on the collector's 233-word floor, smaller than a single 401-word request.
- Slice length is not what makes the cadence exact: a slice prices at most `4 000 / 3 = 1 333` iterations (`reductions == 3N + 5` plus one unit per pre-emption), and 1 333 is not a multiple of `K = 2`, so a slice end lands mid-cycle on roughly half the rows.
  What keeps it exact is the boundary door's **7/8 occupancy watermark**: 7/8 × 843 = 737.6 words cleared by the 802 two iterations fill and not by the 401 one fills, so 7/8 × 2 = 1.75 rounds up to the whole cycle — a boundary offered after one iteration declines, and one offered after the cycle's two is exactly the state in which the next admission request would have collected.
- The relation re-derives from each row instead of a quoted count, so a `beam_gc` sizing change moves the settled capacity, `K` and the expected count together.

## The `ets_copy` allocate-and-abandon rows (`../tests.rs`'s `gcab_*`)
`payload/1` builds a small map, `ets:insert/2` + `ets:lookup/2` validate it, one table and one key per process. The rows' magnitudes are the collector's, so they pin invariants rather than a count: `pinned == 0` on every row, and a collection count growing strictly with N — more than **4×** over the ~8× step for `gcab_free` and its float-literal twin `gcab_lit`, strictly more for the float-using `gcab_flt`.
- The literal-root control `gclit` (a constant payload folded into the module's literal area) is `gcab_free`'s discriminating twin: non-zero collections, strictly more at the larger N, `pinned == 0`, `fallbacks == 0`.
- The valve is **not** the cause of that cadence and neither is the per-slice FR exemption: the `gcab_flt` rows assert `fallbacks_at(ValveDeclined) == 0` and `ensure_heap_full.hits == 0` with `pinned == 0` throughout.
- Each row prints its own counts (`gc-ab <module>: N=… collections … major … pinned … fallbacks …`), so re-derive rather than quote: `erts_rust/scripts/cargo.sh test --manifest-path erts_rust/Cargo.toml -p beam_rt --lib -- the_post_slice_float_pin_plateaus_a_float_using_loops_collections --nocapture`; the literal twin is `a_tagged_literal_root_is_scanned_and_the_loop_collects`.

## Off-heap refc-binary envelopes (the reservoir arm)
- **At a park the reservoir is pruned whole**: the census reads `retained_by_live = 0` and `retained_by_shared = 0` at every park of every shape measured — a plain `<<0:(B*8)>>` build, `binary:copy/2`, `base64:encode/1`, `list_to_binary/1`, `binary:part/3`, a `<<Acc/binary, Chunk/binary>>` accumulator, an ETS-inserted-then-dead payload, a `lists:foldl` lambda — so `refcount() > 1` is a belt, not a working set.
- A park-reaching loop's peak is **one pre-emption window**, never a sum over parks: `erlang:memory(binary)` reads the same fixed figure at N = 20 000 as at N = 40 000 while the park count doubles (dense per-iteration sampling: identical `{min, max}`, identical last-park reading, identical `released` and `retained_by_live`).
- The window is a **count of iterations**, so its bytes are payload-size proportional: the same dense fixture (N = 20 000, 45 parks at every size) peaks at **475 968 / 930 624 / 1 839 936 / 29 119 296** bytes for 1 024/2 048/4 096/65 536-byte payloads — ≈444 payloads each time — against the oracle's **20 176 / 23 176 / 33 504 / 213 776** on the identical source, where the trigger *causes* a collection while here it only gates a prune.
- A pre-emption-reaching loop with a plain binary build is flat in N: `4 144` bytes at both N = 1 000 and N = 10 000 (4 096-byte payloads), `65 584` at both N with 65 536-byte ones — one surviving payload.
- In the measured loop shapes the bin-vheap budget is the *only* gate other than "pid not in the table" ever seen to decline: no shape tripped the collection valve, the prune-only valve or the walk.
- **A monotone ladder is a slice phase, not a leak.** With a 4 096-byte payload and a 1 000-iteration block: `0` at every rung when the block is `999` iterations, `k` payloads at rung `k` for `1 000`, `2k` for `1 001`, `3k` for `1 002`; a 2 000-rung ladder is hard-capped at **999 payloads** with a reset every 1 000 rungs, identically at 1 024/2 048/4 096 B. `registry::all_stats()`'s `AllocType::Binary` `alloc_calls - free_calls` equals the live payloads after every park.
  The two tests of a real leak: does the peak exceed one slice's worth (`DEFAULT_REDUCTIONS_PER_SLICE / this loop's reductions per iteration` payloads), and does it rise with the **rung count** at a fixed total iteration count.
- **The unbounded (never-pre-empting) shape**: `f(N) -> _ = <<0:(B*8)>>, f(N-1).` holds `1 000·k` dead payloads at rung `k` with no trough — 20 000 iterations of a 65 536-byte binary is **1.31 GB** of `binary_alloc` live bytes, exactly linear (measured `N × 4144` B at a 4 096-byte payload, `{0 → 82 880 000}` across N = 2 000 → 20 000, trough pinned at ~1 payload).
  The identical program under `BEAM_RT_TIER=interpreter` is a bounded sawtooth of ≤ 203 payloads with a 6-payload trough — the discriminator is the tier's charge granularity, not the prune.
- A BIF or `call_ext` in the same body does **not** break the chain (both only spend): a tail loop whose body is `_ = length(lists:seq(1, 20))` shows the target pid at **0 parks** at N = 2 000 and at N = 20 000 (the 70 parks in the run are the boot window's, constant in N) with `erlang:memory(binary)` = 0, where the interpreter tier parks 8 at N = 2 000 and 80 at N = 20 000.
- **A fun-loop body never reaches this arm**: each lambda application is a nested `run_label_blocking` machine whose reservoir is adopted into `Runtime::eval_off_heap` on drop, and that store is pruned at the nested epilogue — a converged capacity, measured `377 104` bytes at both N = 1 000 and N = 10 000 with 4 096-byte payloads and `393 504` at both with 65 536-byte ones, against `4 144 000` → `41 440 000` (exactly `N × (Bytes + 48)`) with the out-of-scope payloads retained.
  Witnesses: [`../../../tests/off_heap_reclaim.rs`](../../../tests/off_heap_reclaim.rs)'s `a_retained_off_heap_binary_survives_the_fun_loop_byte_exactly` (anti-dangle) and its green `the_fun_loop_envelope_is_bounded_by_the_bin_vheap_budget`, plus [`../../../tests/eval_off_heap_reclaim.rs`](../../../tests/eval_off_heap_reclaim.rs)'s nested anti-dangle case.
- The point's own per-slice price is one cached observation on a non-triggered bin vheap.

## The escaped store (`Runtime::eval_off_heap`)
Measured with [`../../../tests/eval_off_heap_reclaim.rs`](../../../tests/eval_off_heap_reclaim.rs): 20 evals × 6 throw-away `base64:encode(binary:copy(<<1>>, 40000))` builds (120 payloads) stay at **≤ 327 680 bytes / ≤ 5 handles**, sawtooth, with **23 prunes** and 0 declines; the same loop with the reclaim off (`Runtime::set_eval_prune_enabled(false)`) reaches **7 864 320 bytes / 120 handles**, exactly one per build.
- Sweeping the whole store at the nested epilogue instead of only its own adopted handles makes `lists:map/2` over 200 engine-built 40 000-byte binaries return **190 corrupted elements**, against 0 with the run-scoped sweep — the measurement behind the out-of-scope retention rule.
- The refuse-to-push-a-region-as-a-root rule has a measured shape: `base64:encode(binary:copy(<<1>>, 40000))`'s fourth `-eval` statement (the first whose summed payload bytes cross the budget) reads `0x01` payload bytes as `0x0101_0101_0101_0101`, low bits `TAG_PRIMARY_LIST`, which dereferences to a `SIGSEGV` in `walk`.

## The end-to-end effect on a real workload
`emqx_base62:encode/1` on the unmodified EMQX module: with the prune enabled `erlang:memory(binary)` is flat (~272 KB → ~315 KB across N, `VmHWM` ~120 MB at every N) where the unpruned configuration is a linear 1 072 B/call; ns/call ratio post/pre 0.885, post/oracle 27.4× — a capacity fix, not a throughput one. Figures and caveats: [`../../runner/MEASURED.md`](../../runner/MEASURED.md) *The off-heap reclamation end to end*.
