# `beam_rt::eval` — the `-eval` Erlang expression evaluator (`mod.rs` walker · `ast.rs` tree · `parse.rs` lexer/parser · `bits*.rs` binary segments · `compile.rs` escaped-closure compile · `scratch.rs` statement window · `reclaim.rs` store reclamation)
## Intent
The `-eval` evaluator (contract A.6): a lexer + Pratt parser over an untyped AST plus an `erl_eval`-style tree-walking evaluator, driven by `cli.rs::run`'s `Action::Eval(expr)` (boot, then `Runtime::eval` → `crate::eval::eval`, re-exported at the crate root and in `prelude`; `Runtime::eval` is also the crate's test expression/literal builder, `rt.eval("[call]")`).
The evaluator's closures escape **into loaded modules**: `RuntimeContext::apply_fun` delegates a fun stamped `EVAL_FUN_UNIQ` to `apply_eval_closure`, whose bodies live on `Runtime::eval_closures` (`Vec<Rc<FunBody>>`), so `lists:map(fun(X) -> X*X end, …)` applies one.
It owns **no** term layout, arithmetic or printer: everything is dispatched down (`beam_term`/`beam_engine::terms`/`beam_bifs`) and inherits the lower crates' exact `erl` semantics.
**A BIF's container result outlives the call.** With no scheduled process there is no process heap to copy a registered BIF's result onto, so the copy-out takes the table-less arm (`context/pool.rs::move_result_into_runtime_store`): the container is copied onto the pointer-stable `Runtime::eval_heap` (escaped payloads adopted into `Runtime::eval_off_heap`) and only then is the pooled BIF heap rewound to that call's mark — so `L = lists:seq(1,50), {hd(L), length(L)}` is valid for the run's life and repeated calls do not march the shared pool to its cap. Detail: `../context/CONTEXT.md`, `../context/IMPLEMENTATION.md#the-table-less-arm-and-its-store`; oracle cross-check `../../tests/eval_pool_result.rs`.
## The `-eval` route's term storage and machine ownership (cross-cutting seam)
The four term-lifetime rules, as they bind here:
- **A raw `Eterm` in a long-lived slot roots nothing** — the evaluator's `Env`, a cached closure's captures, a pending reply: each is stabilized onto a scanned region, or latched by a pin, before its owner can relocate.
- **A pointer-stable, never-scanned region is the wrong home for a container whose children live on a scanned young heap** — nothing here builds on a process young heap: every term lands in a `Runtime` field (below), whose children are its own.
- **An exempted move must "refuse ⇒ stabilize, never dangle"** — the whole driver runs under `ctx.with_relocation_exempt(..)`, so a delivery-driven collection of this pid may not move its heap; a door that cannot copy refuses instead.
- **An `Rc` lease across an allocation is a deep-copy or a dangle hazard, never a cheap borrow** — `Runtime::eval_closures` is `Rc`-cloned out before a closure is applied and split with `Rc::make_mut`, never borrowed across the application.
Machine ownership:
- **No `Machine` spans a `-eval` expression.** The `Evaluator` holds only `ctx: &'a mut RuntimeContext<'b>`, which `RuntimeContext::new` builds with `mailbox_table: None` and `gc_armed: false`; the only `Machine`s are the per-nested-call short-lived ones (`context/pump.rs::run_export_blocking`/`run_label_blocking`), whose payload handles are adopted into `eval_off_heap` before they drop.
- **Consequence:** nothing on this route collects or relocates by itself; reclamation is the two mechanisms at the two non-running sites (statement window + region-reclaim pass) plus the payload and resource prunes.
- **The four storage regions:** `arena` (`Runtime::arena` = `MinimalContext::ContextHeap` — every tuple/list/map/float/bignum and ≤64-byte binary; pointer-stable, rewound above the pass's mark); `bif_heaps` (pooled per-call BIF regions, depth-sized, rewound per call); `eval_heap` + `eval_heap_retired` (the store: `stabilize_*` copies, BIF container results, non-byte-aligned or >64-byte binaries; growth clamped at `MAX_EVAL_HEAP_WORDS`); `eval_off_heap` (`beam_message::OffHeapTable`, the off-heap payload owner).
- **A big binary literal is ON-heap:** `Evaluator::materialize_bits` sends a byte-aligned binary ≤64 bytes to the arena and everything else to `eval_heap`. All four regions are observable through `Runtime::retention_stats()` (`RetentionStats`, printed by `BEAM_RT_MEM_TRACE` as one `[rt-mem]` line — figures in `../PERF.md`).
- **`eval_off_heap` is grown by a scheduled `run`-route process too** (`run_export` is blocking-aware for every caller); "no per-pid figure" is about `process_info(P, binary)` attribution, not about who grows it.
- **The runner's park prune cannot be reused here:** it needs `&mut ProcessTable` + a `Process` + `&mut Machine`, and its gate hits the first pointer-bearing word in a `WordRegion::Foreign` region — which every eval-route root is; hence this node's region-agnostic descent (`reclaim.rs`).
- **`OffHeapTable` traps** (no per-entry removal, no de-duplication in `push_binary`, MSO-only `len`/`iter`/`overhead_bytes`, a `GrowOutcome::Moved` growth invalidating every `data_ptr`): `reclaim/CONTEXT.md`.
- **The one rewritable store is `eval_heap`** (`scratch::reset_to`), so any word held outside it must be enumerated at a boundary.
- Detail: `scratch/CONTEXT.md`, `reclaim/CONTEXT.md`, `../runtime/roots/CONTEXT.md` § *The `-eval` store*.
## The escaped off-heap payload store and its prune points (`reclaim.rs`, `reclaim/resource.rs`)
`Runtime::eval_off_heap` **owns** every off-heap `RefcBinary` payload a term on this route names, and `reclaim.rs` is its reclamation (`prune(runtime, extra)` walks the reachable payloads from an explicit root list and rewrites the store with the survivors).
- **The one payload call point** is `Evaluator::run_top_level` → `Evaluator::prune_escaped`, **between** the top-level statements — the only moment the evaluator's live term set is exactly its `Env` plus the value the statement just reduced (both handed over as `extra`). A recursion-depth test inside the walker would not be equivalent: the same statement list is reached from `case`/`try`/`fun` clause bodies, whose subject terms and partially built containers are live and unnamed.
- **The resource half** (`reclaim/resource.rs`: `prune_at_statement_boundary` at the statement boundary, `prune_beyond` at a nested run's epilogue) reuses the same descent and releases only candidates minted after its `Frontier`, because neither site has a complete root enumeration.
- **The refusal rule: doubt ⇒ decline.** The trigger is `O(1)` per call (handle count vs the last observation, the byte figure re-derived only when it moved) past `STORE_BUDGET_WORDS = 46 422`; an exhausted `WALK_VISIT_BUDGET` (`1 << 20`) or an unknown boxed header then declines the whole prune, and a handle whose `refcount() > 1` is retained. The global collection valve (`Runtime::gc_enabled`) is **not** a guard — this prune moves nothing — and `Runtime::any_engine_ever` is a **diagnostic**, because an engine's term always brings its own payload handle, which the refcount belt retains. Every residual is a **leak, never a dangle**. `Reclaim::prune` is enabled only by the `-eval` driver (`Evaluator::prune`); `apply_eval_closure` builds a fresh evaluator with `prune: false`, because its caller's terms are live and unnameable from there.
- Detail, the rejected guards, the `OffHeapTable` traps and the resource half's gates: `reclaim/CONTEXT.md`.
## The statement window on the term store (`scratch.rs`, `scratch/tests.rs`)
`eval_heap`'s frontier only moves forward and `grow_eval_heap` retires its replaced region into `eval_heap_retired`, so between reclaimable sites the store is the **total volume the run ever copied**, not its live set. `scratch.rs` rewinds the dead prefix of the **current** region at the boundary **before** every top-level statement: a comma sequence discards every statement's value but the last, so at that instant the live set is the evaluator's `Env` plus the named root families and nothing else.
- **The rewind rule (doubt ⇒ keep):** `[mark, used)` is reclaimed **only** after the same reachability descent the payload prune uses (`reclaim::walk` with a `WindowProbe` sink over `Runtime::gather_off_heap_roots` plus the `Env`) found no word inside it. A descent that cannot complete, a replaced region, a mark above the frontier, a reached word and a refused rewind all **decline** and keep every word; `MIN_WINDOW_WORDS` (1 024 words = 8 KiB) is the smallest window worth a descent, so a small one is deferred, never lost.
- **A pin latches the window** (advancing its mark, never resetting) for a step the descent cannot enumerate: `Runtime::stabilize_term`/`stabilize_term_with` and `../port/completion.rs::decode_caller_tag` (**`PinKind::Lifetime`** — the store still names the word after the driver returns), and `apply_eval_closure` plus the two nested-run sites in `../context/pump.rs` (**`PinKind::Transient`** — the machine that holds them is dropped inside the statement; the pump latches only when the run actually **wrote** on the store, `Runtime::pin_window_if_store_written`). The classification changes nothing in the window itself (any pin makes it unresettable); it exists so the region-reclaim pass can honour a lifetime pin at the exit site too.
- **Lifetime:** the window is open for the whole driver run but **resets only at a boundary before a statement**, so a one-statement `-eval` (the `compile:file/2` shape) never resets — its retired chain is the exit-site pass's job — and a single giant statement is inherently retained (its value *is* the result). Two `Machine`-building sites sit outside the enumeration: `../native/file_server/get_until.rs::EngineContinuation::call` runs inside the `{get_until,…}` route `epp`/`compile` take, sound only because the term it hands out stays inside the request/continuation, while `../boot/drive.rs` completes before `open` takes its mark, so its words lie below every window. **The engine's own term store is bounded by the region-reclaim pass, not by this window** (below).
- Mechanism, the writer/holder classification, the decline list, the cost and the "no mid-run site can carry a move" analysis: `scratch/CONTEXT.md`. Figures and the oracle cross-check (`../../tests/eval_region.rs`): `../../PERF.md` *The `-eval` statement window*; the 7 asserting pins are `scratch/tests.rs`.
## The region-reclaim pass (`../runtime/reclaim.rs`, `../runtime/reclaim/`, `../runtime/reclaim/tests.rs`)
The window owns the **current** region's `[mark, used)` prefix; the sibling pass owns the other two things a `-eval` run retains — the **retired** regions a growth created and the **engine arena** above the window's mark. It runs at the **same two non-running sites** (`scratch::open` opens both windows; `scratch::boundary`/`scratch::close` each run one pass) over **one** descent (`eval::reclaim::walk` with a `RangeProbe` sink plus `Runtime::gather_off_heap_roots`). Design record: `../runtime/reclaim/CONTEXT.md`.
- **Two mechanisms.** Whole-region actions in `reclaim.rs` (drop a proven-dead retired region whole, `reset_to(cursor)` the current region's dead window, Tier-A `heap_rewind` the arena to the mark the window opened at — Tier B's `reclaim_heap` is deliberately refused here, because its fallible `forward` abandons the caller's roots on `Err`). The **evacuation move** in `reclaim/compact.rs` (copy everything reachable from `Runtime::for_each_root_mut`'s eighteen families plus the caller's `MoveSlots` into ONE fresh destination through `beam_gc`'s `FromSet`/`LinearDest`/`Relocator`, then rewrite the roots in place).
- **What the route must obey.** A move runs only when the candidates hold ≥ `MIN_WINDOW_WORDS`, **at least half** their words are dead (the exact `beam_gc::live_words_in` count over the same corpus) and at least one region is released; headroom is a **correctness property, not a tuning knob** (`eval_heap` has one growing consumer, so a destination sized from the live set alone would shrink every other writer's bound — `../context/CONTEXT.md` *The store's writers that do not grow*), and the win is collapsing N regions into one, never making that one small.
- **The refusal rule — doubt is a refusal at both sites.** A move's completeness precondition is a **post-move verification**: the same descent re-runs from the forwarded roots over the OLD ranges, and **any hit** (or a copy that failed mid-way) keeps **BOTH** — destination installed as a *retired* region, every source kept, nothing dangles, no fall-through to the whole-region drop — and a hit is **attributed** (`CallerSlot(i)` / `Family(family, slot)` / `OutsideCorpus`), never merely counted. The three decisive declines are **`R_PIN`** (a runtime-lifetime pin latched the window and a move cannot rewrite its slot — empirically necessary: with the check bypassed the verification really does hit on `cerl_clauses` and the compile then fails `system_limit`), **`R_REASON`** (a recorded raise's live reason word whose address stands in a candidate range — a reader, no writable slot) and **`R_VERIFY_HIT`**; the whole-region half declines on the switch, no window, a withheld live term (a `Flow::Fatal` whose `BeamError` may carry a live reason word), `Runtime::in_machine` set, no statement window, a pin (transient only at the **boundary**), a walk decline or a refused rewind. Both full lists, the other `R_*` codes and the `&'static str` on `RegionReclaimStats::reclaim_last_decline` are `../runtime/reclaim/CONTEXT.md`'s; every decline leaves both regions byte-identical. A **recorded** raise is not a decline — its live reason word joins the descent's corpus.
- **Two guards tie the mechanisms together:** a lifetime pin registers its region with the pass (never reset again, every region retired from then on excluded), and the window's mark is the pass's **current-region floor**. The switch is `BEAM_RT_REGION_RECLAIM` (`all`/`store`/`store-whole`/`arena`/`nomove`/`off`) plus `Runtime::set_region_reclaim_enabled`; it can only *disable* an action, never a guard. The arena's live figure is `beam_engine::ContextHeapStats::live_words` (`arena_words_reclaimed` is the cumulative released one); `Runtime::alloc_words`'s `arena_words` is *cumulative demand* and a rewind never lowers it.
- **What it buys.** A one-statement `-eval` has no second boundary, so its retired chain is provably dead the moment its result is the only live term — the exit-site pass collapses it with **one** descent and no move (`compile:file/2` pins the store, so the move declines `R_PIN`); a two-statement `-eval` is where the arena action fires and its move declines `R_VERIFY_HIT`, the corpus over-approximating a parked machine's used extent. Neither refusal is a silent no-op; the measured A/B and the `[rt-mem] exit` lines are `../runtime/CONTEXT.md` (*Measured A/B*), figures also `../M1_GAPS_GC.md`.
- **Phase 2 — reclaiming *during* a nested compile is blocked** (no mid-run site can carry a move: the in-flight machine stack and the evaluator's Rust-frame root set have no `&mut` reachability); the three points and the required seams are `scratch/CONTEXT.md`'s.
## API Surface
### Entry points (`mod.rs`)
- `pub fn eval(ctx: &mut RuntimeContext<'_>, expr: &str) -> Result<Eterm>` — the crate-root-re-exported entry point: parses `expr`, tree-walks it in a fresh `Env`, returns the value term on the process heap (valid for the runtime's lifetime); the driver runs inside `ctx.with_relocation_exempt(..)`.
- `pub(crate) fn apply_eval_closure(ctx, fun, index, args) -> Result<Eterm>` — applies a stored closure by `index`, from `RuntimeContext::apply_fun` for a fun stamped `EVAL_FUN_UNIQ`; `BadArg` when none is stored there.
- `pub(crate) const EVAL_FUN_UNIQ: u64 = 0x6265_616d_7274_0001` — the sentinel `uniq` on every fun this evaluator builds, so it is recognisable and resolvable.
- `pub(crate) struct FunBody { kind: FunKind, env: Env }` — a closure body pushed onto `Runtime::eval_closures` by `register_fun`.
- `parse::parse(src) -> Result<Expr>` and the AST are private (`mod ast; mod parse;`).
- Internal: `Evaluator` (the walker), `Flow { Raise{class,reason,origin,head}, Fatal }` + `EResult`/`Env`, `finish` (the **only** terminal `Flow::Raise` → `BeamError` conversion, attaching a non-atom reason to the live-reason slot), `BitBuf` and the bit helpers.
### Grammar coverage (`parse.rs` + `ast.rs`)
The Erlang **expression** subset (`erl_eval`'s input) with Erlang's exact precedence/associativity — `binop_of`'s binding powers mirror `erl_parse.yrl` (`catch` lowest, then `=`/`!`, `orelse`, `andalso`, comparisons, `++`/`--`, additive, multiplicative, prefix):
- literals in every base (`Base#Digits`, 2..=36), floats with exponent, atoms (bare/quoted), strings, chars (`$a`, `\xHH`, `\x{…}`, octal, the named escapes), lists (incl. improper `[1,2|3]`), tuples, maps, binaries;
- variables (`X`, `_`, `_Name`) and `Pattern = Expr`; `case`/`if`/`begin`/`try`/`catch`/`receive`;
- `fun`s (anonymous, named self-recursive `fun F(…)->…end`, captured `fun f/A` / `fun M:F/A`);
- list/map/binary comprehensions (`<-`, `<=` binary generators, filters), incl. the `<<…>>` template recorded as one segment;
- calls: auto-imported local `f(Args)`, remote `m:f(Args)`, and a fun-valued expression `(fun…end)(Args)`.
A trailing `.` is tolerated (the CLI strips one, `cli.rs::strip_trailing_dot`).
**A quoted atom is always an atom** — never a keyword, operator or variable (`'case'` is `Tok::QuotedAtom`, `case` a `Tok::Atom`, mirroring OTP's scanner), so keyword guards and `binop_of` match only the bare variant while `expect_atom_name` (after `:`, in `fun M:F/A`) accepts both; a quoted *call head* is always `erlang:Name/Arity` (`'abs'(-1)` is `erlang:abs/1`, `'Foo'(1)` reaches the oracle's `undef`); `fun 'foo'/0` is a fun reference while `fun 'foo'(X) -> X end` is a syntax error; `1 'and' 2` is the typed gap it is on the oracle; integers are exact to `i128` (past it, a gap); the parser's two context flags are `in_segment` (`/` a type specifier) and `no_remote` (a `try` clause's `Class:Reason` head).
Semantics come from below (nothing re-implemented): arithmetic/comparison/bit operators are **`erlang` BIFs** through `RuntimeContext::call_bif` (`1/0` ⇒ `error:badarith`, bignums for the six bit operators), a call is a `call_bif` (the load-on-demand stdlib path), and terms are built through `beam_engine::terms`/`beam_codec::term`, inspected only through `beam_term`. The special forms `beam_bifs` does **not** own — `and`/`or`/`xor`/`not`/`andalso`/`orelse`, `++`/`--` — are implemented here with the oracle's error behaviour (`not 5` ⇒ `error:badarg`). The raise helpers (`error/1,2`, `exit/1`, `throw/1`, `nif_error/1,2`) and `apply/2,3` are served here so a **live reason term** survives (the atom-only `ExitReason` cannot carry one), and an unresolved target yields a raise carrying the synthesized callee head item (`Flow::Raise.head`); `erlang:raise/3` is deliberately **not** served here (it must validate both class and stacktrace), so it falls through to the registered `beam_bifs::convert::raise_3` (an invalid shape is that BIF's **returned** `badarg` atom, never a raise).
### Binary segments (`<<…>>`) — construction and matching
Both directions are oracle-cross-checked; the pure bit work lives in three siblings (`bits.rs`, `bits_decode.rs`, `bits_match.rs`).
- **Construction, integers** (`append_segment` + `bits::integer_le_image`): **any** width from a small int or exact bignum, two's complement, big or little endian (`native` is little-endian here) — `little` is laid out **byte-wise**: the `n_bits / 8` whole bytes least-significant first, then the next byte's **low** `n_bits % 8` bits pushed into the high bits of the final partial byte (`<<-300:12/little>>` = `<<212,14:4>>`).
- **Construction, `float`** `:16`/`:32`/`:64` (default `64`), either endianness, rounded half-to-even; an integer value is converted (`<<(1 bsl 64):64/float>>` = 2^64 as a double); `signed` is accepted and ignored. **`utf`**: `utf8`/`utf16`/`utf32` from a code point, an astral one becoming a surrogate pair and `utf16-little` swapping **each 2-byte unit**; a lone surrogate, a code point past `16#10FFFF` or a negative value is `error:badarg`.
- **Matching** (`match_binary_segments`): integer segments of **any** width (exact; `signed` sign-extends within the width; past the 60-bit range a real bignum, never `system_limit`), `float` (`:16`/`:32`/`:64`), and `utf8`/`utf16`/`utf32` (1–4 / 2 or 4 / 4 bytes).
- **The absolute rule:** an invalid, truncated or out-of-range pattern field is a **match failure** (`Ok(None)` → `false`), never a raise — a malformed UTF sequence, a lone surrogate, a code point past `16#10FFFF`, a float width ∉ {16,32,64}, too few bits. A `<<…>>` pattern must consume the subject exactly, so `case <<255,…,255>> (9 bytes) of <<A:65>> -> A end` is `case_clause`, not a gap.
- **A literal string is one segment per character** (`parse_segment_expanded`), so construction, matching and the comprehensions see one shape: `<<"abc">>` is three 8-bit segments, and a utf-typed or sized string keeps its kind/size per character (`<<"abc"/utf16>>` = `<<0,97,0,98,0,99>>`). A *variable* holding a list with a utf kind stays the oracle's `error:badarg`.
### Capability gap (anything outside the grammar)
Anything outside the grammar is a typed capability gap: the parser (and a few evaluator arms) raise a `BeamError` built by `crate::error::bad_eval`/`error::unsupported`, whose reason atom starts with `unsupported`. `Evaluator::flow_from_error` turns it into `Flow::Fatal`, so the evaluated expression's own `try … catch` cannot swallow it — it propagates out and the CLI exits **3** with one `beam_rt: unsupported: <reason>` line (`crate::error::unsupported_reason` is the single classifier). The reason atom is deliberately **not** interned on the shared table (`context/calls.rs::name_raise_reason_atom` skips a capability gap), which keeps exit 3 uncatchable. `erlang:halt/0,1` is likewise `Flow::Fatal`, so `… halt(0) catch … end` terminates with status 0; genuine `error`/`exit`/`throw` raises (and BIF structured reasons like `{badmap,M}`, `{badkey,K}`, `{case_clause,V}`) stay catchable with their live reason term preserved.
## Constraints
- **`unsafe`-free**: the crate is `#![deny(unsafe_code)]`; this node's only raw reads live in `reclaim.rs`/`reclaim/resource.rs`, each behind its documented override.
- **Return-based errors, no silent swallowing:** the public entry points return `beam_common::Result`; the internal walker carries its own `Flow` signal, converted once by `finish`. No crate-local error enum, no `panic!`/`unwrap()` on runtime data.
- **Call down, never duplicate:** every operator and call goes through `RuntimeContext::call_bif`; terms are built/inspected only through `beam_term` (+ `beam_engine::terms` / `beam_codec::term`); the `~w`/`~p` printer is not re-implemented here.
- **`Flow::Fatal` is the uncatchable channel** (halt + capability gap); only `BeamError::Raise` becomes a catchable `Flow::Raise`.
- Files ~1000 lines: `mod.rs` is one cohesive unit, the bit-string machinery is three files split by direction, and `compile.rs`/`scratch.rs`/`reclaim.rs` own their subjects.
## Known Issues (typed capability gaps — never wrong results)
- **`receive` is the route's one waiting primitive, and its gaps are typed.** A bare `receive` is `unsupported` when nothing can deliver (no window bounds the wait) — unless a live port's driver holds one of its own asynchronous operations (`Runtime::ports_awaiting_async`: a passive `gen_tcp:recv/2,3`, an `accept`, a datagram read), which is sat out over the blocking pass under the same three progress signals and `crate::port::PORT_EVENT_DEADLINE` (5 s of consecutive no-progress rounds) as the compiled route's `RuntimeContext::wait_for_port_event`, keeping the blocking `pump_scheduler` (its sleeps give a runnable child time to deliver). `receive … after infinity` is the typed "block forever" gap, and a bare `receive` **inside an in-slice spawned process** (a closure `spawn`ed from running loaded code, which keeps the tree-walking entry) is `unsupported` too — it runs inside a scheduler slice and may not re-enter the scheduler.
- **`receive … after T` ends on its own deadline, with one port-poll slice of pickup latency.** The `after` arm scans the running process's **real mailbox**, tests the deadline, pumps non-blockingly (`Runtime::pump_scheduler_now`, bounded by `RECEIVE_PUMP_SLICES = 64`), takes one port-poller pass over `remaining.min(PORT_POLL_SLICE)` and spends the rest on the **timer heap** (`Runtime::wait_for_timer`: sleep to the earliest pending deadline or the window end, then one bounded pass) — all in one iteration; the loop rescans the mailbox before it ever runs the `after` body, so a closed window pays no pump, no poll and no sleep and a message that arrived at the boundary still wins over the `after` body. Every message a port round produces is **delivered into the mailbox** through the runtime's one delivery path (`RuntimeContext::poll_port_messages_into_mailbox`), never tried against the clauses inline, so the `receive` is selective over port frames exactly as the oracle's is: an unmatched frame **stays queued** for a later `receive`, and frames drained in the same round as a matching one are not discarded with it (corpus `driver/line_full_line_noeol_flush` and its selective half); a delivery sends the loop straight back to the scan. The one residual is by construction: a message arriving in the **timer heap** is picked up by the next iteration, i.e. within `PORT_POLL_SLICE` (25 ms). Pinned by `eval::tests::{an_after_window_fires_on_its_own_deadline, a_message_that_beats_the_deadline_is_picked_up_promptly}` and `…::{a_long_after_window_is_waited_out_in_port_poll_slices, a_sub_slice_after_window_never_loops}`; the deadline table is `../../PERF.md`.
- **A spawned `-eval` closure is compiled into a synthetic module and runs as a real process entry.** `compile.rs::entry_for` takes the closure's own source text — sliced verbatim by the parser (`FunBody::source`) — emits `-module(beam_rt_eval_fun_<index>). -export([run/<k+A>]). run(<capture names…>, BeamRtArg1…N) -> (<closure source text, verbatim>)(BeamRtArg1…N).`, compiles it **in process** (`compile:file/2` with `binary`, `no_spawn_compiler_process`, `return_errors`, `return_warnings` over a uniquely named temp `.erl`, removed on drop) and loads it with `code:load_binary/3`, so the spawn creates an ordinary `ProcessEntry::Export` and a bare `receive` or a nested `timer:sleep/1` parks the child. A capture is the generated entry's parameter keeping the **captured variable's own name** (name-sorted; the spawn's args follow); embedding the *text* rather than printing the AST lets the compiler derive the free variables, the self-recursion name and every nested fun; a capture the body appears to rebind is a **match** against it, as `erl_eval`'s clause binding is. `CompiledClosure` is cached on `Runtime::eval_compiled` by the closure's index — **a decline included** — so a repeat spawn costs one map probe and an uncompilable closure is never re-compiled; every failure (no recorded source, a compile error, an unloadable image, no reachable compiler) **declines** to the tree-walking entry, so its typed gaps are unchanged rather than replaced by a wrong value — a body with an **undefined variable** cannot compile, so the child dies with `variable "UndefinedVar" is unbound` (the oracle rejects the whole expression at *parse* time, so that case is not oracle-comparable). The first compile in a fresh runtime dominates (the compiler's own modules load), so a **pickup** bound is measured only on children that carry none (a compiled-MFA child, the closure's second spawn) while the cold span is pinned against `COLD_CLOSURE_COMPILE_BUDGET` (2 s); figures `../../PERF.md`. The residual is the **in-slice** spawn: a `spawn` from inside a running engine (`../native/process.rs::spawn_in_pass`) may not re-enter the scheduler, so it keeps the tree-walking `ProcessEntry::Fun { target: None }`, where a bare `receive` reports `unsupported: receive (no after clause) inside a spawned process` and a parking callee folds into `beam_engine::blocked()` (`may_pump_scheduler() == false`). A *resumable* walker's engine-side half exists anyway (`beam_engine::FunOutcome::{Return, Suspend}` + the defaulted `ExecutionContext::apply_fun_outcome` at the `call_fun`/`call_fun2` seam's `Ok(None)` arm); nothing on the spawn route needs it now.
- **`catch Expr` folds by class**, exactly like `erl_eval`: `throw` yields the reason, `exit` yields `{'EXIT', Reason}`, `error` yields `{'EXIT', {Reason, Stacktrace}}` — always a 2-tuple, even for a non-atom reason; a `try … catch Class:Reason:Stacktrace` clause's third pattern matches the same cooked trace, which `Evaluator::cook_supplied_trace` renders innermost first from the raise's **synthesized callee head item** (`Flow::Raise.head`) and the supplied `erlang:raise/3` frames through `beam_engine::encode_supplied_stacktrace` (an `AtomTable::fork` as the atom source — read-only, `lookup`-resolved, index-identical). **Known divergence (latent):** the head item is consed **in front of** the supplied list, which the oracle never does, and `beam_engine`'s compiled-code cook takes the *opposite* precedence; latent because `head` exists only for the bare `undef` while a `raise/3` flow carries `head: None`.
- **An `undef` carries the missing callee's head item.** When an unresolved call target makes `CallBif` answer the bare `error:undef`, the call site (`Evaluator::call`/`apply_value`) synthesizes `{TargetModule, TargetFunction, ArgList, Location}` from the **real** evaluated argument list with `Location = []` — the item the engine's compiled-code path builds and OTP's `logger_server:call_h/4` matches on. **No `erl_eval`/`init` frame is fabricated** (there is no `erl_eval` port here), so the cooked trace is the head item followed by `[]`; `/2`'s fun form contributes it only for an **external** `fun m:f/A`. Gated to the bare `undef` (class `error`, reason **atom** `undef`) and only when no head item is present yet, so a re-raise through a non-matching `try` keeps it; a raise with neither head item nor supplied trace (`erlang:error/1`, an arithmetic error) renders an **empty** trace.
- A **utf pattern segment carrying an explicit `:Size`/`unit:`** is a typed `unsupported`: the oracle rejects such a segment *syntactically* (`<<C:8/utf8>>` is a syntax error), so no differential case can exist and no value may be fabricated.
- A **non-final unsized** `binary`/`bitstring` pattern segment is a typed `unsupported` (Erlang requires such a segment to be last).
- **The `-eval` action's own uncaught raise is rendered with its real reason term:** `finish` populates the live-reason slot, `Runtime::eval` stabilizes that term at the action process's escape boundary (`Runtime::stabilize_error_live_reason` — the action child's heap dies right after), and the CLI's `Action::Eval` arm routes the failure through `cli.rs`'s `reported_reason`, exactly as `Action::Boot` and the `run` action do. So `beam_rt -noshell -eval 'erlang:error({badkey, binary:copy(<<"abc">>)})'` prints `beam_rt: error {badkey,<<97,98,99>>}` (exit 1), never the `<structured reason>` placeholder. Pinned by `../../tests/structured_reason_lifetime.rs`.
- A non-empty list's clause `apply_closure` with no matching arity is `BadArity`.
- `erlang:alias/0`, NIF/driver dispatch, `open_port` and every other host facility behave as the lower crates specify — this node only *calls* them.
- The store/window/pass residuals (a declined walk retains the store; a payload an earlier nested run built is retained for the store's lifetime, so the fun-loop envelope converges on a capacity; the `-eval` store's per-pid figure is never published; the descent duplicates `graph_holds_reference`'s shape as a deferral; a single giant statement is inherently retained): `reclaim/CONTEXT.md`, `scratch/CONTEXT.md`, `../M1_GAPS_GC.md`.

## Raw-term-holder inventory (what a moving/compacting store would invalidate)
Every holder below stores a raw `Eterm` (for a boxed/list term, an address) and every one is **pointer-pinned** — this is the roster a heap-compaction design must either relocate through a root table or refuse to compact across. Sites are named by **symbol**, not line number.
| site | owner | holds | lifetime |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Env` (`mod.rs`) | the driver's `env` + every clause-local clone | whatever a sub-expression produced: the **arena** (`alloc_words`, this route's `mailbox_table == None` arm), the **store** (`eval_heap`, the table-less BIF copy-out), a **literal**, or an immediate | one frame; the driver's `env` spans every statement |
| `FunBody` (`mod.rs`) | `env: Env` | closure captures (same heap mix) | **runtime** — `Runtime::eval_closures`, enumerated by `FunBody::env_terms` → `gather_off_heap_roots` |
| `Flow::Raise` (`mod.rs`) | `reason`, `head: Option<Eterm>` | a live reason (often a **pooled BIF heap** word, read by `flow_from_error` while that heap lives) + a synthesized `undef` head item | one unwind; `finish` attaches `reason` via `Raise::with_live_reason` |
| `CompOut::List/Map` (`mod.rs`) | the comprehension accumulator `Vec<Eterm>` / `Vec<(Eterm,Eterm)>` | comprehension elements | one comprehension |
| `BitBuf` (`mod.rs`) | `bytes: Vec<u8>` + `bits` | **no terms** | one binary build |
| `CompTemplate<'x>` (`mod.rs`) | AST refs | — | one borrow |
| `Evaluator::{terms,list,map}` (`mod.rs`) | the walker's locals (`Vec<Eterm>` / `acc` / `Vec<(Eterm,Eterm)>`) | evaluated sub-expressions | one call |
| `Evaluator::apply_value` (`mod.rs`) | `function: Eterm`, `args: &[Eterm]` | fun term + args | one application |
| `Evaluator::try_mailbox_receive` (`mod.rs`) | `messages` (from `ctx.mailbox_messages()`), `bound: Env` | mailbox words + bindings | one receive scan |
| `Evaluator::catch_clauses` (`mod.rs`) | `cooked_trace: Option<Eterm>` | stacktrace list | one catch |
| `CompiledClosure` (`compile.rs`) | `Runtime::eval_compiled: FastHashMap<u32, Option<CompiledClosure>>`, `captures: Vec<Eterm>` | **word copies** of `FunBody.env` values (aliased, not deep-copied); `arguments()` copies them again | **runtime** |
| `Window` (`scratch.rs`) | `ScratchState`: `base`/`mark: usize` | **address identity + word mark**, no terms | one `-eval` run |
| `WindowProbe` (`scratch.rs`) | `lo`/`hi: usize` | an address range | one boundary |
| `Live` (`reclaim.rs`) | `payloads: FastHashSet<usize>` | `RefcBinary::data_ptr()` addresses | one prune |
| `walk` (`reclaim.rs`) | `work: Vec<Eterm>` | a worklist it **dereferences** | one prune |
| `Frontier` (`reclaim/resource.rs`) | `ResourcePruneState.frontier` + per-run, `words: Vec<usize>` (`term.raw()`) | resource-term word addresses | runtime + one run |
| `ResourceSink` (`reclaim/resource.rs`) | `candidates`/`reached`/`ids` | word addresses + ref ids | one prune |
Compaction consequences (binding):
- **The `Env` chain is the evaluator's only host-side root set, and it lives in Rust locals/parameters, never in the `Evaluator`** (which holds exactly `ctx`, `depth`, `prune`): the driver clones `env` per clause/guard/`case`/`if`/`try`/`receive`/comprehension and republishes only the root `env` (as `extra`), so a clause-local clone is invisible to every root enumeration and the other Rust-stack locals stay unenumerable. **`FunBody.env` and `CompiledClosure.captures` are ONE slot, not two families** — `compile.rs`'s "no new root family" claim holds only while they stay word-identical, so a compaction must rewrite both in lockstep.
- **`Window::base` and `RangeProbe`'s ranges are address identities** (from `runtime/reclaim.rs`'s `Cursor` and the arena's `for_each_heap_region` bases): a stale `base` declines safely, but a stale range can answer a false *miss*, which is why the move re-anchors both the pass's cursor and the statement window (`ScratchState::stop` drops it). And `reclaim.rs`'s halves differ under compaction — `collect_region_payloads` only *pattern-matches* the `HEADER_BIN_REF` word (sound if `BinRef.val` tracks the payload) while `walk` *dereferences* every boxed/list word, so any walk across a move must run on rewritten words.
- **The mutable seam is `Rc::make_mut`, not `iter_mut`:** `FunBody::for_each_root_mut` and `CompiledClosure::for_each_root_mut` hand out the writable capture words (`RootFamily::EvalClosureEnv`/`EvalCompiledCaptures`, sound only at a non-running site), and `scratch::boundary`/`scratch::close` take `env: &mut Env` + `extra: Vec<&mut Eterm>` and build the move's `MoveSlots` from those very slots — `extra` is **borrowed slots, never a snapshot** (at the exit site the driver lends its own `outcome`'s fields, because a copy would be the corpus the move rewrites while `finish` returned the original word). The **pruning** sinks stay read-only (`&[Eterm]`), and the arena plus a nested run's `Machine` roots are the two locations the move cannot reach.

## Notes for Agents
### The evaluation-order contract (sub-expressions run in `erl_eval`'s order)
The evaluator evaluates a construct's sub-expressions **left-to-right in source order**, exactly as `erl_eval` does: a **list**'s elements left-to-right then its optional tail; a **tuple**'s elements; a **map field**'s key before its value, fields in source order; a **binary segment**'s value before its size; **call** arguments in order; `++`/`--` left operand before right; a **comprehension**'s template once per generated element in generator order.
Order is observable only through a side effect (an `io:format/2` in an element), so it is pinned by the spy-closure probes in `mod.rs::tests::literals_evaluate_their_sub_expressions_in_source_order` and in `../../tests/eval_order.rs` (which cross-checks that the **system `erl`** prints the same bytes).
The list's **cons chain** is only *built* right-to-left after the values are already evaluated (`Evaluator::list` collects the element values, then folds them onto the tail) — that changes no list's *value*, only the order the side effects ran in. Do not describe the current order as "right-to-left".
### Other facts
- A fun built here is recorded in `Runtime::eval_closures` and can escape into a loaded module; `fun M:F/A` is **not** a closure — it is the real `EXPORT_EXT` term (no `uniq`), applied through the host's dynamic call.
### Bit-string invariants
- A `<<…>>` **pattern** failure is `Ok(None)`/`false`, never a raise: an unreadable field (wrong width, too few bits, malformed UTF, out of range) simply does not match — do not "improve" one into `error:badarg`.
- `read_bits_le` is a thin delegation to `bits_match::read_little_endian_bits`; keep that single implementation, and keep it the exact inverse of `bits::integer_le_image` (byte-wise, the trailing partial field entering *above* the whole bytes).
- A **non-atom** raise reason reaches the live-reason slot through `finish` (the module's only terminal conversion, so every raise site is covered); a consumer that wants to *render* it goes through `crate::error::live_reason_term`, because `BeamError`'s `Display` shows only the `<structured reason>` placeholder.
